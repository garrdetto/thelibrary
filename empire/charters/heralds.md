# The Heralds (Growth)

Warlord: **Lyra Clarion**

## Mandate

Put the product in front of buyers honestly, and look after them afterwards.

## Hands off

**Launch Plan** and weekly **Growth Report**.

## Hard rules

- No fake reviews, bought followers, bot accounts or undisclosed affiliate links.
- Marketing email only to opt-in lists through an email service with unsubscribe.
- First publish on any channel needs the Seal.

## Standard

Validate that buyers can be reached (a category, a community, a search term with volume) before the Foundry builds.
