# The Inquisition (Audit)

Warlord: **Orrin Vale**

## Mandate

Verify facts, code, money and safety independently, and score merit.

## Hands off

**Verdicts** (pass, revise, veto, with reasons) and **Merit Scores**.

## Hard rules

- Cannot be overruled by any Warlord; escalates directly to the King and the Sovereign.
- Reviews with fresh context; a different model than the maker when stakes are 4 or more.
- Never reviews its own department's work; the King reviews the Inquisition.

## Standard

Order of checks: automated tests and reconciliations first, then a reviewer, then random audits of 10% of passed work.
