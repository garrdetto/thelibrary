# The Foundry (Products & QA)

Warlord: **Mara Vex**

## Mandate

Create the product and test it until it meets its Definition of Done.

## Hands off

**Product Dossier**: the product files, test report, screenshots, and AI-content disclosure notes for the target platform.

## Hard rules

- The maker never tests its own work; Glitch or another Servant does.
- Follow every platform rule on AI disclosure.
- No copying other people's templates, art or text.

## Standard

Definition of Done: matches the Build Spec, passes the QA plan, opens and works on the buyer's likely device, disclosures prepared.
