# Demo Derby

County-fair demolition derby module. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- Demo Derby (`modules-src/demoderby/`, module id `demoderby`) is built on `engine/` like Fairway Nine: a county-fair demolition derby on a 36 × 22 m dirt pad (a little bigger than a basketball court) ringed by concrete barriers, with bleachers, tents, light towers and a ferris wheel for the fair. Cars are original lofted 1970s full-size bodies (`wagon`, `sedan` specs in `config.ts`, skinned by `models/Loft.ts` + `models/CarBody.ts` with arches, chrome, lamps, grille, rack and a caged interior; no makes, models or logos), flat-painted from 16 colours with a spray-painted number (up to three letters or digits, six spray styles, eight colours, optional outline) on both doors and the roof (`systems/Numbers.ts` canvas textures). The garage card saves the player's look per device (`tl-demoderby-look`) and shows the car on a turntable; opponents get seeded looks with unique numbers. Physics is Rapier's `DynamicRayCastVehicleController` (rear drive, low side grip for dirt, a torque curve strongest off the line) with chassis boxes; `engine/physics/World.ts` `step(dt, events?)` drains an `EventQueue`, and `systems/Heat.ts` turns contact-force events into hits at the solver's contact point (`resolveContacts(events, world)`): force above `contactThreshold` resets the no-contact clock, above `hitThreshold` it damages both cars (0–1 strength up to `hitFull`, zone multipliers front/rear/side, front hits also cost engine health which cuts power to `powerFloor`); barrier hits above `wallHitThreshold` damage the car alone at `wallScale`. Every hit also spreads into components (`systems/Damage.ts`, renderer-free and tested): seven panels, wheels that bend (camber and toe), blow and come off, the radiator (holed, it cooks the engine under throttle until it catches fire and burns the car out), the rear driveline, glass, lamps, bumpers (sag, then fall off) and hood and tailgate (buckle). `handlingOf` turns that into per-wheel grip, springs, ride height and rolling radius, drag, lost steering lock, a steering pull and lost drive, which `Vehicle` pushes into the controller; rolls crush the roof. The garage's damage level (`damagePresets` Light/Realistic/Brutal, `damageFor`) scales hits and part failures. The body mesh dents in place (`CarBody.dent`, a crush-resolution shell whose node-material paint scrapes to bare steel and soot from a per-vertex `wear` channel), and `CarBody.showDamage` shows the parts and returns pieces that came off for `models/Debris.ts`; sparks, shrapnel, steam, fire, smoke (soft instanced sprites, `models/Particles.ts`) and tire marks (`models/TrackMarks.ts`) follow, and the HUD shows a damage diagram. A car is out when its body health hits 0 (`wrecked`, smoke, or burned out) or after `idleOutSeconds` (60) without contact (`idle`); placings count from the back, the last car running wins (if the last two go out together the later-retired car wins) and the heat holds `finishHoldSeconds` before finishing. AI (`systems/Ai.ts`, levels Rookie/Local/Veteran with `lead`, `evasion`, `aim`, `protectEngine` and `steerGain`) picks targets by distance, weakness, exposed flank and a wall to pin against, leads moving targets and aims at the exposed flank and the hurt end, backs in to protect a hurt engine, cancels its own steering pull, dodges cars charging its flank, backs off for a run-up instead of grinding, avoids wrecks and walls and escapes when stuck or not closing on its target. Rendering: tiers carry a 1080-line render floor (`QualitySettings.minRenderHeight`, `App.setRenderHeightFloor`, dropped to 720 then off when frames run under 40 fps) and the post chain from `config.looks` (GTAO, TRAA, bloom, vignette and grain on high and ultra). Score (`demoderby` in `src/lib/module-scores.ts`) is hit points + 250 per knockout + 2 per second survived + 1000 for the win, sent through `Scores.submit` when the player is out or the heat ends, with Watch-the-rest from the results card. Damage goes past the panels and wheels: hard hits bend the frame (`frame`, `frameBend`; the shell sweeps into a banana and the rear axle steers by `frameCrab`, so the car crabs), crushed corners rub their tires (`rub`) and wear the tread to a flat as the car rolls, big nose hits can stall the engine for a moment (`stall`), rear hits on a hurt driveline strip forward or reverse gear (never both; the AI then backs into everything), the hood and trunk lid (or tailgate) buckle and then tear off to show the engine bay or the load floor, and the exhaust drops, drags sparks and falls off. The arena stands in a real Thrill Park: `data/park.json` is the owner's Thrill Park 2 park Land of Speed, trimmed by `tools/park-scenery.mjs`, which also picks and levels the derby lot beside its kart and coaster tracks and lays a footpath to it; after boot a worker (`models/parkScenery.worker.ts`) runs Thrill Park's own `ParkRenderer` into a recording backend (`models/parkSceneryBuild.ts`) and `models/ParkScenery.ts` assembles it in three.js with every flat ride animated, then `arena.fitToLot` drops the fair tents and ferris wheel and the stand camera looks across the pad into the park. Re-run the tool with a `/api/park?view=` response to move the derby to another park. Tests: `modules-src/demoderby/tests/` run the component-damage rules, a launch test, whole AI heats and the park scenery build through `scripts/demoderby-tests.mjs` (part of `pnpm test`).
