# Truck Simulator

Babylon.js open-world trucking module, its lot builder and truck wash, and its saved careers in `/api/trucksim`. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- Truck Simulator (`modules-src/trucksim/`, module id `trucksim`, page `public/modules/trucksim.html`; thumbnail `public/images/modules/trucksim.webp`) is an open-world trucking module: a standalone page on Babylon.js 9.34 (WebGPU, WebGL2 fallback, through Rack & Reel's `createEngine`; if `Standalone.boot` throws, `game/BootRecovery.ts` reloads once on `?gpu=webgl` and after that the boot text shows the real error; `render/world/Pipeline.ts` must import the WebGL and WebGPU `engine.multiRender` extensions because SSAO's geometry buffer needs `createMultipleRenderTarget`; `?quality=low|medium|high|ultra`, `?tonemap=neutral`; without `?quality=` every driver starts on low, and a tier picked in the pause menu is remembered in `localStorage` `trucksim:quality`; `Standalone.setTier` retiers the atmosphere, the post pipeline and the world renderer (stream radius, trees, props)) in a right-handed scene (Y up, metres, heading `h` faces `(sin h, 0, cos h)`, +X the driver's left) with Rapier 0.14 rigs (`sim/`: `TruckRig`, `TrailerRig`, `Coupling` fifth wheel and RGN gooseneck, `DumpBody`, `ExcavatorRig`, `LoaderAI`, renderer-free). Controls: WASD drives; the arrow keys only turn the camera (`stepKeyLook` in `game/Cameras.ts`) and it swings straight back behind the truck when they are let go (the excavator keeps the arrows for its swing and boom); Esc or M opens the county map, where a click sets a waypoint (`fullMapToWorld` in `game/MapDraw.ts`, `TruckSession.setWaypoint`) whose road route shows on the minimap until you are within 30 m (it takes over the GPS line from a job until then; Clear route removes it); P is the pause menu; F10 opens the administrators' developer panel (`ui/DevPanel.ts`, only when `/api/trucksim` returns `admin: true`): it switches to a sandbox copy of the career with $5,000,000 that is never saved (`Progress.enterSandbox`, `sandboxEdit`; a reload brings the real career back) to drive any truck in any design, hitch any trailer, make the truck dirty, damaged, clean or fuelled, and it teleports to any site, sets the time of day and the graphics tier and shows the memory report. A new driver's lot starts with a free 10 × 40 m gravel driveway at the gate under the spawn point (`starterDriveway`, `withStarterDriveway` in `shared/Actions.ts`, item id `driveway`, no refund when demolished), given by the Worker's GET and by guests' borrowed lot. Overheads (fuel and wash canopies, the overpass, a lot's fuel island and wash bay) are built as their own meshes and registered with `render/SeeThrough.ts`, which fades them to 16 % while the truck is under them. Diesel islands at the truck stops and on lots are drawn by `dieselIsland` (`render/lots/Items.ts`): kerb with rounded noses and yellow edges, crash posts, a high-flow dispenser per truck length with retractor-boom hoses on both sides, a DEF pump, squeegee bucket and bin. `MemoryGuard` draw calls over budget only ever warn (cascaded shadows multiply them), so they no longer drop players off high and ultra; engine sounds skip parameter glides under 0.5 %. You start with a paid-off Ridgeline RD-10 dump truck and $3,000 on a 4 km county (`shared/WorldLayout.ts`, `Terrain.ts`, `RoadGraph.ts`: highway loop, Main Street and County Road through Millbrook, quarry, construction sites, refinery, farms, elevator, cold storage, steel and lumber yards, dealer and rentals) and the 16 player lots of Haulers Row, haul loads, earn your way into semis (Kestrel CX, Pinnacle Classic, Northstar Aerodyne, all original) and trailers (tanker, flatbed, grain hopper, reefer, lowboy), and paint and part them in the garage. Each truck comes in a factory design (`DESIGNS` in `shared/Catalog.ts`: Factory, Heritage, Fleet aero, Workhorse, Showroom; `designLook` applies a design's parts, finish and stripe and keeps the colours, `designOf` recognises one) picked on the dealer card (priced `model.price + lookCost(stock, look)`) or in the garage's Designs row; parts include grilles (`stock`, `bars`, `slats`, `mesh`, `blackout`, drawn by `grilleFill` in `render/models/truck/FrontEnd.ts`), roof fairings (`low`, `tall`; `roofAllowed` is false for dump trucks and raised sleepers), side skirts (`skirtsAllowed` is false for dump trucks) and two-tone and chevron stripes; `fitLook` clears parts a truck can't carry and `normalizeLook` fills them in for older saves. Every haul has its realistic procedure (`game/Procedures.ts`, run by `JobRunner`: dump = loaded by an AI excavator or wheel loader, raise the bed; tanker = connect the rack arm or hose; flatbed = forklift or crane, strap or release every tie point; grain = line up under the spout or over the pit, tarp and gates; reefer = back square into a dock, doors, forklift; lowboy = lower the deck, detach the gooseneck, drive the excavator on, reconnect, chain four points); jobs are generated per 30-minute slot from a seeded hash (`shared/Jobs.ts`). The lot builder (`game/BuildMode.ts`, `ui/BuilderBar.ts`, `render/Lots.ts`, `render/lots/`, rules in `shared/Lots.ts`) is a sims-style top-down mode with a 1 m grid for textured gravel and asphalt paving, steel shops, pole barns, offices, add-ons (including a drive-through wash bay and a $2,800 pressure washer, which must stand within `WASHER_SHOP_GAP_M` = 6 m of a steel shop: `washerBesideShop`, `washSpots` in `shared/Lots.ts`) and fences, every placement one `build` action; its Land tab buys up to four 30 m strips (`buy-land`, `lotExtent`). Trucks get dirty with distance (`render/models/DirtLayer.ts`), and E in the travel center's wash lane or your own wash bay, or with the truck centre within `WASHER_REACH_M` = 11 m of your pressure washer (`TruckSession.ownWashAt`), opens the interactive pressure wash (`game/WashMode.ts`: hold the mouse to spray the mud off; `render/models/WashGear.ts` draws the wand at the nozzle and, from your own washer, an updatable sagging hose (`hosePath`) back to its reel; the `wash` action takes off the share cleaned, and a clean truck earns a bonus). Money, jobs and lots all go through the single `applyAction` (`shared/Actions.ts`), run by the Worker (`/api/trucksim`) when signed in and in the page for guests (`net/Progress.ts`). Memory: every Babylon resource lives in a `Scope` (`systems/Scope.ts`) and is torn down on `pagehide`; `ChunkStreamer` streams 256 m chunks with a time budget; `MemoryGuard` samples counts and the JS heap, flags growing counts through Rack & Reel's `ResourceTrend` and shrinks radii or drops a quality tier under pressure; `window.__truckMem()` returns the report and `?perf=1` shows the overlay; node tests build and dispose visuals repeatedly on a `NullEngine` and require counts to return to baseline. Life Sim seam (nothing in it is wired yet): everything draws under a `frameRoot` given by a `TruckHost` (`game/TruckHost.ts`; `game/Standalone.ts` is the standalone host, `game/TruckSession.ts` the reusable core), rigs take a `PhysicsHost` (`sim/PhysicsHost.ts`, collision groups equal to Life Sim's `CG`/`MASK`, kept equal by `tests/groups.test.ts`), rules read a `HaulWorld` (`shared/HaulWorld.ts`, the county is `COUNTY`), `applyAction` returns the cash `delta` for a shared wallet, and the Worker and `Progress.load(world)` take `?world=`. Tests: `scripts/trucksim-tests.mjs` (esbuild bundle of `modules-src/trucksim/tests/*.test.ts`, optional name filters, `node --test`), run by `pnpm test` after `tsc -p modules-src/trucksim/tsconfig.json`. Every make and model is original (no real brands, logos or brand colours), ground and rubber never shine (gloss rule in `render/Materials.ts`), and no visible copy says game. Montello (in progress, `docs/trucksim/MONTELLO.md`): `TruckSession` no longer reads county code directly (heights from `host.physics.heightAt`, sites from `host.world`, GPS lines from `HaulWorld.route`, and the host's optional `TruckHost.placeName` for road and town names), so a Life Sim host can run it; Life Sim already builds Haulers Lane, the lots and the sites into Montello (`foxlake/trucks/TruckEdits.ts`) and hosts the session with `?trucks` (`foxlake/trucks/LifeSimTrucks.ts`: walk up, E into the cab, drive, E out; see the Life Sim notes). the module is moving onto Life Sim's map. Phase 1 is the haul world `MONTELLO` (`shared/montello/MontelloWorld.ts`, id `montello`, Life Sim's town frame: x east, z = −map z): sixteen lots numbered 100–115 (`MONTELLO_LOT_BASE`, `knownLotIndex` in `WorldLayout.ts`) on Haulers Lane, a new lane north off State Highway 23 at map x 2000 just east of Foxtails; new outskirts sites outside the city limits (dealer, truck plaza, equipment rental, quarry, bulk fuel terminal, cold storage, lumber, steel, two farms, two construction sites) each with a driveway (`DRIVEWAY_ENTRIES`, snapped onto the roads at load); and zones at places already in Montello under generic names (three gas stations, the feed mill, the south side hardware and grocery stores, the tire shop by Foxtails). Routes run on Montello's roads and road bridges (kinds 0–3) baked by `tools/bake-montello-roads.mts` into `shared/montello/MontelloRoads.ts` (simplified to 2 m, so the network joins and merges road ends within 2.5 m: `createRoadNetwork(roads, tolerance)` in `RoadGraph.ts`, which buckets segments in 64 m squares); job distances come from the zone-pair table `shared/montello/MontelloDistances.ts` baked by `tools/bake-montello-distances.mts` (the Worker never builds the graph; `tests/montello.test.ts` fails while it is stale). `HaulWorld` carries its `lots`/`lot(index)`, and `Actions`, `Progress` and the Worker read lots from the world. `/api/trucksim?world=montello` keeps Montello careers on profile key `montello:<user>`, and `trucksim_lots` rows use the world's profile key as `user_id`, so a driver holds one lot per world with no migration; public lots are filtered to the asking world. Realism: the driven truck's headlights throw a real beam (`render/models/HeadBeam.ts`: one spot light at the bumper aimed 30 m down the road, standard falloff so the road is lit evenly, always enabled and switched by intensity because enabling a light recompiles every shader, scaled by the host's `session.darkness` so it barely shows in daylight); the loading excavator shows its load in the bucket (`ExcavatorPose.fill`) and the falling stone pours from its bucket lip (`ExcavatorVisual.bucketLip`); tree trunks are solid (`chunkTreeObstacles` in `PropsPlacement.ts` streamed by `SimWorld.setObstacleSource` with the ground chunks at the current tier's tree density, rebuilt on a tier change); roads get W-beam guardrails on posts on fills (0.9 m drop within 10 m of the shoulder) and the outside of highway bends under 400 m radius, solid for the truck, and concrete curbs along Millbrook's streets (`render/world/RoadEdges.ts`: `planRoadEdges`, `railBoxes`, `RoadEdgeLayer`), never at junction mouths, lot gates or site entrances; the quarry is laid out from its centre so it works at any site, with stockpiles at a 35° angle of repose (slumped shoulders, spilled toe stones), the crusher's surge pile at the same slope, and a radial stacker conveyor feeding a pile beside the crusher (`stacker` in `sites/Parts.ts`). Traffic: cars, pickups, SUVs, vans and semis drive the county on their own (`sim/Traffic.ts`, renderer-free and seeded): each keeps the right-hand lane of a road (`buildLanes` finds every junction), follows the Intelligent Driver Model (smooth acceleration, time gap to whoever is ahead including the player's truck and trailer, comfortable braking held to `FIRM_BRAKE` × comfortable unless the room left needs a hard stop), slows ahead of curves by `LATERAL_COMFORT` and before turns, signals and turns at junctions (one vehicle holds a junction at a time, and it waits for a clear slot in the target lane), never takes a dead-end spur and turns around if it reaches one; poses roll out in curves, dive under braking, squat under power and pitch over hills. `render/TrafficVisuals.ts` draws each agent (original procedural cars in `render/models/CarModel.ts` with brake and turn lamps switched by visibility; semis are catalog tractors in random designs pulling catalog trailers that pivot on the fifth wheel and follow the tractor's path) and gives each a kinematic Rapier body so traffic is solid; `Standalone` runs it before the physics step with `TRAFFIC_BY_TIER` vehicles (8 low … 30 ultra), spawning 320–750 m from the rig and removing past 950 m. Design is in `docs/trucksim/PLAN.md`, the ordered build prompts in `docs/trucksim/PROMPTS.md`, files in `docs/ENGINE-MAP.md` under trucksim, and a developer README in `modules-src/trucksim/README.md`.
- `src/server/routes/trucksim.ts` + `src/server/trucksim.ts`: `/api/trucksim`, Truck Simulator's saved state in D1 `trucksim_profiles` (one JSON profile per user: cash, owned trucks and trailers, active rig, accepted job, lot company and build, stats; migration 0049, also created on first use) and `trucksim_lots` (lot index to user, `seen_at`). GET returns `{signedIn, username, profile, lots}` for the signed-in driver, giving them the first free Haulers Row lot on arrival (`assignLot`; every visit refreshes `seen_at`, and a lot unseen for 45 days, `LOT_RELEASE_MS`, is released first), or `{signedIn:false, lots}` for guests; `?lots=1` returns only the public lot list (index, owner, company and build, never user ids). POST (signed in, same-origin, Zod, 16 KB body) runs one action through the shared `applyAction` (`modules-src/trucksim/shared/Actions.ts`): `buy-truck`, `buy-trailer`, `sell-truck`, `sell-trailer`, `customize`, `select`, `accept-job`, `complete-job` (the job is regenerated from its id `<world>-<slot>-<n>`, so the Worker never stores jobs; pay comes from `Jobs.ts` with damage and on-time adjustments), `abandon-job`, `refuel`, `repair`, `build` (at most 80 items per request, validated against the lot size), `demolish`, `rename`, `claim-lot` (moves the claim only if `lotIsFree`, then `claimLot`) and `odometer`; the reply is `{ok, message, delta, paid, profile}` and the save is conditional on `updated_at` (409 when another window saved first). `?world=<id>` (or `?game=<id>`) picks the haul world from `HAUL_WORLDS` in `src/server/trucksim.ts` (`county` today; each entry gives a `HaulWorld` and a `profileKey`, so Life Sim can add `lifesim` on its own key and the shared wallet using `delta`); an unknown id is a 400. Guests play on an in-memory profile in the page.
