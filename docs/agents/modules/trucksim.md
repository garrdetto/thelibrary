# Truck Simulator

Babylon.js open-world trucking module, its lot builder and truck wash, and its saved careers in `/api/trucksim`. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- Truck Simulator (`modules-src/trucksim/`, module id `trucksim`, page `public/modules/trucksim.html`; thumbnail `public/images/modules/trucksim.webp`) is an open-world trucking module: a standalone page on Babylon.js 9.34 (WebGPU, WebGL2 fallback, through Rack & Reel's `createEngine`, `?quality=low|medium|high|ultra`, `?tonemap=neutral`) in a right-handed scene (Y up, metres, heading `h` faces `(sin h, 0, cos h)`, +X the driver's left) with Rapier 0.14 rigs (`sim/`: `TruckRig`, `TrailerRig`, `Coupling` fifth wheel and RGN gooseneck, `DumpBody`, `ExcavatorRig`, `LoaderAI`, renderer-free). You start with a paid-off Ridgeline RD-10 dump truck and $3,000 on a 4 km county (`shared/WorldLayout.ts`, `Terrain.ts`, `RoadGraph.ts`: highway loop, Main Street and County Road through Millbrook, quarry, construction sites, refinery, farms, elevator, cold storage, steel and lumber yards, dealer and rentals) and the 16 player lots of Haulers Row, haul loads, earn your way into semis (Kestrel CX, Pinnacle Classic, Northstar Aerodyne, all original) and trailers (tanker, flatbed, grain hopper, reefer, lowboy), and paint and part them in the garage. Each truck comes in a factory design (`DESIGNS` in `shared/Catalog.ts`: Factory, Heritage, Fleet aero, Workhorse, Showroom; `designLook` applies a design's parts, finish and stripe and keeps the colours, `designOf` recognises one) picked on the dealer card (priced `model.price + lookCost(stock, look)`) or in the garage's Designs row; parts include grilles (`stock`, `bars`, `slats`, `mesh`, `blackout`, drawn by `grilleFill` in `render/models/truck/FrontEnd.ts`), roof fairings (`low`, `tall`; `roofAllowed` is false for dump trucks and raised sleepers), side skirts (`skirtsAllowed` is false for dump trucks) and two-tone and chevron stripes; `fitLook` clears parts a truck can't carry and `normalizeLook` fills them in for older saves. Every haul has its realistic procedure (`game/Procedures.ts`, run by `JobRunner`: dump = loaded by an AI excavator or wheel loader, raise the bed; tanker = connect the rack arm or hose; flatbed = forklift or crane, strap or release every tie point; grain = line up under the spout or over the pit, tarp and gates; reefer = back square into a dock, doors, forklift; lowboy = lower the deck, detach the gooseneck, drive the excavator on, reconnect, chain four points); jobs are generated per 30-minute slot from a seeded hash (`shared/Jobs.ts`). The lot builder (`game/BuildMode.ts`, `ui/BuilderBar.ts`, `render/Lots.ts`, `render/lots/`, rules in `shared/Lots.ts`) is a sims-style top-down mode with a 1 m grid for textured gravel and asphalt paving, steel shops, pole barns, offices, add-ons (including a drive-through wash bay and a $2,800 pressure washer, which must stand within `WASHER_SHOP_GAP_M` = 6 m of a steel shop: `washerBesideShop`, `washSpots` in `shared/Lots.ts`) and fences, every placement one `build` action; its Land tab buys up to four 30 m strips (`buy-land`, `lotExtent`). Trucks get dirty with distance (`render/models/DirtLayer.ts`), and E in the travel center's wash lane or your own wash bay, or with the truck centre within `WASHER_REACH_M` = 11 m of your pressure washer (`TruckSession.ownWashAt`), opens the interactive pressure wash (`game/WashMode.ts`: hold the mouse to spray the mud off; `render/models/WashGear.ts` draws the wand at the nozzle and, from your own washer, an updatable sagging hose (`hosePath`) back to its reel; the `wash` action takes off the share cleaned, and a clean truck earns a bonus). Money, jobs and lots all go through the single `applyAction` (`shared/Actions.ts`), run by the Worker (`/api/trucksim`) when signed in and in the page for guests (`net/Progress.ts`). Memory: every Babylon resource lives in a `Scope` (`systems/Scope.ts`) and is torn down on `pagehide`; `ChunkStreamer` streams 256 m chunks with a time budget; `MemoryGuard` samples counts and the JS heap, flags growing counts through Rack & Reel's `ResourceTrend` and shrinks radii or drops a quality tier under pressure; `window.__truckMem()` returns the report and `?perf=1` shows the overlay; node tests build and dispose visuals repeatedly on a `NullEngine` and require counts to return to baseline. Life Sim seam (nothing in it is wired yet): everything draws under a `frameRoot` given by a `TruckHost` (`game/TruckHost.ts`; `game/Standalone.ts` is the standalone host, `game/TruckSession.ts` the reusable core), rigs take a `PhysicsHost` (`sim/PhysicsHost.ts`, collision groups equal to Life Sim's `CG`/`MASK`, kept equal by `tests/groups.test.ts`), rules read a `HaulWorld` (`shared/HaulWorld.ts`, the county is `COUNTY`), `applyAction` returns the cash `delta` for a shared wallet, and the Worker and `Progress.load(world)` take `?world=`. Tests: `scripts/trucksim-tests.mjs` (esbuild bundle of `modules-src/trucksim/tests/*.test.ts`, optional name filters, `node --test`), run by `pnpm test` after `tsc -p modules-src/trucksim/tsconfig.json`. Every make and model is original (no real brands, logos or brand colours), ground and rubber never shine (gloss rule in `render/Materials.ts`), and no visible copy says game. Design is in `docs/trucksim/PLAN.md`, the ordered build prompts in `docs/trucksim/PROMPTS.md`, files in `docs/ENGINE-MAP.md` under trucksim, and a developer README in `modules-src/trucksim/README.md`.
- `src/server/routes/trucksim.ts` + `src/server/trucksim.ts`: `/api/trucksim`, Truck Simulator's saved state in D1 `trucksim_profiles` (one JSON profile per user: cash, owned trucks and trailers, active rig, accepted job, lot company and build, stats; migration 0049, also created on first use) and `trucksim_lots` (lot index to user, `seen_at`). GET returns `{signedIn, username, profile, lots}` for the signed-in driver, giving them the first free Haulers Row lot on arrival (`assignLot`; every visit refreshes `seen_at`, and a lot unseen for 45 days, `LOT_RELEASE_MS`, is released first), or `{signedIn:false, lots}` for guests; `?lots=1` returns only the public lot list (index, owner, company and build, never user ids). POST (signed in, same-origin, Zod, 16 KB body) runs one action through the shared `applyAction` (`modules-src/trucksim/shared/Actions.ts`): `buy-truck`, `buy-trailer`, `sell-truck`, `sell-trailer`, `customize`, `select`, `accept-job`, `complete-job` (the job is regenerated from its id `<world>-<slot>-<n>`, so the Worker never stores jobs; pay comes from `Jobs.ts` with damage and on-time adjustments), `abandon-job`, `refuel`, `repair`, `build` (at most 80 items per request, validated against the lot size), `demolish`, `rename`, `claim-lot` (moves the claim only if `lotIsFree`, then `claimLot`) and `odometer`; the reply is `{ok, message, delta, paid, profile}` and the save is conditional on `updated_at` (409 when another window saved first). `?world=<id>` (or `?game=<id>`) picks the haul world from `HAUL_WORLDS` in `src/server/trucksim.ts` (`county` today; each entry gives a `HaulWorld` and a `profileKey`, so Life Sim can add `lifesim` on its own key and the shared wallet using `delta`); an unknown id is a 400. Guests play on an in-memory profile in the page.
