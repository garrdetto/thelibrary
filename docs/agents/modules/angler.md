# Northwoods Angler

PlayCanvas idle fishing module. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- `src/server/routes/angler.ts` + `src/server/angler.ts`: `/api/angler`, Northwoods Angler. Tables `angler_profiles` (one JSON profile per user: Tackle Credits, owned gear, look, boat paint, stats, achievements), `angler_sessions` (the anchored session: lake, spot, rig, next bite time, last seen) and `angler_catches` (every landed or lost fish; weight in tenths of an ounce, length in quarter inches) — migration 0020, also created on first use. GET `?board=<species>&lake=<id|all>&period=all|month` and `?hall=1` (the three heaviest of every species with when they were caught; what both record books show) are public; GET with no params returns the signed-in profile and recent catches. POST (signed in, same-origin, Zod): `start` (anchor at x/z with a rig; the server schedules the next bite on its own clock), `tick` (heartbeat with play-time and miles; when the bite is due and the angler was seen in the last 150 s the server rolls the catch with `crypto.getRandomValues`, writes it, pays credits and achievements), `stop`, `buy`, `equip`, admin `reset-records` and `void-catch`. At most one bite per 45 s per account; closed tabs earn nothing. `/api/angler-lake` (`src/server/routes/angler-lake.ts`) upgrades to the Durable Object `AnglerLake` (`src/server/angler-lake.ts`, binding `ANGLER_LAKE`, one per lake instance `<lake>:<0-7>`, 4 boats each, 8 connections): shared lakes with other anglers' boats, crews of two on one boat, and catch news. It stores nothing; catches still come only from `/api/angler`, which passes the instance (`room`) so the DO can announce landed fish and confirm crews for the Boat Party achievement.
- Northwoods Angler (`modules-src/angler/`, module id `angler`) is a PlayCanvas (`playcanvas` 2.23.3, bundled, WebGL2) idle fishing module: pick one of five original Wisconsin-style lakes (`shared/Lakes.ts`), drive the owner's bass boat (Meshy export in `assets-src/angler/bass-boat.glb`), drop anchor and fish. Fishing plays itself, and the Reel In button (or F) lets the player take over: power casts and jigging, the hook-set, reeling against tension, bowing to jumps and netting; catches celebrate with a floating weight and confetti, and the catch card can save a photo. The angler is the owner's scanned fisherman (`assets-src/angler/fisherman.fbx`), decimated and auto-rigged by `modules-src/angler/tools/build-models.mjs` into `public/modules/angler/*.bin`; rerun that script after changing either source and commit the outputs. Brand names and logos on the source boat are painted out by the bake; keep them out. Outfits are fitted per vertex from the look (`scene/Outfit.ts`; vertex colours are sRGB, so materials set `vertexColorGamma`), hats and sunglasses are procedural, the side panel (`ui/Panels.ts`: Wardrobe C, Boat B, Shop P, Achievements J) previews looks and paint live and saves through `/api/angler` `equip`/`buy` (guests change only their page copy), and the boat hull is repainted from a livery and palette at runtime (`scene/Boat.ts`). Shared lakes: `game/Online.ts` joins the lake's AnglerLake instance on arrival; E boards a stopped boat alongside (or leaves), crew fish whenever their captain anchors, the crowd count of nearby boats slows the bite (0.9^n), and the module answers the chat's `tl-host` with a six-character invite code (`shared/Online.ts`), joining from `?room=`. Sky, season, weather and moon follow the real clock in Central time; `?clock=` previews another time in the page only. The lakes stay open water all year (winter brings snow on the shore, not ice); ice fishing would be its own stage. Bite activity saturates smoothly toward 3 (`shared/Catch.ts`), so the Guide's Almanac (G, `ui/Almanac.ts`) can rank good water against the best; everyone sees the bite now and sun and moon times, and the 24-hour outlook, spot ranking and species shares open with the shop's `almanac` item (4,000 TC). Sound is synthesised in the page (`systems/Audio.ts`, N toggles) and every boat leaves a wake (`scene/Wake.ts`). Rules shared with the Worker live in `modules-src/angler/shared/` and must stay renderer-free and deterministic. Details are in `docs/ENGINE-MAP.md`.
