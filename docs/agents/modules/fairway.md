# Fairway Nine

Nine-hole golf module and record book. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- `src/server/routes/fairway.ts`: `/api/fairway`, the Fairway Nine record book in D1 `fairway_records` (migration 0018, also created on first use). GET `?board=round|pin|match` (public) returns the top 20 ascending by value then date, plus the signed-in user's best; POST `record` (signed in, same-origin, Zod, `ON CONFLICT(id) DO NOTHING` on `<user>:<run>`) and `reset-records` (admin). Values: gross strokes (round), centimetres to the pin (pin), finishing place (match).
- Fairway Nine (`modules-src/fairway/`, module id `fairway`) is the first module built on `engine/` from `_template`: a nine-hole projectile-motion course with a four-input concentric swing meter (`systems/SwingMeter.ts`: hold and release for power, tap at the bottom mark for path/push, click and hold at the top for contact/face, release as the inner ring meets its mark for rhythm, a miss costing up to 7 % distance), deterministic ball flight in `systems/BallFlight.ts` (semi-implicit Euler, drag, Magnus lift, wind; shot types full, chip, flop and spinner change launch, spin and how the ball lands and rolls, and travel in the shot params so peers replay them), a procedural skinned golfer posed by `systems/GolferRig.ts` (swing-plane club, two-bone IK arms and legs) that follows the meter, a faded perfect-strike path with a landing zone (`systems/ShotPlanner.ts`, planned power on R/F) and a hole minimap, a bottom shot dock (club bag, shot types, spin ball with a vertical spin meter, power), solo rounds, closest to the pin (a forward tee 140 m out on par 4s and 5s) and three-hole online rooms for up to four over PeerJS (`tl-fairway-<code>`; the host is the authority and every peer replays shots from `(params, seed)`). Holes, tunables and copy live in `data/holes.json`, `tuning.ts` and `config.ts`; trees and grass are InstancedMeshes. Results post to `/api/fairway` and the iframe sends `{type:'tl-fairway', board}` so `FairwayRecordBoard.tsx` under the frame refreshes. Tests: `modules-src/fairway/tests/` via `scripts/fairway-tests.mjs`.
