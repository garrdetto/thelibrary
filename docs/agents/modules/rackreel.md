# Rack & Reel

Babylon fishing and hunting module; Life Sim runs on its code. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- `src/server/routes/rackreel.ts` + `src/server/rackreel.ts`: `/api/rackreel`, Rack & Reel saved progress in D1 `rackreel_profiles` (migration 0026, also created on first use): one JSON profile per user (cash, owned items, loadout, three active tasks, lifetime stats). GET returns the signed-in profile (`{signedIn:false}` otherwise). POST (signed in, same-origin, Zod): `fish` / `game` (species, value, legendary, boat / doe and stand; the server rejects impossible sizes, pays from `modules-src/rackreel/shared/Economy.ts` with a per-account cooldown and advances tasks), `tick` (time outdoors, at most two minutes since the last save), `buy` and `equip` against the shared catalog. Guests play with an in-memory profile only. High scores work like the site's other record books: D1 `rackreel_records` (migration 0030, also created on first use) holds one row per category for every accepted catch that reaches that category's top 20 (`BOARD_LIMIT`), keyed `<user>:<catch>:<category>`, with `detail` JSON (size class and all the animal's measurements). Categories are in `modules-src/rackreel/shared/Records.ts` (fish heaviest and longest; buck antler score, inside spread, points and field-dressed weight; bear weight and skull); the real Wisconsin marks there are shown only as a reference. An accepted (off-cooldown) `fish` or buck/bear `game` POST carries the animal's `seed`, and the server works out every measurement itself from species, size and seed (`measure` in `LootTableManager.ts`, deterministic, so shooter, online guests and server agree), writes each placing row through `saveWithKnockouts` (game `rackreel`, so knocked-out players get the usual notice) and replies with the in-game top three (`records`) and `placed`; it also derives `legendary` from `classify` instead of trusting the browser. GET `?records=1` (top three of every category, for the module) and `?board=<category>` (top 20 plus the viewer's best, for `src/components/RackReelRecordBoard.tsx` under the module frame, which refreshes on the module's `{type:'tl-rackreel', species}` message) are public; POST `reset-records` (admin, optional `category`) clears a board and is the board's admin reset target. Signed-in players share one wallet with Fox Plains and Life Sim: the Fox Plains world holds the balance (`RoomLogic.adjustMoney`, the `MapleStreetWorld` Durable Object's internal `/wallet`, called through `src/server/shared-wallet.ts`); the profile's own `cash` moves in once (`walletMerged`, claimed with a conditional save so it can't happen twice) and every action's net change is applied there, never overdrawing (503 when the world can't be reached). Guests keep separate cash. POST `create-character` `{look, appearance?}` (only the cosmetic `CHARACTER_CHOICES`, once) gives and wears the outfit, stores the look (`appearance`: face, hair, beard, face-shape and body sliders, normalized by `shared/Appearance.ts`) and sets the profile's `verified`, which every profile, old saves included, starts without; the module shows its character creator (`ui/CharacterCreator.ts`) on Head out until the profile is verified. POST `makeover` `{appearance}` is the one free change of look for a character made before the current options: allowed only while the profile's `fitted` is below `APPEARANCE_VERSION`, which it then sets (bump that constant to offer everyone another), and the module shows the creator's Update your look screen on the next Head out for exactly those profiles. POST `reset-character` `{username?}` (administrators only; `?game=foxlake` for Life Sim) unmakes that account's character with `resetCharacter` (`verified` off, default appearance, `fitted` 0; cash, gear, clothes and stats kept) so the creator opens on their next Head out; the console page is `src/components/lifesim/ResetCharacter.tsx` (Module settings → Life Sim → Reset character, `#admin/lifesim-reset`), for the admin's own character or any username. Guests' catches only go on this tab's copy of the board, and guests make a character each visit.
- `src/server/routes/rackreel-lobbies.ts` + `src/server/rackreel-lobbies.ts`: `/api/rackreel-lobbies`, Rack & Reel's open lobbies. D1 `rackreel_lobbies` (migration 0032, also created on first use): one row per PeerJS party code with the host's name, player count and a hash of the host browser's random key. GET (public) lists parties refreshed in the last 45 s (`LOBBY_TTL_MS`, newest first, 20 at most); POST (same-origin, Zod, guests allowed) `open {code, key, players, guest?}` lists or refreshes a party (the host's page heartbeats every 20 s; only the key that listed a live code can refresh it; signed-in hosts show their username) and `close {code, key}` unlists it. The module's Esc menu (`modules-src/rackreel/ui/PauseMenu.ts`: Resume, mouse-look and scope/bow sensitivity plus the flip left/right (on by default) and flip up/down look switches, saved per device in `tl-rackreel-sensitivity`, a Graphics section whose Shadows switch (off by default, saved per device in `tl-rackreel-graphics`) turns sun and moon shadows off through `ctx.shadowLayers.setEnabled`, and applied to the mouse (`EngineSetup` replaces Babylon's mouse input and keeps the mouse-look speed in `ctx.lookSensibility`, since the camera's `angularSensibility` reads 0 once that input is gone) and the arrow keys, Invite player, the Open lobby switch, join by code, Inventory, Record book, and Respawn, which puts the player on foot at the cabin door and moors their own boat back at the dock; every time a player heads out from the start screen they start on foot at the cabin door the same way (switching browser tabs never moves them, so a party can keep fishing together on the boat), and anyone found on foot in deep water off the dock is sent there too; Respawn fully resets the hunter's model (unparented, unscaled, shown) at the door) turns it on and off through `Online.setLobbyOpen`, and the start screen's `LobbyBrowser` lists open lobbies and takes invite codes (`Online.joinAndWait`).
- Rack & Reel (`modules-src/rackreel/`, module id `rackreel`) is a Babylon.js (WebGPU, with WebGL2 as the fallback) fishing and hunting module on a square of northern Wisconsin: fish the lake on foot or from the bass boat at the dock (20 % wider and longer than first built, with four pedestal fishing seats besides the driver's and passenger's; F moves round them), drive a UTV on the trails (its headlights are on from the start, L switches them, `game/Headlights.ts`), and still-hunt whitetails and black bear with a scoped rifle. All models are procedural Blender builds (`tools/build-models.py`, rerun in Blender and commit `public/modules/rackreel/models/*.rrm`). Every animal is sized on creation by `systems/LootTableManager.ts` (`RARITY`): a new all-time record 0.01%, a near-record (from 95% of the book's #3 up to the #1) 0.05%, a trophy 2%, and the rest everyday animals around the species `avg` (60% at or a little below it, bigger ones thinning out exponentially to the trophy line); inside each class sizes fall away exponentially. Bait only changes the trophy odds (`BaitStyle.trophyOdds` in `shared/Economy.ts`), never the record odds. Bounties are flat: $10 a carp, $50 a deer (buck or doe), $100 a bear (`CARP_BOUNTY`, `DEER_BOUNTY`, `BEAR_BOUNTY`). Live bait (nightcrawlers, minnows) sits under a float: holding the reel moves it and pulls it from a nibbling fish. Lures (spinners, bucktails) only draw strikes while being reeled (`EncounterDirector` progress counts only then) and strike straight to a short hook-set window. Reeling is hold-only (left mouse or R): with no fish on, the line comes in `RETRIEVE_SPEED` m/s while held and stops when let go; in a fight, progress only grows while held and letting go settles the line at `restTension` (a fish left alone for `idleLimit` seconds throws the hook). Buck racks score up to 20 points (`MAX_TYPICAL_POINTS`), with a 0.005% freak rack above that (`FREAK_RACK_CHANCE`); every animal rolls a `seed` that, with its size, fixes all its measurements. Fish find a working bait a third sooner (`FISH_SPEEDUP`). Lone bucks are `BUCK_SHARE` 0.52 of new groups (30 % more than before), and the property holds `HERD_SIZE` 12 animals at once (50 % more than the old 8); Life Sim runs the same herd. Calm deer and bears wander, stand looking round or feed, and a buck with a calm doe nearby sometimes walks up and mounts her for a few seconds (`nextIdle`, `IdleAct` in `systems/Herd.ts`, acted out by `Wildlife.ts`). Every five minutes (`HERD_TURNOVER_SECONDS` in `systems/Herd.ts`) the deer on the property run off and are removed, and a fresh batch enters at random spots out of sight (`Wildlife.turnover`). Double right-click locks the scope up so left-click alone fires (trackpads); right-click again lowers it. While driving (car, UTV, quad or a boat under way), the camera turns with the vehicle (`systems/VehicleCamera.ts`): the mouse and arrow keys still look around, and `LOOK_HOLD` seconds after the last look the view eases back behind the vehicle; Life Sim and Tello drive through the same camera. The dock gets procedural wood (`world/Surfaces.ts`, patterns in `world/SurfacePatterns.ts`). A downed buck or bear the player shot tags itself once they walk (or ride the UTV) within 10 yards (`TAG_RANGE`), and E works from 10 yards; the catch card then shows the top three of every category that animal counts toward, and K opens the full high-score book. Encounters come every one to three minutes of active exploring (`EncounterDirector.ts`). Trophy-class results post to the `rackreel` module-scores board (Trophy Room). The logo is `public/modules/rackreel/logo.webp`. Details are in `docs/ENGINE-MAP.md`. Standalone Rack & Reel is always daytime (the clock holds at 2 PM, `DAYLIGHT_HOUR`) except during Wild Man's carp bowfishing tour, which skips to full dark and runs the night clock (75 s per hour, sun then full moon) until the tour ends; `?hour=` lets the clock run for testing. The lake country has nine ladder stands and two box blinds (`world/TreeStand.ts`). Hitting a tree in the UTV at half its top speed or more throws the driver through the windshield to roll on the ground ahead and stand back up (`game/Ejection.ts`). At night Wisco Wild Man (Meshy character at his cooler down the shore from the dock) takes you night carp bowfishing on his boat: he drives on autopilot along the shoreline, island flats or the back bay under the interstate bridge (F), blurts out redneck lines on his own (Space never makes him talk), and on the way home (Tab) sends it full speed over the island and beaches the boat beside his camp (`planHomeStunt`, `sendItHome` in `systems/BowRoutes.ts`), you walk anywhere on the boat (W/A/S/D; T/G sets his speed), right-click (or double right-click to lock it) raises the bow and zooms down the sight, left-click shoots (aim low: carp are drawn where refraction puts them, 1.6× their length: water magnifies by a third, plus 20 % so they read clearly), fight, tub and get paid (`carp`, 10–30 in, bowfishing only; `CARP_BANDS`: 75 % 10–15 in, 15 % 15–20, 5 % 20–25, 5 % over 25, longer rarer inside each band); up to four party members can share the boat; in daylight, E at his camp starts the tour by skipping to full dark and boarding in one step, and it turns night for the rest of the party too. Hands stay on the gear through a two-bone arm solve (`systems/ArmReach.ts`, `game/Grips.ts`): two hands on the rod for the cast, while waiting and while reeling, both hands on the rifle carried and shouldered, bow hand on the riser and draw hand on the string; poses were checked against Meshy reference clips measured in Blender. The hunter is a skinned Meshy model (`hunter.rrs`, built by `tools/builders_skinned.py` from `assets-src/rackreel/meshy/hunter-rig.glb` and `hunter-clips.json`; `tools/ingest-hunter.py` turns the raw Meshy downloads into those and the textures; task ids in `hunter.meshy.json`): walk, run, idle, crouch and backing-up clips move the body (`systems/Locomotion.ts`) and the hand poses and arm reach ride on top (`game/Avatar.ts`). Wild Man stays on the rigid 11-joint rig. Players pick a face, hair and facial hair (mixed from 13 painted heads on a head atlas, `game/HeadPaint.ts`), five face-shape sliders (`systems/FaceShape.ts`) and three body sliders (`shared/Appearance.ts`) in the character creator. A landed fish flops and splashes at the angler's feet before the catch card. Outfits are Meshy retextures of the hunter (`HUNTER_COMBOS` / `HUNTER_PANTS` / `HUNTER_HATS` in `tools/builders_meshy_hunter.py`): camo, flannel, canvas, blaze parka, ocean angler sun shirt and tank top (arms repainted in the base skin tone) under each vest; camo, jeans, waders, blaze bibs and khakis; blaze, camo, bucket, backwards trucker, visor and straw cowboy hats. Everyday clothes (`assets-src/rackreel/meshy/hunter-everyday.meshy.json`): white, black, navy and grey T-shirts, grey and black hoodies, an Oxford shirt, a polo, a denim jacket and a cable-knit sweater (vests don't show over them), dark jeans, chinos, joggers and cargo shorts, and No hat (`cap-none`); `ingest-hunter.py` takes `HUNTER_ONLY=<names>` to ingest only some outfits. Details are in `docs/ENGINE-MAP.md` under rackreel.
