# Kestrel County

PlayCanvas county world with flight. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- Kestrel County (`modules-src/county/`, module id `county`, in `src/lib/modules.ts`) is a separate 4 × 4 km PlayCanvas world planned with an airport, flyable helicopters, Fox Plains' driving physics and mowing, in a glossy realistic-clipart style: real-world proportions, modelled detail, bevelled edges, vertex-coloured finishes from `src/style/Kit.ts`, Neutral tone mapping, and no blocky or balloony shapes. Phase 1 streams the county in 256 m chunks (`src/world/Chunks.ts`, heights from a worker) with graded terrain (`shared/Terrain.ts`) and a road network generated from `shared/Layout.ts` by `shared/Roads.ts`: one cross-section (carriageway, curb, verge, sidewalk), junction curb returns, driveway and ramp curb cuts with aprons, checked by `tests/roads.test.ts` (`scripts/county-tests.mjs` in `pnpm test`). Walking and driving import Fox Plains 2's physics, avatar and camera unchanged, and every built-in, bundled and published Fox Plains vehicle (drafts with `?dev=1`) can be spawned from the F8 developer panel. Phase 2 adds a flyable helicopter and trainer plane: renderer-free flight models (`shared/HeliFlight.ts`, `shared/PlaneFlight.ts`) with Easy and Pro assist (`shared/FlightAssist.ts`) and node tests (`tests/flight.test.ts`), put on Rapier bodies by `src/flight/Flyer.ts`, plus the airport's runway, taxiway, apron and helipads (`shared/Airport.ts`). Phase 3 adds the terminal, tower, hangar and windsock (`src/models/AirportBuildings.ts`) and AI air traffic as a pure function of real time (`shared/AirTraffic.ts`, tested in `tests/traffic.test.ts`, posed by `src/flight/TrafficManager.ts`, with go-arounds when the runway is occupied). `?lookdev=1` keeps the Phase 0 scene. Never fork Fox Plains rules or physics into it. Details in `modules-src/county/README.md`.
