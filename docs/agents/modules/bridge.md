# Bridge Builder

Canvas 2D truss builder module and its saved progress. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- Bridge Builder (`modules-src/bridge/`, module id `bridge`) is a Canvas 2D truss builder (plain TypeScript, no engine): 100 levels in ten chapters of ten (`shared/Levels.ts`: `chapterPlan` sets each level's span in 2 m grid squares, anchors on the banks, on cliff faces, on high rock and on pillars, no-build ship channels, water depth and the vehicles; `CHAPTERS`, `NAMES`, `TIPS`). Beams join grid points and run only flat, at 45° or at 90° (`beamProblem` in `shared/Design.ts`), up to two squares long (cables eight); road is flat only and is the only thing wheels touch. Materials (`shared/Materials.ts`): road and wood from level 1, steel from 11, cable (tension only) from 21, steel road from 31. `shared/Sim.ts` is a deterministic XPBD simulation (32 substeps a frame): joints get half of each beam's mass, beams are compliant distance constraints whose force is read from the multiplier, a beam snaps when its frame-averaged force passes its strength (cables never push), and vehicles (`shared/Vehicles.ts`: hatchback to 32 t crane truck, all original) are rigid particle frames whose wheels collide with road and the banks and drive at their own speed; a level passes when every vehicle reaches the flag and fails when one falls or stalls. Every level has a reference bridge found by `tools/solve-levels.ts` (run with `npx tsx`; tries truss shapes below, above or both, depths 1–4, wood or steel, road or steel road, single or cross bracing, cheapest first, and keeps the first that passes with every beam under 90 % of its strength) and baked into `shared/Reference.ts`; the budget is the reference cost times a slack that tightens from 1.32 to 1.10, and stars come at 92 % and 80 % of budget. Rerun the solver after changing materials, vehicles, levels or the sim, and keep `tests/levels-*.test.ts` (every reference crosses with no breaks) green. The page (`ui/App.ts`, `ui/Editor.ts` with undo/redo and long beams split at existing joints, `ui/Renderer.ts` themed scenery and drawn vehicles, `ui/Sound.ts` synthesised sounds) shows the level select, drag-to-build editor, stress colours, test speed and result card; drafts are kept per device (`tl-bridge-draft-<n>`) and solved bridges go to `/api/bridge`. Levels open three ahead of the furthest solved one.
- `src/server/routes/bridge.ts`: `/api/bridge`, Bridge Builder progress in D1 `bridge_progress` (migration 0048, also created on first use): one row per user and level with the cheapest passing design (packed beams `[x1,y1,x2,y2,material]`), its cost and stars. GET returns the signed-in player's progress, stars and points (`{signedIn:false}` otherwise); `?level=<n>` returns that level's saved design. POST `solve {level, beams}` (signed in, same-origin, Zod) re-checks the design with `designProblem` from `modules-src/bridge/shared/Design.ts`, recomputes cost and stars against the level's budget (`getLevel`), keeps it only when it is cheaper than the saved one and then writes the player's total to the server-only `bridge` module-scores board (100 points a star plus the percent of budget left, summed over levels) through `saveWithKnockouts`. It trusts the page that the vehicles crossed; it never trusts the cost.
