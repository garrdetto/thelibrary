# Sun Vault, Beanstalk Bounty and Mo' Dollaz

Original reel modules on play credits. Admins add money to anyone's shared slot wallet from People & permissions (`src/components/admin/SlotWallet.tsx`, the Slot balance group in `PersonAccess.tsx`) through `/api/slot-wallet` (`src/server/routes/slot-wallet.ts`, admin only): GET `?id=<userId>` returns the balance and `canChange`, POST `{action:'add', id, cents}` (1 cent to $1,000,000) adds credits with `SlotEngine.addCredits` and saves with the wallet's version check, retrying on a clash; only the owner can change another administrator's balance. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- `src/server/routes/sunvault.ts` + `src/server/sunvault.ts`: `/api/sunvault`, Sun Vault's saved balances. D1 `sunvault_profiles` (migration 0040, also created on first use): one row per user with the serialized engine (`SlotEngine.toState`, RNG included), the daily streak and last collect day, and the open round's win, bet and forced flag, saved with an optimistic `version` check (a second tab gets 409). GET returns the snapshot, daily status and, mid-respin, the grid to resume; GET without a session returns `signedIn: false` and the page plays practice credits. POST (signed in, same-origin, Zod): `spin` (bet from `BET_LEVELS_CENTS`; the Worker runs the spin, so balances and records can't be forged; `force` is admin only and marks the round unranked) and `collect` ($500 plus $50 per consecutive prior day, days in Central time, `engine/daily.ts`). When a round ends the Worker writes the server-only boards `sunvault-win`, `sunvault-multiple` (hundredths of the bet) and `sunvault-total` (running sum per user) into `module_scores`; `/api/module-scores` refuses client posts to boards marked `serverOnly`.
- Sun Vault (`modules-src/sunvault/`, module id `sunvault`) is an original Egyptian-temple five-reel, 25-line spinner on play credits (no money, nothing saved): React 19 + Tailwind HUD over a raw WebGPU renderer (no three.js; WGSL reel shader with motion blur and barrel curve, gold-foil sweep, compute particles, procedural canvas atlas) and a WebGPU-required fallback card. The math is pure TypeScript in `engine/` (seeded RNG, weighted strips, about 96% long-run return checked by `tests/` and the developer panel's simulator): a Sphinx collector that can expand to 2×2/3×3 and multiply its collect, free spins with a growing Sphinx Zone, Vault Respins (Hold & Spin, GRAND for 15), and a random match-3 Jackpot Pick. Bets run $0.20–$5 (`BET_LEVELS_CENTS`, every level whole cents so rounding never moves the return; features opened at an older, larger bet finish at that bet). It is phone friendly: `ui/useViewport.ts` switches between a desktop layout, a phone portrait layout (jackpot plaques, big SPIN row) and a short-landscape layout (controls in a side column) without remounting the canvas, every control is at least 44 px, safe areas are respected, and it opts into the site's mobile full screen (`initEmbedFull`, a `.tl-full-only` Exit full screen button). Rounds of 15×/50× the bet end with the shared Big/Huge Win celebration (`modules-src/_shared/win-fx.ts`), and collection features (Sphinx collect, Vault Respins count, Jackpot Pick, temple total, daily collect) add up in big animated letters in the middle of the screen with its `tally()`; banners are kept for triggers. Signed-in members spin on a balance kept by `/api/sunvault` with a daily collect and three high-score boards under the frame; guests and practice mode run the same engine in the page. On all three slot machines (Sun Vault, Beanstalk Bounty, Mo' Dollaz) the developer panel is shown only to site administrators, and guests who run out of play credits are refilled automatically. Keep names and art original; never copy a commercial machine's names, characters or art. Details in `docs/ENGINE-MAP.md` and its `README.md`.
- Beanstalk Bounty (`modules-src/beanstalk/`, module id `beanstalk`) is an original fairy-tale five-reel, four-row, 1,024-ways spinner on Canvas 2D (no WebGPU needed) with a React 19 + Tailwind HUD. Its math is pure TypeScript in `engine/` (deterministic, shared with the Worker, about 97% long-run return checked by `tests/` via `scripts/beanstalk-tests.mjs`): Sprout Wilds (×1/×2/×3, multiplying every way through them) climb up their reel and spread Vine Wilds sideways (`grow`, `GrowStep`), three Cloud Castles open the Sky Garden (5 free spins where every wild sticks), and Haywire (about 1 in 75 paid spins: Overgrowth, Giant Stalk or Bean Burst ×2/×3/×5). Bets run $0.25–$5 (`BET_LEVELS_CENTS`, default $1; a winning way pays at least 1 cent; a Sky Garden opened at an older, bigger bet finishes at its own bet). It fits phone portrait (two-row footer), phone landscape (controls in a right-hand rail) and desktop, with 44 px tap targets and safe areas, and opts into the site's mobile full screen (`initEmbedFull()`, an "Exit full screen" header button). Rounds of 15× or 50× the bet end with the shared Big/Huge Win celebration (`modules-src/_shared/win-fx.ts`); the Sky Garden adds each free spin's win to a big SKY GARDEN total in the middle of the reels (faded while they spin) that finishes as SKY GARDEN WON; ordinary wins keep the board plaque. Signed-in members spin on the same wallet as Sun Vault through `/api/beanstalk` (`src/server/routes/beanstalk.ts` + `src/server/beanstalk.ts`: D1 `beanstalk_profiles`, migration 0046, also created on first use, holds the machine's state and open round; the balance stays in `sunvault_profiles`, and both rows are written in one batch, each update conditioned on the other row's version so they never drift) with the shared daily collect and three server-only boards (`beanstalk-win`, `beanstalk-multiple`, `beanstalk-total`). Sun Vault also has a Haywire (about 1 in 100 paid base spins: Wild Surge, Coin Storm or Double Trouble). Keep names and art original. Details in `docs/ENGINE-MAP.md` and its `README.md`.
- Mo' Dollaz (`modules-src/modollaz/`, module id `modollaz`) is an original cash-and-bling five-reel, 20-line spinner on Canvas 2D (`render/stage.ts`, procedural art in `render/art.ts`: a money-bag mascot, cash bills and gold coins showing their values, a gold-bar wild, a vault-door scatter) with a React 19 + Tailwind HUD built phone first (portrait, a side control rail in short landscape via the `short` variant, desktop; tap targets of 44 px or more, safe areas, mobile full screen through `_shared/embed-full.js`). Its maths is the Life Sim casino's `dollaz` family (`modules-src/foxlake/casino/math/machines.ts`: `MO_DOLLAZ_ID`, `dollazCollect`, `playSpin`; about 95.5% long-run return, checked by `modules-src/modollaz/tests/` via `scripts/modollaz-tests.mjs` and by `modules-src/foxlake/tests/casino.test.ts`), wrapped in cents by `engine/game.ts` (`DollazGame`, bets $0.25 to $5, forced outcomes for the developer panel): a money bag on reel 1 with any cash bill on reels 2-5 starts Cash Collect (any bill may also start it at random): the bills are paid and copied onto a 5×5 board, a 2×2 collector zone collects the coins that land inside it and then hops to every new bill and collects it, and 5, 9 and 12 bills grow it to 3×3 (+3 spins), 4×4 (+2) and 5×5 (+1, no more bills); three vault scatters give 8 free games without the royals (Cash Collect can start inside them); any wild may start a 15-safe match-3 jackpot pick (fixed jackpots of 20×, 60×, 100×, 200× and 2,000× the bet); bills, vaults and wilds fill three meters (levels 1-5) that guarantee their feature when full; features that land together play one after another (`chain`). A whole round resolves in one call, so signed-in members spin on the shared vault wallet through `/api/modollaz` (`src/server/routes/modollaz.ts` + `src/server/modollaz.ts`: D1 `modollaz_profiles`, migration 0047, also created on first use, keeps the meters and stats; wallet and machine rows are written in one batch, each conditioned on the other's version, as Beanstalk does) with the shared daily collect and three server-only boards (`modollaz-win`, `modollaz-multiple`, `modollaz-total`); guests and practice mode run the same engine in the page. Big and Huge wins and the Cash Collect, free games and jackpot totals use `modules-src/_shared/win-fx.ts` (`createWinFx`: `celebrate(win, bet)` for BIG WIN from 15× and HUGE WIN from 50× with a count-up, tap to skip; `tally(title)` for a big centred running total), shared by every slot. Keep names and art original; never copy a commercial machine's names, characters or art. Details in `docs/ENGINE-MAP.md` and its `README.md`.
