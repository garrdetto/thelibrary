# The Hood

Shared street of player-owned houses. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- `src/server/routes/hood.ts` + `src/server/hood.ts` + `src/server/hood-lot.ts`: `/api/hood`, The Hood. GET lists a street (`?street=`, 12 lots: owner, home/visitor presence, the public `shell`); POST `claim` (signed in, same-origin, Zod) moves one household per account into an empty lot with the starter house; a same-origin WebSocket upgrade (members by session, guests by a zod-checked key) walks onto a claimed lot through the Durable Object `HoodLot` (binding `HOOD_LOT`, one per `<street>:<lot>`), which runs `modules-src/hood/shared/Room.ts` as the authority, saves to D1 `hood_lots` (migration 0023, also created on first use) and borrows a visiting member's Sim from their home lot's object (`/depart`), handing it back with its needs on leaving (`/return`). GET `?street=&town=1` returns the street's service calls from the last hour (D1 `hood_requests`, migration 0024, also created on first use; written by the lot's object when a household calls a service) and the residents on a shift (from each lot's public shell), never owner ids.
- The Hood (`modules-src/hood/`, module id `hood`) is a shared street of player-owned houses on `engine/` (three.js WebGPU, WebGL2 fallback). The street view and each lot are separate scenes, so only one detailed lot is loaded at a time. Members move one household in, build rooms, walls, doors, windows and floors, design the exterior (`shared/Style.ts`: two-tone cladding with brick or stone wainscots, trim, shutters, window grids, doors, lanterns, hip/gable/flat roofs in four roofings, plus canopies, porch roofs, pergolas, fences with gates and chimneys), furnish from `shared/Catalog.ts` plus `shared/CatalogMore.ts` (141 items in eleven categories, models in `src/models/Furniture.ts` and `src/models/FurnitureMore.ts`), and look after up to eight residents with six needs, utility-AI autonomy, social interactions and freelance work. Families (`shared/Family.ts`): residents are babies, toddlers, children, teens or adults by `age` in sim days on the household clock and grow up as it runs; owners add, rename, restyle and move out residents from the Family panel (`src/ui/FamilyPanel.ts`); two close adult housemates can try for a baby, who is born a sim day later and takes after both parents; only adults work. Anyone on the street can walk into any yard; the inside of a house is only for the household and for visitors the owner lets in at the door while home, and the guest list clears when the owner leaves. Visitors in the yard never receive interior objects, floors or Sims. Both ends of the road hold a working town (`shared/Town.ts`, `shared/TownSim.ts`): police, fire, hospital (EMS and nurses), City Works, a lawn company, a prison, a gas station, a burger restaurant and a resort, each with its own vehicles and staff. The town is a pure function of the street number, the real clock and the street's service calls, so it runs with nobody online and every page draws the same thing; emergencies link the jobs (a fire brings the engine, police and EMS, a patient goes to the nurses, an arrest goes to the guards for booking) and routines do too (lunch runs, fuel stops, the inmate work crew with City Works, the resort shuttle). Residents take one of ten careers (`shared/Jobs.ts`, `shared/Careers.ts`): a shift is real time, tasks and live calls raise performance, pay and promotions follow, and the street sees them at work. Households call services from the lot (police, fire, EMS, burgers, mowing, City Works, a resort day); a responder vehicle drives out and the effect lands when it arrives. Keep the town deterministic (no `Math.random`, no ticking state) and rules in `shared/`. `?solo=1` (and the Practice lot button) runs the same `Room` in the page, unsaved. Residents and furniture are Blender models: `modules-src/hood/tools/build-sim.py` (skinned resident with Feminine/Slim/Broad morph targets, four hair meshes, recolourable slots) and `tools/build-furniture.py` (one node per original catalog id plus the street maple; the newer items in `shared/CatalogMore.ts` use their procedural models) write `assets-src/models/hood/`; run them in Blender, then `pnpm assets` and commit `public/models/hood/`. The Look's `body` (masculine/feminine; older saves infer it from hair) drives the Feminine morph. If the models can't load (or with `?sims=0`/`?props=0`) the procedural figure and furniture are used. Rules live in `modules-src/hood/shared/` (renderer-free, deterministic, tested by `node scripts/hood-tests.mjs`); keep them out of `src/`. Details in `modules-src/hood/README.md`.
