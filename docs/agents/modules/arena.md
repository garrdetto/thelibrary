# Vector Arena

Babylon first-person arena module. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- `src/server/routes/arena-stats.ts`: `/api/arena-stats`, Vector Arena's 1v1 Duel record book. D1 `arena_duel_stats` (one row of career totals per player: duels, wins, eliminations, deaths, rounds fired, rounds that hit) and `arena_duel_runs` (each duel counted once by `<user>:<run>`), migration 0044, also created on first use. GET `?sort=kd|accuracy` (public) returns the top 20 by K/D ratio (kills over deaths, at least one death counted) or accuracy (players with 30 rounds fired or more) and the viewer's own line; POST (signed in, same-origin, Zod) `match` from the arena page (`modes/DuelTally.ts`) when a duel ends, admin `reset-records`. Shown under the frame by `src/components/ArenaDuelBoard.tsx` (sort tabs, K/D first).
- Vector Arena (`modules-src/arena/`, module id `arena`) is a Babylon.js (bundled `@babylonjs/core`, imported by path, WebGL2) first-person shooter in an original symmetrical military sci-fi arena built from primitives (bases with canopies, ramps, catwalks, gate arches, chest-high bulwarks, olive cover crates, a centre pylon) in an outdoor valley: painted daytime sky with clouds used for PBR reflections, a warm sun with soft shadows, haze, procedural hills and mountains, PBR materials with canvas-painted textures and normal maps, and a bloom/ACES post pipeline. It takes broad cues from early-2000s military sci-fi shooters but copies no map, asset, logo or name from any game. Live matches run on the shared Railway service as the `/arena` namespace (`server/arena.mjs`, shared events and rifle stats in `server/arena/`); the client connects to `VITE_POOL_SERVER_URL` by default (`?server=<origin>` overrides, `?solo=1` stays offline) and falls back to solo practice when the server is unreachable. A mode menu (`client/ui/Menu.ts`, M or the Modes button) picks 1v1 Duel and 2v2 Squads (server rooms with matchmaking, six-character private codes that also answer the chat's `tl-host`, a countdown, a score limit and clock, results and a rematch), Open Battle (the drop-in team match), the Training Range (sixty seconds of pop-up targets, board `arena-range`), Rolling Siege (board `arena-horde`, time held in ms: 2,000 shared rounds against Meshy-made raiders in wheelchairs built in Blender by `modules-src/arena/tools/build-raider.py`, who climb the deck ramps, swing bats and melt when killed; 40 health so two body bursts or one all-head burst from the Battle Rifle kills; spawn rate and speed in `server/arena/horde.mjs` are tuned so nobody lasts past two minutes, which `arena.test.mjs` checks) and Free Practice. The Battle Rifle does 7 a round (14 on the head), so a player goes down to five body bursts or three head bursts. 1v1 Duels keep each player's K/D ratio and accuracy (`/api/arena-stats`, board under the frame sorted by K/D or accuracy). Holding right-click with the Battle Rifle zooms 2× (`zoom` in `server/arena/weapons.mjs`) while it still fires. Both boards are grouped under `arena` in `src/lib/module-scores.ts` and shown under the frame. Details in `modules-src/arena/README.md`.
