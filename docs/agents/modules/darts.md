# Darts

Darts module, record book, settings and online rooms. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- `src/server/routes/darts-records.ts` + `src/server/darts-records.ts`: `/api/darts-records`, the Darts record book, one per board (`steel`, `spider`) and format (`501`, `301`, `cricket`, `countup`; rules in `modules-src/darts/shared/Records.ts`). D1 `darts_legs` and `darts_matches` log every saved leg and match once (`<user>:<run>`), `darts_bests` keeps each player's best with `times` (how often they hit it; ties sort by more times, then who set it first) and `darts_averages` their running darts and points/marks (migration 0031, also created on first use). GET `?board=&mode=` (public) returns the bests, the top five all-time averages (PPD for 301/501, MPR for Cricket, from 30 darts) and the signed-in player's own; `&players=a,b` returns those players' totals. POST (signed in, same-origin, Zod) `leg` and `average` from the page for local play, admin `reset-records`; online rooms save their members' results from the Durable Object.
- `src/server/routes/darts-settings.ts` + `src/server/darts-settings.ts`: `/api/darts-settings`, the administrator's Darts settings (`modules-src/darts/shared/Settings.ts`) in D1 `library_settings` key `darts-settings` (created on first use). GET (public) returns them merged over the defaults; POST (admin, same-origin, Zod, then `sanitizeSettings`) `save {settings}` or `reset`. The console page is `src/components/darts/DartsSettings.tsx` (Module settings → Darts, `#admin/darts`).
- `src/server/routes/darts.ts` + `src/server/darts-room.ts`: `/api/darts`, Darts online rooms. GET `?room=<code>` says whether a room is open, full or playing; a same-origin WebSocket upgrade with `mode=create|join` (members by session, guests by a zod-checked key) takes a seat in the Durable Object `DartsRoom` (binding `DARTS_ROOM`, one per `room:<code>`, up to four seats, nothing stored). The room is the authority: THROW_MADE carries only the aim, speed, yaw and steadiness; the room rolls the release error with `crypto.getRandomValues`, flies and scores the dart with `modules-src/darts/shared/` and broadcasts THROW_MADE, TURN_SWITCH and GAME_OVER (45 s turn timeout, 90 s seat hold), and saves signed-in players' finished legs and match averages to the record book. It reads the admin's meter settings when a room opens and applies them (`applyMeterSettings`) right before each launch. RESTART_REQUEST is the pause menu's Request Restart: the room restarts the match once every connected player has asked (RESTART_VOTES; votes lapse after 30 s).
- Darts (`modules-src/darts/`, module id `darts`) is built on `engine/` from `_template`: a regulation board (steel-tip bristle, or the red and black Spider 360 3000 soft-tip in its housing with an LED score display, white plastic dividers and tip holes in rows that follow the rings, three straight rows of full-size holes in each double and triple and no divider between the inner and outer bull, picked as Steel Tip or Soft Tip (the default; Double Bull on for 301/501, split bull in Cricket) at the top of the setup form or in the pause menu and kept per device; the soft-tip doubles and triples are 10 mm deep instead of 8 (`SOFT_RADII`, `radiiFor`, scored by passing the board to `scoreAt`/`simulate`/`targetPoint`); 4096² board maps and a 1440-pixel render floor on the high and ultra tiers) (radii in `shared/Board.ts`, bull at 1.73 m, oche at 2.37 m) in a pub corner, drawn from 2048² canvas maps (`render/BoardTexture.ts`: face, packed roughness/metal so wires and numbers shine, normal map) under real metal spider wires and a shadowed board lamp. Throwing uses the cross meter by default; the administrator can hide the classic one-stage meter (the default), offer it under Throw meter in the setup form and pause menu (cross first or classic first, kept per device as `tl-darts-meter-v2`) or make it the only meter (`meter.classic` `off`/`option`/`default`/`only`). The admin console's Darts settings (`src/components/darts/DartsSettings.tsx`, `/api/darts-settings`, rules in `shared/Settings.ts`) also set the meter's swing time and speed range, its zones (Perfect window, solid green, fade, yellow, side reach) and miss distances, the setup defaults (board, computer level, double out, Double Bull), both boards' colours and the flight, barrel, shaft and point colours and barrel finishes; the page loads them at boot (`net/Settings.ts`, defaults on failure) and every default is the module as built. Aiming and throwing are separate: tap or click the board to put the target there, or drag it (a press within `grabRadiusPx` 40 px of the target picks it up and moves it by the drag, anywhere else it jumps under the press first; `systems/ThrowControl.ts`); the board never starts or stops a meter. The round Throw button at the bottom right (`#throw-btn`, shown only on a person's turn at this screen, dimmed while a dart is in the air) runs the meters. The cross meter: hold Throw and a height meter swings, let go to lock it, then press Throw or Space again to stop a side meter that sweeps across it at the green (`ThrowInput.cross`, `ThrowIntent.cross`, `crossMiss`: high/low from the first, left/right from the second) (`systems/ThrowInput.ts`, steps from `throwStep`: the meter swings 0.7×–1.2× `IDEAL_SPEED` every `chargeSeconds` (1.76 s) and is always on screen by the board (`Overlay.placeMeter` → `ui/MeterLayout.ts`: beside the board on the right, above it on the right in a tall phone view, otherwise against the right edge over the number ring, always below the top bar and clear of the Throw button) in a bevelled frame, with a solid green Perfect centre fading to yellow; with the classic meter letting go of Throw throws; keyboard: arrows move the target, hold Space (or Enter on the focused button) for Throw). The camera frames the board at `boardHalfExtent` 0.26 m (`minFov` 10°, `render/CameraFit.ts`): where the meter stops sets the accuracy (`METER`, `meterReach` and `meterZone` in `shared/Physics.ts`; the meter fades green → yellow → red): the Perfect window between the meter's two white lines (±0.24 % of the ideal speed, inside the ±0.57 % solid green that fades to full yellow by ±3 %, `METER.fade`) lands exactly on the aim point, the rest of the green within seven soft-tip holes (3.4 mm each) of it in a random direction, scaled by how far from the middle the release was (never more), the miss eases slowly up to 16 holes across the wide yellow, and in the red the dart also drops (soft) or rises (hard). The computer keeps its Gaussian release error by `SPREAD` level, and the dart flies with gravity and drag. `shared/Rules.ts` is the single authority for 301/501 (double in/out, master out (`masterOut`: finish on a double, a triple or the bullseye; it overrides double out, so steel-tip master-out legs stay off the record book), busts, and the soft-tip-only Double Bull option `doubleBull`, where `scoredDart` turns an outer-bull dart into a 50 double), Cricket and Count Up with legs and sets; keep `shared/` renderer-free and deterministic because the `DartsRoom` Durable Object runs it. Every person throws with the same steadiness (`HUMAN_LEVEL` in `session/Session.ts`, the same as online rooms); the only difficulty setting is the computer's level. Solo, against the computer (`shared/Cpu.ts`, four levels), pass-and-play for up to four, teams, a tournament, or online rooms (answers `tl-host`, joins from `?room=`). Teams (local only, `MatchConfig.teamPlay`): two to four teams of one or two, any player a person or the computer, either a shared score (`stacked`: teammates take turns on one score) or own scores (`regular`: up to eight seats, a checkout or Cricket close-out wins the leg for the team and round limits add teammates' points); team matches never go on the record book. Tournament (`shared/Tournament.ts`, `ui/Bracket.ts`): eight players, one against one, best of three legs through quarter-finals, semi-finals and a final on either board, with a leg rotation (format of legs 1, 2 and 3) for the whole tournament; each leg is its own single-leg match, and computer-only matches can be watched or simulated (`shared/QuickSim.ts`). On the soft-tip board, a 301, 501 or Count Up turn that isn't a bust or a checkout earns a soft-tip award (`softTipAward` in `shared/Rules.ts`): 100 or more is LOW TON!, exactly 150 HAT TRICK! with three blue top hats, more than 150 HIGH TON! (the steel tip keeps its Ton and 180 calls), drawn by `award` in `ui/Overlay.ts`. Solo eight-round Count Up also posts to the `darts` module-scores board. Each board keeps its own record book (`ui/RecordBook.ts`, `/api/darts-records`): fewest darts to finish a 501 or 301 leg (steel tip needs double out; soft tip may single out and defaults to Double Bull on) or to close out Cricket, and the best eight-round Count Up, from any leg alone, against anyone or in a tournament (local legs save as Player 1, the signed-in account, when a person throws for Player 1); all-time averages (PPD, MPR) count only real-against-real matches or ones against the Pro computer, show in the record book under the module frame (`src/components/DartsRecordBoard.tsx`, board and format tabs, top five averages; the module posts `{type:'tl-darts', board, mode, saved}` so it follows the setup form and refreshes after a save) and live in the All-Time Averages panel during a match (bottom-left in landscape, above the Throw button in portrait). The darts themselves follow the board: the Meshy-made steel-tip model (`assets-src/models/darts/dart-steel.glb`, fitted in Blender by `modules-src/darts/tools/fit-dart.py`) whose `flight` material takes each player's colour, and the soft-tip model built in Blender by `modules-src/darts/tools/build-soft-dart.py` after the Meshy soft-tip dart (black moulded point, knurled metallic gray barrel, black shaft, four white standard flights in a cross on `flight-white`, which is never tinted); after either, run `pnpm assets` (`render/Dart.ts`, lathe-built fallback). Tests: `node scripts/darts-tests.mjs`.
