# Short Track Showdown

Short-track racing module, custom cars, AI paint and handling. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- Lap records are stored per track and mode. Short Track Showdown (`modules-src/shorttrack/`, served as `public/modules/shorttrack.html`) posts to `/api/racing` with track `shorttrack` (Showdown Bullring), `dells` (Dells Short Track), `dellswide` (Dells Wide Oval: the Dells layout 8° flatter, 30 m turn radius, 10% wider, bare grass infield with no pit road, garages, haulers or pylon via `bareInfield`), `prison` (Prison Speedway: a blend of the three asphalt layouts with more grip for every car, `theme:'prison'` adds the castle-style stone guard towers, crenellated curtain wall, razor wire, night searchlights and orange-clad inmate crowd from `modules-src/shorttrack/prison-speedway.js`) `parkoval` (Thrill Park Speedway: a 1/3-mile (536 m centreline: 163 m straights, 33.5 m turns) asphalt oval with 12° turns and 4° straights, `theme:'park'` sets it inside the Thrill Park 2 park "Land of Speed" (the same saved park as the Demo Derby): `tools/park-scenery.mjs` clears and levels a 272 × 120 m lot south of the rides (`LOT`/`LOT_TILES`: about 10 m past the back-stretch wall and 13 m past the turn walls; quarter turn 2, so the rides rise behind the back stretch), eases the map border to the lot height, first lays `TALL_COASTERS` (four extra-tall out-and-back designs registered only in the tool, 26–46 m lifts: Skyline Tempest, Sky Bolt, Thunder Titan, Timber Giant) along the back stretch within `TALL_ROWS` of the lot, then builds extra coasters (the park's own `COASTER_PRESETS`) and flat rides on the free land within `RING` (14) tiles of the lot, nearest spot first (`RING_RIDES`, through the park's own `preset`/`flat` commands), so rides stand right beside the turns and back stretch, and writes `data/park.json`; `park-speedway.ts` loads it at runtime, the module's own grass only fills the land outside `PARK_MAP` and its tree ring is left out; grandstands and crowd run only along the front stretch (`STAND0`/`STAND1` in `render-three/main.js`, 14 m past each end, closed with end walls) so the park shows behind the turns and back stretch; away from the stands the light towers stand at `HW+5` instead of behind the stands; it has more grip for every car (`grip` 1.2, `apronGrip` 0.94, `gripUp` 1.15); new track, so it has no classic recordings and the golden tests cover only `CLASSIC_TRACKS`) or `dirt` (Dells Dirt Oval, dirt handling; picked in the module menu via `?track=`, which defaults to `prison`, listed first; the walled infield cell house comes from `buildPrisonInfield`; day or night is a switch on the track picker, `#sNight`, carried through the track reload as `?night=1`) and modes `time-attack`, `race` (shown as Solo Race) and `multiplayer`; races start with a half-lap rolling start from the back stretch, two wide with a car length between rows (`GRID_S`, `GRID_ROW`); on the next vehicle model `gridLanes` puts the outside file up by the wall (1.1 m off it, 1.3 m on dirt) and the inside file a car length further down the track and staggered a car length back (the classic model keeps -3.6/0.6 side by side for its golden traces); the AI drives every car, the player's included (`c.auto`), in a rigid formation at `FORM_V` (the slowest centerline speed both files can hold through the turns, at most `CFG.aiPaceSpeed`) until the leader takes the green at the line, then the player takes over; `AI_LEVELS` sets AI corner skill, braking, passing reach and aggression, while traffic awareness is the same at every level (path scan for the car ahead with a stopping-distance speed limit, `laneClear` sweeping the whole lane change, `passLane` picking the inside or outside, wreck avoidance via `isHazard`, lifting near the wall, catching slides, and a bump-and-run when held up: `bumpT` closes at a tap, and a tapped AI gets loose for `looseT`; lateral margins come from `AI_TOUCH`/`AI_GAP`/`AI_PASS`, lane changes move at `AI_SHIFT` m/s through `relLane`, turn speed is capped at `AI_CORNER` of the real grip limit, and the two files hold their lanes off the green for `startHoldT` via `startLane`; dirt uses wider margins); Time Attack is never stored in `settings`, so a race after a Time Attack session still gets the rolling grid; the in-race HUD shows the running order, and only Time Attack shows the track's top 20 laps and lap-record popups (race laps are still saved to the site board), AI cars come from `AI_FIELD`, built from `ICONIC_FIELD` in `modules-src/shorttrack/iconic-schemes.js`: each AI number runs a look-alike of the iconic NASCAR scheme that number is known for (parody driver names, knockoff logos in `LOGOS`, no real trademarks), named by `lv.scheme` (kept by `cleanLv`) and painted by `drawSchemeSide`/`drawSchemeHood` on the truck decals (`tmSideTex`, `tmHoodTex`) and the stock-car atlas (`carLiveryTex`); numbers are unique, and an AI car whose number a human is running sits out rather than being renumbered; a random subset of the field starts each race and the whole grid, humans included, is shuffled in `hostStart`; AI pace: `aiBaseline()` averages the track's top 20 Time Attack laps (fetched on load, public), each AI gets a lap target `tl` = baseline x `AI_LEVELS[diff].pace` (hard 0.99375, medium 1.01875, easy 1.0375) +/- `vary`, and `aiPace` measures rolling laps at the line and half distance to trim `c.paceK` toward it (above 1 it adds corner speed, power and grip; below 1 it takes mostly power via `aiCornerK`/`aiPaceApply`, so the AI keeps its corner speed); AI turn speed runs at `AI_CORNER` 0.998 of the real grip limit (0.975 on dirt) with a grip edge `gb` of 1.081/1.106/1.131 (easy/medium/hard); with fewer than 3 record laps the AI runs its raw level; pack boost: every car gains top speed per position behind the leader, `NEXT_PACK_BOOST` 5 % on the next model (`CFG.packBoost` 2 % on the classic one), tapering over 2 s after a pass; next-model AI racecraft: 0.35 m more side room in `aiConsts` on asphalt, bump-and-runs only on the straights or past 60 % of a turn (a softer tap mid-turn), a hard lift (`brkTight`) when closing on the car ahead faster than it can lift off, and easing off while its own rear grip is still missing after a hit; car-to-car rebound and contact yaw kick are scaled by `CFG.carBounce` (0.7); on the next vehicle model (the classic model and its golden traces are untouched) cars are `CFG.strength` (1.5) times stronger (damage taken is divided by it), car-to-car hits cost `CFG.contactDrag` (0.4) of the classic contact speed loss, and a hit takes grip away for a moment by how hard it was (`hitGrip` in `physics.ts`, `CFG.contactGrip`), only from the car hit in the tail or a rear quarter (rear grip, `hitRearShare`: full on the tail, less on the rear quarter, none from the middle of the doors forward); the car that does the hitting never loses front or rear grip (`HIT_GRIP_MAX.front` is 0); `contactGripLoss` in `dynamics.ts` fades it out and hands the rear back once the tail steps out, and `holdAfterHit` takes out rotation past `HIT_HOLD_BETA` for 2 s after a hit, so a hit unsettles a car without spinning it; dents are drawn at `damageLookK()` (stock strength 1.5 × `CFG.damageLook` 1.6) times the applied damage, which is already divided by `CFG.strength`, so a weaker car looks more beaten up from the same hits and a stronger one less (impact sparks and smoke scale the same way through `impactLookK()`), with creases and scrapes, and bumpers, smoke and debris follow the visible damage; in the turns the AI lifts instead of braking (`brkAt` 5 m/s over target) unless `brkTight` (a wreck within 35 m or a car it is about to hit), and its turn-entry target rises from the entry speed to the mid-corner speed over the first fifth of the turn, so a driver following closely is not brake-checked; every completed lap by the signed-in driver is sent (every time attack lap, and every race lap until that driver takes the checkered flag), and boards list every lap, so one driver can hold several places. The truck body is the owner's race-truck model (lofted body, greenhouse, roll cage, fascia, bed wing), embedded in the module as `TM` and scaled to the physics size; after the world and each truck are built, `batchStatic` (`modules-src/_shared/static-batch.js`) merges rigid parts per material (a 12-truck race went from about 4,800 to 1,240 draw calls per frame); anything that moves, deforms or is toggled on its own (wheel pivots, the deformable body and decals, sky, clouds) must stay out of it or be passed in `exclude`; liveries are conforming decal meshes on the sides, hood and roof. Race numbers come from `modules-src/shorttrack/shorttrack-numbers.js` (20 Google Fonts digit subsets, 11 styles plus slant, size 60–150% with 125% default, fill/outline/accent colors) and sit on their own decals on the doors, roof and tailgate; `cleanLv` keeps older liveries and older peers working (`italic` becomes `block` plus slant). Drivers pick a vehicle from a dropdown on the track screen or in the garage, stored as `veh` in the livery (`truck` or `car`): the car is a plain super late model loaded from `public/modules/shorttrack-car.bin` (decimated from the owner's model with its original graphics removed), built by `buildStockCar`. Its paint faces carry atlas UVs (side, roof, rear and front regions listed in the file's `regions`), and `carLiveryTex` paints the livery pattern and race numbers into one texture in world metres (`CAR_NUM_SPOTS` sets the door, roof and tail number spots). It uses the truck's scale, physics and dimensions. Admin-made custom cars (`shared/CustomCars.ts`) carry an optional `size` (0.3–1.2, scales the whole car in the race) and `records` (false keeps their laps off every board: the module skips the post and `/api/racing` refuses a `lap` whose `vehicle` is such a car); each custom car collides with its own body footprint (`carFootprint` → `SimCar.fl`/`fw`, used by `collidePair`, `walls`, `aiConsts(w, c)` and `gridGeometry`), so small bodies race close. AI fields use the host's choice; the pace vehicle stays a truck. `src/components/LapRecordBoard.tsx` shows the all-time top 20 per mode (tabs) under each racing module page: Max Secure Circuit and Oval Speedway via `Racing.tsx`, Short Track Showdown under its iframe. Short Track's board uses the `-v2` keys with the classic books under its Classic tab (`archive` prop). `GET /api/racing?records` is public; the module posts `{type:'tl-lap',track}` to the parent after a saved lap so the board refreshes.
- `src/server/routes/shorttrack-cars.ts` + `src/server/shorttrack-cars.ts`: `/api/shorttrack-cars`, admin-made Short Track Showdown bodies. Rows in D1 `maplestreet_assets` with kind `stcar` (spec checked by Zod `carSpecSchema`, types in `modules-src/shorttrack/shared/CustomCars.ts`), models in KV `shorttrack-car/<id>`. GET lists published and retired cars (public; drafts only to the admin with `?drafts=1`); `?model=<id>&v=<version>` serves the .glb with an immutable cache. POST (admin, multipart: `name`, `spec`, optional `model` (.glb), `status` `draft`/`published`, optional `id` to save over a car); PATCH `{id, status?, name?, spec?}` publishes, retires or restores (never back to draft). Every custom car drives on the stock Super Late Model's physics; the spec places the model, sizes the wheels, picks the part that takes the driver's paint (`body`, the default, paints the whole model; `none` from older saves now means `body`), sets `garage` (which Garage options drivers get: `schemes`, `logos`, `numbers`; the main color is always theirs; missing means all; the AI field ignores it and always wears its full iconic scheme, logos and numbers, `liveryGarage` in `render-three/main.js`) and the door, roof and tail number spots and brake lights. `modules-src/shorttrack/shared/carRepaint.ts` puts the full Garage livery on any body: the livery is drawn in the stock car's atlas as two layers (base paint and decals: logos, lettering, painted-on numbers) and every texel of the body is painted from its own 3D point by `modules-src/shorttrack/shared/liveryWrap.ts`: base paint follows the smooth normal with a narrow, side-biased blend so side and top/end art meet along clean lines instead of jagged face edges, decals are projected onto every outer texel facing them so a logo runs over a seam in one piece, and `fitDecal` moves (and if needed shrinks) side logos and door numbers onto painted panel, off wheel openings and windows, using the body's own projection maps (`customGeo`; wheels added as discs). The stock car gets the same treatment (`carLiveryTex` with `stockGeo`/`stockPlan`: seam texels planned once at 1024, upsampled for the crisp 2048 livery). `paintShade` keeps glass and tyres dark and flattens blotchy generated textures; where a model's UVs overlap the outer surface wins; numbers are decals at placed spots and painted on at the stock spots otherwise. This needs nothing from the admin: every body uploaded later is mapped from its own geometry. Windows and the rear spoiler never take paint: `modules-src/shorttrack/shared/carClear.ts` finds them on textured custom bodies (windshield, rear window between the sail panels, spoiler plate above the deck) and `custom-cars.js` draws them as tinted see-through glass and clear Lexan; the stock Super Late Model's glass is see-through and its spoiler is split into its own Lexan material.
- `src/server/routes/shorttrack-paint.ts` + `src/server/shorttrack-paint.ts`: `/api/shorttrack-paint`, the Garage's AI paint designer. POST `{prompt}` (signed in, same-origin, Zod, 2–400 characters, `looksLikeCode` refused) asks Claude Opus (`PAINT_MODEL` `claude-opus-5-5`, fixed, not affected by the studio model overrides) through the forced `paint_scheme` tool for a scheme in the iconic-scheme frame; the system prompt only accepts car paint requests and returns `{refused, message}` for anything else, keeps designs original (parody sponsor names, no real brands or people) and family-friendly, and includes `NO_CODE_RULE`. The answer is cleaned by `sanitizeScheme` (`modules-src/shorttrack/shared/PaintScheme.ts`: shape, text and logo limits, `SCHEME_BRAND_PATTERN`) and returned as `{scheme, message}`. `PAINT_DAILY_LIMIT` (40) designs per member per 24 h counted in D1 `shorttrack_paint_requests` (migration 0025, also created on first use); the admin is unlimited. The page keeps the scheme in the livery as `custom` with pattern `custom` ("AI design" in the Paint scheme list); `cleanLv` re-sanitises it, and `schemeOf` hands it to the same painters as the iconic AI schemes (`drawSchemeSide` also draws `dot` and `text` ops and the scheme's own `logos`), so it shows on the truck, the stock car, every custom car and to other drivers online. The Garage UI is `modules-src/shorttrack/paint-designer.js`. The same designer recreates the AI field's iconic schemes: admin POST `{action:'field', scheme, notes?}` first has Opus research the real car on the web (`RESEARCH_SYSTEM`, `webSearch`; the reference names are in `modules-src/shorttrack/shared/FieldRefs.ts`), then designs it with `fieldRequest` (real layout and colours, parody sponsor from `FIELD_REFS`, no real names painted) and stores the scheme and research notes in D1 `shorttrack_field_schemes` (one row per scheme id, migration 0027, also created on first use); `{action:'field-clear'}` goes back to the hand-made scheme. GET `?field=1` is public (`{designs}`, plus `info` with the notes for the admin); the page loads it into `FIELD_DESIGNS`, and `schemeOf`/`cleanLv` paint an AI car from its design (colours and number colours too) instead of `ICONIC`, so every driver sees the same field. The admin panel is the race car editor's AI field paint tab (`src/components/shorttrack/cars/FieldPaint.tsx`: side-view preview, swatches, notes, Design/Redesign, Design all missing, Use hand-made).
- `src/server/routes/shorttrack-handling.ts`: `/api/shorttrack-handling`, Short Track Showdown handling per track. D1 `shorttrack_handling` (migration 0028, also created on first use): one row per track id with a JSON tune of multipliers on the track's stock `Config` numbers (`modules-src/shorttrack/shared/Handling.ts`: grip (player and AI), apron grip, power, launch, brakes, lift-off slowing, drag, steering lock, steering fall-off, steering response). GET is public (`{tunes, updated, admin}`); POST (admin, same-origin, Zod) `save {track, tune}` publishes and `reset {track}` goes back to stock. The module applies it through `modules-src/shorttrack/track-test.js` at each race start; the admin edits it in the console's Module settings → Short Track Showdown → Handling (`src/components/shorttrack/HandlingSettings.tsx`) or on track with the module's admin-only TRACK TEST panel (`?test=1`), whose Push live publishes the tested settings for the page's track and whose Reset car (or R at any speed) puts the car back on the track. The tune also holds the Contact and damage settings (Vehicle strength, Damage looks, Contact slowdown, Contact grip loss → `strength`, `damageLook`, `contactDrag`, `contactGrip`; `NEXT_CONTACT_KEYS` are left out of the classic geometry comparison). Track Test can bring AI cars along: the panel's AI cars and AI difficulty (and the Track Test setup screen's race fields) pick them, Restart test run starts again, and with any AI cars the test runs as a solo race with a rolling start (`startTestRun` in `render-three/main.js`); with none it is a lone run. No lap driven in Track Test mode is saved to the records.
- Straight Rail Setup Lab track test (`?lab=1`, opened from the Lab's Test on track, see `docs/agents/modules/railsetup.md`): the track is `labTrack` in `next/sim/config.ts` (Thrill Park Speedway's def and setting with the Lab's corner radius `?rad=` ft as `R`, its banking `?bank=` as `turnBank`, straights a third of it unless `?sbank=`, overall length kept, so straights shorten as the turns open; `labParkShift` moves the park scenery and its grass cut-out along z so the lot keeps its room behind the back stretch). The page skips the menus, drives the stock Super Late Model (`veh: 'car'`), applies no live handling tune (Track Test id `labtest`), saves no laps and has no rear-view mirror or pause menu (Escape posts `rail-test-close`). The car runs on `next/sim/railDynamics.ts` whenever `SimCar.rail` is set (`stepCar` hands it over before either vehicle model, so the classic and next models and their golden traces are untouched): every number comes from the Lab's `RailVehicle` (`next/sim/railCar.ts`, Zod-checked from the `rail-setup` message): a sprung body with heave, pitch and front and rear roll joined by the frame twist, corner springs with motion ratios and bump springs from the Lab's tables, end of travel at 3.5 in of bump, wheels that lift, roll centres, jacking, anti-dive and anti-squat (nose lift and squat on throttle, dive on the brakes), front camber and bump steer from each wheel's travel, rear axle steer from roll and heave, a spool with the rollouts' stagger, the Lab's load-sensitive tire (`tireMu`, ×1.12 for an asphalt late model tire) with camber, splitter downforce that grows as the nose drops and stalls when it scrapes, and the frame rails and nose hitting the track (sparks, drag). Banking loads the car through the true surface normal plus the path's vertical curvature, so the banking transitions compress it. 4 substeps per 120 Hz tick; engine 720 N·m at 5,600 rpm with a 7,800 rpm limiter, one ratio picked for the track (`railRatio`). The test driver (`next/sim/railDriver.ts`) follows the groove with pure pursuit, yaw and slip feedback (quick hands: `RailState.direct` skips the keyboard steering ramp), feathers the throttle on wheelspin and oversteer, stops adding lock past the fronts' peak slip, lifts when a push will reach the wall, and learns a speed plan per 2 m (bracketing the fastest clean and slowest failed speed; an incident backs every corner off 4 %); stopped for 2 s it is put back on the groove (`railRecover`, R or Reset car for drivers). `rail-test.ts` draws the page: Test driver / Drive yourself, Reset car, telemetry (Data: speed, g, roll, pitch with nose lift and dive, ride, rpm and ratio, downforce, front-minus-rear slip angle balance, corner loads and travel with the bump spring point, BUMP SPRING / BOTTOMED / LIFTED, frame and nose clearance, scales), laps (time, setup name, driver, minimum clearance, scraping time, mid-turn balance; incidents marked; a note with the test driver's last three clean laps), and replays recorded at 60 Hz for the last 24 laps with play/pause, ¼–2×, scrubbing and a speed / nose-clearance / scraping trace. Cameras: Chase, Trackside, Overhead, In car, Side (pitch), Front (roll), Nose to track, RF corner; Multi-angle adds three tiles (click one to make it the main view), drawn by the presenter's `FrameOptions.views` or the classic renderer's scissor pass. Motion ×3 exaggerates body motion only; the wheels stay planted (`visual` re-parents nothing, it counter-moves the wheel pivots). `window.__stLab` (`state`, `replay(n)`, `ff(seconds)`) is the test hook. `tests/rail.test.ts` (with `tests/railRun.ts`) checks the track, the message schema, deterministic clean laps, nose lift and dive, load transfer and roll with the bar, bottoming on low ride heights, bump springs, banking and stagger.
