# Life Sim (folder modules-src/foxlake) and the Montello builder

Life Sim is Montello, Wisconsin built on Rack & Reel. Its code is in modules-src/foxlake (its old name was Fox Lake) and modules-src/tello. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- Life Sim (`public/modules/foxlake.html`, module id `foxlake`; it replaced Fox Plains 2, and `#module-foxplains2` redirects to it) is Montello, Wisconsin alone: Tello's baked map of the real city and the country three miles round it, as one shared world (the Fox Plains town, the county road, the interstate and the Bear Paw Lake country are gone from it; classic Fox Plains and standalone Rack & Reel are unchanged). It **is** Rack & Reel's `Game` started with `{ terrain: new TelloTerrain(land), extension: FoxLakeTown, site: LakeSite, ridesBought: true, bowfishAnyHour: true, gearAtCabin: true }`, so the hunter and character creator, fishing, hunting, boats, UTV and quad, Wild Man's carp bowfishing (any hour here), wildlife, records and the hunting party are Rack & Reel's own code; never fork them into `modules-src/foxlake/`. Rack & Reel's 1024 m lake country still stands, sealed and switched off (`RegionCuller`), at the scene's origin about 7.8 km from Montello (`MONTELLO_AT` in `region.ts`); the lake gameplay is on Lake Montello: `lake/Rebase.ts` moves every height in the baked map down by the lake's level (`LAKE_LEVEL` 10.7) so the water is at y = 0 like Bear Paw Lake's, and `lake/LakeSite.ts` finds the dock, boat, UTV, start, Wild Man's loops, hunting stands and wild land (`rackreel/world/LakeSite.ts` `LakeSiteConfig`, `World.siteLandmarks`, `BowRoutes.setLakeShape`, `Wildlife.wild`, `TreeStand.standSiteAt`; standalone Rack & Reel passes none of them). The town frame (the shared world's: (map.x, y, -map.z), `TOWN_OFFSET` in `region.ts`) is Montello's map with z turned, so `MontelloPlaces.ts` (generated by `scripts/montello-places.mjs`, which keeps its own copy of the constants; rerun it after re-baking) is in it. The scene is `scene = (town.x - 4200, town.y, -6600 - town.z)`. Montello is built by Tello's `Montello` (zero shift), the houses, cars and neighbors by Fox Plains 2's code moved to `modules-src/foxlake/fp/` (`Houses`, `physics/`); lit and fogged by Rack & Reel's renderer with no shadows for anyone (`FOX_LAKE_SHADOWS` in `look.ts` → `GameOptions.shadows: false`; `town/TownGfx.ts` keeps the caster cells and the sky environment cube for its PBR). The maps are Tello's (`TelloMap`). Your Fox Plains vehicles are the same Rapier cars (`fp/physics/`) driven through Rack & Reel's `VehicleController.addExternal` (`town/Cars.ts`). Life Sim's shared world (`FoxLakeWorld` Durable Object, `WorldServer` option `foxLake`) is Montello's lots only (`MONTELLO_LOTS`: the house parcels and vacant parcels across the map), storage generation `FOX_LAKE_GENERATION` (`fl1:`), no seeded neighbors or traffic; the UTV, the quad and the boat are bought once each at the dealership on Main Street (`Account.rides`, `LAKE_RIDE_ITEMS`, `shopItems`), and `GameHost.setRides`/`setGearRackHere`/`openGearPanel` (with `FoxLakeTown.gear`) are the hooks a store counter uses for the gear rack, wardrobe and catalog. `scripts/foxlake-capture.mjs` takes screenshots, frame times and a leak check (its spots still need updating). Life Sim starts Rack & Reel with `gearAtCabin` (rod and rifle taken and swapped only at a gear rack). Shops, bars, the bank, fuel pumps and paid work are `modules-src/foxlake/places/` (Montello's places, pumps, fire station and houses come from `maplestreet/shared/MontelloPlaces.ts`), with the shared world (`RoomLogic`) as the authority for every purchase, loan, fill-up, delivery and fire call. The casino (`modules-src/foxlake/casino/`: eleven video slots in six researched game styles with original themes (the eleventh, Mo' Dollaz on the back wall, shares its `dollaz` maths with the standalone `modollaz` module), blackjack and a bar; every slot ends a round of 15× the bet or more with the shared Big Win / Huge Win celebration (`modules-src/_shared/win-fx.ts`, 50× and up is Huge) and counts Hold & Spin, Cash Collect, house prizes, free games and jackpots up in big letters mid-screen (`tally`); renderer-free maths in `casino/math/`, simulator `scripts/casino-sim.mjs`, tests in `modules-src/foxlake/tests/casino.test.ts`, `/api/casino`) is mounted as the Foxtails Lounge & Casino on Foxtails Gentlemen's Club's lot (`FoxLakeTown.mountCasino`, placed by `MONTELLO_CASINO` and `casino/CasinoLayout.ts` `SITE`; the club's scanned building is taken down and Tello draws only its pole sign); guests play for fun money. Every Fox Plains and Fox Outfitters counter is a real Montello place in `places/` (Kwik Trip, BP and Mobil gas, Freitag's groceries and freight depot, the bars, the H A Freitag & Son dealership with the UTV, quad and boat, Maple Threads, Montello Body & Wheel, the bank, the outfitter at Rendezvous Paddle & Sports, the sheriff's office, the fire station). Scanned vehicle bodies get clear glass from `fp/physics/ScanGlass.ts` (the shared `maplestreet/shared/WindowDetect.ts` detector with speck dropping and hole filling, or the vehicle's traced `glass`); Life Sim's own vehicles (`vehicles/LifeSimVehicles.ts`: the Kestrel and the Bumblebee bubble helicopter, flown by `fp/physics/Flight.ts`, model from `tools/build-helicopter.py`) are registered only by Life Sim's page and `FoxLakeWorld`; Space jumps on foot (`GameOptions.jump`); the F8 panel's Vehicle tuning edits any vehicle's top speed and handling live and saves it for everyone (`/api/lifesim-tuning`); the F8 developer panel's Trace windows (`dev/WindowTrace.ts`) lets the administrator lasso the glass on the nearest scanned vehicle (Add/Remove, undo, tidy, clear, start from automatic) and save it for everyone. People are tailored (`GameOptions.tailored`): `rackreel/game/Tailor.ts` refits the scanned body once (slim smoothed sleeves, collar drawn in) and paints every outfit from `rackreel/game/Garments.ts` into shared textures (2048 px for the player), with Life Sim-only `tailored` clothes (basketball jerseys and shorts, gym and jean shorts), hairstyles from `rackreel/game/Hair.ts` (`Appearance.style`, `APPEARANCE_VERSION` 3), matte materials, no shadows on people and feet planted on the ground (`Avatar.plantFeet`). Everyone loads on Standard detail (`foxlake/core/Detail.ts`: 1080 lines, 700 m cull, less grass and tree reach, no bloom); the Esc menu's High detail switch changes it live and is kept per device. Details in `modules-src/foxlake/README.md`; design in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-fox-lake-design.md`.
- Truck Simulator in Montello (in progress, `docs/trucksim/MONTELLO.md`): `foxlake/trucks/TruckEdits.ts` builds world edits from Truck Simulator's Montello world and `foxlake/index.ts` `loadMontello` puts them in front of the published World Editor edits (`withTruckEdits`; ids start with `trucks-`, and any published op with that prefix is dropped): Haulers Lane as a graded street north off State Highway 23 at map x 2000 (z 590 → 1450), the sixteen truck lots and the outskirts sites each flattened to their own average height (read from the grids before any edit, so it is the same every load) and cleared of trees, and each site's driveway as a gravel road. The World Editor shows only published edits, so it does not draw these. Tests: `foxlake/tests/truck-edits.test.ts`. With `?trucks` on the page, `FoxLakeTown.ready` creates `foxlake/trucks/LifeSimTrucks.ts`, a `TruckHost` for Truck Simulator's `TruckSession` on the `montello` career: the rig, the Haulers Lane lots (`LotRenderer`, their solids as town statics) and the work-zone equipment are built under the town root; `update` runs its fixed-step loop inside the session's physics callback with the rig's per-step forces on `Physics.onPreStep`, and the rig and trailer join the streaming focus points; the cab is registered with Rack & Reel's `VehicleController.addExternal` through an unmirrored stand-in (like `Cars`), so walking up and pressing E seats the player and E gets them out at the driver's door; driving keys reach the session only while seated, a rig-sized chase camera goes through `GameHost.setCameraShot`, truck prompts show through the new `GameExtension.vehiclePrompt`, and E stays with the truck while one is showing (`GameExtension.claimsInteract`).
- Montello's builder (`modules-src/tello/`, no page of its own: the standalone Tello module was removed and `#module-tello` opens Life Sim) is the code Life Sim builds Montello with, at 1:1 on Life Sim's engine and look (its ground, road and bridge materials are named `tello-…` so `Lighting`'s ground lift reaches them, and they follow the gloss rule), so the renderer, sky, hunter and animations, grass, trees, water shader, building finishes, Rapier world and vehicles are Rack & Reel's and Life Sim's own code; never fork them into `modules-src/tello/`. Rack & Reel's lake country stays sealed in its square at the origin with its scenery switched off; Montello sits a whole map width north (`region.ts`: map, scene and town frames; the map centre must stay a multiple of the map size from the origin because `rrWater` reads Tello's depth map through a repeating texture). Only the City of Montello and three miles round it, plus a mile round White Lake on the east side with White Lake Golf Club (`EXPANSIONS` in the bake), are built: the bake reads the city limits (OpenStreetMap relation, admin level 8) and ships them with the playable area (the limits widened by `PLAY_PAD`, 4,828 m, in a 16,384 m frame) as `limits` in the JSON and a signed-distance grid `play`; buildings outside the ring are dropped, ground, water, roads and woods are drawn only 240 m past the playable edge (`TelloLand.seen`), invisible walls follow its edge (open at County Highway F in Life Sim) and roads that leave it end at barricades. Every roof takes its colour from the NAIP aerial photo (`rc`). Downtown's storefronts, lamp posts and the water tower are `world/Storefronts.ts`, `world/StreetDressing.ts` and `data/Downtown.ts`; `data/RoadFixes.ts` rounds road corners, bends streets and highways clear of buildings and finds where guardrails stand (`world/Guardrails.ts`, W-beam, solid), `world/JunctionShapes.ts` fills junction notches, `world/StreetPaint.ts` paints highway edge and centre lines (residential streets stay unmarked), `data/LevelStreets.ts` levels every street that has a sidewalk from side to side at load (ground under the street, curbs and sidewalks set to the street's centre-line height, fading out past the sidewalk), `data/RiverWall.ts` + `world/RetainingWall.ts` give Main Street its concrete retaining wall where the river comes within ten feet of it, or within 14 m where the bank already falls 0.3 m within 5 m of the street's edge (so the road never drops off toward the water; the wall runs from about z -314 to -166) (the whole run on one straight grade between the street's heights at its two ends, low spots built up and bumps cut, so street and guardrail are dead level end to end; the far sidewalk is set to that grade and eased back over 4 m, banks just past the face are capped at the wall's top, and footbridges that land on the wall take its height; street levelled to the wall's face, the strip a deck for `heightAt`, drawn ground sunk under it, bank dug down, guardrail always on top except where a footbridge lands), `data/Streets.ts` corrects the baked map at load (West Montello, East Montello and Main Street one lane each way with parking on both sides, Main Street's island split at the light, the West Montello Street bridge level with the street over the dug-out river, missing and low buildings), with raised sidewalks and curbs (ten feet on Main and East Montello, five on West Montello) and the paint in `world/StreetPaint.ts`, the municipal building and fire station in `world/Landmarks.ts`, the light, island and signs in `world/Intersection.ts`, the falls and the granite park's quarry faces in `world/Falls.ts` and the mural and realty marquee in `world/Landmarks.ts`; storefront details (cornices, brackets, hoods, window frames, sills, pilasters, doors, turret roofs) and the falls, quarry faces and small falls are Blender models from `modules-src/tello/tools/build-montello.py` (`assets-src/models/tello/`, then `pnpm assets` and commit `public/models/tello/`), laid into the merged builders by `world/Kit.ts` with the older shapes as the fallback; only the owner's own photos are used as reference, never Google Street View (or photos of it). The map is baked from OpenStreetMap, USGS 3DEP lidar, the 2018 lidar point cloud (each building's roof type, eave and ridge, bridge decks, and every tree with its height), NAIP imagery and Microsoft building footprints by `modules-src/tello/tools/bake_map.py` into `public/modules/tello/montello.bin` and `montello.json`, which are committed; never hand-edit them, and keep the credits on the start card. `TelloLand` (`region.ts`) is renderer-free and is what the tests read (`modules-src/tello/tests/`, `scripts/tello-tests.mjs` in `pnpm test`). White Lake Golf Club is laid out hole by hole by `tools/bake_golf.py` (`golf` in the JSON: number, par, length, line, tee, tee box, green, pin, fairways, plus bunkers) and drawn by `world/GolfCourse.ts` (striped fairways, greens with fringe and flagsticks, tee markers, hole signs, ball washers, benches, bunkers; Blender parts in the fields kit), ready for a later playable course. Driveways the aerial photo shows are read off the one-metre NAIP tiles by `tools/bake_driveways.py` for every house and garage and baked as roads of class `driveway` (`DRIVEWAY_CLASS`; kind 3 gravel or 2 paved) that stop at the road's edge. Load-time clearance (`world/Clearance.ts`: buildings a street or lane runs through are left out everywhere, walks stop at curbs and walls) and the fuel stations' paved forecourts (`data/Forecourts.ts`) keep roads clean. Details in `modules-src/tello/README.md`. Details on the streets: the Blender kits `house-kit.glb` (laid on every house by `world/HouseDetails.ts`), `places-kit.glb` and `fields-kit.glb` (`tools/build-town-kit.py`) dress houses, the fuel stations (Kwik Trip, BP and Mobil, with pump islands and lit canopies), Freitag's, the bars, Foxtails (exterior only: `shellSkips` in `world/Buildings.ts` leaves its shell out for an enterable interior), the sheriff's office, the freight depot, the school's lots, buses and entrance, and the baseball and softball fields (`world/TownDetail.ts`, `Businesses.ts`, `School.ts`, `BallFields.ts`, `Lots.ts`); signs are plain lettering on dark boards, never logos or brand colours.
- `src/server/routes/lifesim-tuning.ts`: `/api/lifesim-tuning`, Life Sim's vehicle tuning from the developer panel (top speed, power, grip, side grip, steering, brakes; a helicopter's climb, turn rate and agility; `modules-src/foxlake/vehicles/Tuning.ts`). Public GET; admin same-origin POST checked with Zod, stored in D1 `library_settings` key `lifesim-vehicle-tuning`. Life Sim applies it at boot; Fox Plains never reads it.
- `src/server/routes/world-edits.ts` + `src/server/world-edits.ts`: `/api/world-edits`, storage for Life Sim's World Editor (admin). The document is `WorldEdits` in `modules-src/tello/edits/WorldEdits.ts`, checked by Zod `worldEditsSchema` within `EDIT_LIMITS`; only world `lifesim` exists. D1 `world_edit_revisions` (migration 0038, also created on first use: `id` `wr_<10 hex>`, author username, note, op count, bytes, `kept`) and `world_edit_state` (one row per world: `draft_id`, `published_id`, `published_at`); each revision's JSON is in KV `world-edits/<world>/<revisionId>`. GET (public, cached 10 s) returns `{edits, rev, publishedAt}` for the published revision (null edits when nothing is published); `?draft=1` (admin) returns `{edits, draftRev, publishedRev, publishedAt, updatedAt, revisions}` (the draft, else the published edits, and the latest 60 revisions). POST (admin, same-origin, Zod): `save` (overwrites the draft while it is neither kept nor published, otherwise starts a new revision), `snapshot` (keeps the draft so the next save starts a new one), `publish`, `restore` (copies a revision into a new draft), `unpublish` and `delete` (never the published or current draft revision); each answers with the draft shape. Browser calls are in `src/lib/world-edits-client.ts`.
- Life Sim World Editor (`#admin/world-editor`, `src/components/world/WorldEditor.tsx`, lazy-loaded, opens filling the window): loads the same `montello.json`/`.bin` Life Sim does, keeps the street-fixed map untouched (`model.ts`, `EditorModel`) and replays the edit list on a working copy (`EditSession` from `modules-src/tello/edits/Apply.ts`; new edits apply on top, undo/redo and changes to an older edit replay the whole list). Views: a 2D map (`MapView.tsx`: shaded tiles from the grids in `tiles.ts` with a Map or Heights-and-contours mode, roads, lots, buildings, trees and extras drawn as vectors in `draw.ts`, picking in `hit.ts`), a 3D world (`WorldPreview3D.tsx` with `preview3d/build.ts` for the scene and `preview3d/draft.ts` for the live preview of what is being drawn; it follows the camera and every tool works on the 3D ground) or both side by side. Tools (`settings.ts`, `tools.ts` `ToolRunner`, options in `ToolPanel.tsx`): raise, lower, flatten, smooth, roughen and grade the land (brush, outline or rectangle of any size), fill low ground, set a water body's level, paint or drain water, rivers, the road builder (plotted centre points rounded into arcs or a spline, width, class, name, sidewalks, grading, snapping onto roads), redraw and remove roads, add and remove buildings, draw, reshape, remove and trim parking lots, plant and clear trees, guardrails, streetlights (single or in a row) and walls. `Inspector.tsx` changes the selected road, building, lot or edit; `EditList.tsx` lists edits with switch-off, reorder and delete; `Revisions.tsx` keeps, restores and deletes versions and unpublishes. Heights shown and typed are relative to Lake Montello's surface (`toLake`/`fromLake`). The draft saves itself; Publish makes it the world Life Sim loads; Open in Life Sim opens `/modules/foxlake.html?solo=1&edits=draft&at=x,z`.
