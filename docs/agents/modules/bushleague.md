# Bush League Baseball

Baseball module, ranked ladder and online matches. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- Bush League Baseball (`modules-src/bushleague/`, module id `bushleague`) is built on `engine/` and planned in seven phases in `docs/bushleague/PLAN.md` (Phases 1–5 are in: rules core, graphics, rigged players, broadcast presentation and online play; manual fielding (4b), career mode and the other parks come next). `sim/` is the deterministic rules and physics core: no DOM, no `Math.random` (seeded `Rng` streams, one for AI choices and one for outcomes), no clock, so a seed plus the recorded intents (`Contest.log`, `replay`) reproduces a contest exactly; keep it that way, because the Durable Object runs the same code. Online play: `/api/bushleague` (`src/server/routes/bushleague.ts`) is the public ranked ladder on GET and, on a same-origin WebSocket upgrade with `mode=queue|create|join`, the entry to the Durable Object `BushLeagueMatch` (`src/server/bushleague-match.ts`, binding `BUSHLEAGUE_MATCH`; instance `queue` pairs signed-in members for ranked three-inning matchups, `room:<code>` instances seat two players, guests welcome in private rooms). The room runs `MatchCore` from `modules-src/bushleague/shared/Online.ts` as the authority and relays each resolved intent with a state digest; both pages apply the same records (`systems/Online.ts`, `Director.startOnline`). Only the owner of a decision may send it, early swings are held until the pitch could have reached the plate on screen, silent owners get safe defaults at deadlines, a dropped seat is held 90 s and then forfeits. Results and Elo go to D1 `bushleague_matches` and `bushleague_ratings` (`src/server/bushleague.ts`, migration 0022). Keep protocol and rules changes in `shared/Online.ts` and `sim/`, never in the page. The module answers the chat's `tl-host` with a private room code and joins from `?room=`. A pitch is `prePitch` → `preparePitch` → `resolveSwing`, each taking an intent or `null` for the AI; human intents are integers (millimetres, milliseconds). Looks (time-of-day presets, per-tier budgets, park themes) live in `looks.ts`; the module uses the engine's Neutral tone mapping (`?tone=agx|aces` to compare) and a clean post chain (`engine/render/Post.ts`: a light bloom over a multisampled scene pass; no AO, TAA, depth of field, vignette, grain or lens flare, and adaptive resolution never drops below 1×, so the picture stays sharp and clear), every park is dressed in the home club's colours (seats, wall padding, fence cap, distance signs, scoreboard lettering; `view/teamLook.ts`), and The Outhouse and Sandlot Classic have their signature dressing in `view/Signature.ts`. Players are Blender-built skinned bodies with fingered hands and spiked cleats, a Blender glove and bat (`tools/build-gear.py` writes `glove.glb` and `bat.glb`) and a seeded face sheet per player (`modules-src/bushleague/tools/build-ballplayer.py` writes `public/modules/bushleague/players/ballplayer-{lean,stocky,belly}.glb`; rerun it in Blender after changing the body and commit the outputs; `players/Models.ts` loads them, with the procedural body as the fallback) on one shared 21-bone skeleton (`players/`: `Rig.ts` bone names, five body types, `Uniform.ts` slot colors so any body wears any club, `Clips.ts` + `Animator.ts` for blending and glove IK); generated bodies come from the admin panel's Bush League tab (Meshy prompts in `assets-src/models/bushleague/prompts.json`) through `scripts/blender/bushleague/cleanup.py`, which renames rigs to those bone names, writes `_slot` and exports 25k/8k/2k LODs. Difficulty is part of the contest setup (`aiSkill`, `pciScale` per side, 0 and 1 being the calibrated league), so it stays deterministic. Sound is synthesised (`systems/Audio.ts`) and voice lines use the browser's speech engine with subtitles; never add audio files with real broadcast calls or music. League-wide numbers live in `sim/tuning.ts` and the 10,000-contest test (`tests/league.test.ts`, worker threads) pins batting average .240–.260, 4.0–5.0 runs per nine and HR/FB 10–14 %; rerun `node scripts/bushleague-tests.mjs` after touching contact, flight, fielding or tuning. The 32 clubs (two leagues, four divisions), ten park layouts and the name tables are original and PG-13; keep real teams, players, logos, uniforms, parks and broadcast branding out. Visible copy uses matchup, contest, season, first pitch, final and box score (never the words game, gaming or ballgame).
- Bush League Baseball's `modules-src/bushleague/sim/` is the single authority for its rules: change rules there, never in `systems/` or `ui/`, and keep the replay test green (`tests/rules.test.ts`, determinism cases).
