# Floe Fling

Floe Fling (`floefling`, `modules-src/floefling/`) is an arcade distance launcher on Babylon.js 9.34.0 (WebGPU, WebGL2 fallback or with `?renderer=webgl`): Bruno the polar bear tosses a fish from an ice hole on a glacier and swats it with a wooden club across an endless ice plain. Five fish a round; the longest is the score. All meshes are procedural (`MeshBuilder` + `StandardMaterial`), no model files. The HUD is DOM (`index.html`, `styles.css`), not Babylon GUI, so it keeps the site's keyboard and screen-reader support. Visible copy never says game; the name and the bear are original, not taken from any commercial title.

- Rules live in the renderer-free `sim/` (`tests/sim.test.ts`, run by `scripts/floefling-tests.mjs` from `pnpm test`; `modules-src/floefling/tsconfig.json` is type-checked there too). Units are metres, x downrange from the bear, y up; the glacier top is `CLIFF_HEIGHT` (9 m) up to `CLIFF_EDGE`.
  - `sim/swing.ts`: the toss is a floaty arc (`TOSS_SPEED`, `TOSS_GRAVITY`); a swing connects `SWING_DELAY` after the press, and the fish's height then against the strike zone (`ZONE_Y` ± `ZONE_HALF`) gives `quality` −1..1. Within `SWEET_SHARE` it is a home run at 35–45° and full speed (`SWEET_BONUS`, hit-stop, shake, starburst); above (early) a high pop fly, below (late) a low grounder; outside the zone, or once the fish is near the ice, a whiff.
  - `sim/flight.ts`: fixed 1/120 s steps with quadratic drag against the wind; landings steeper than `NOSEDIVE_ANGLE` at speed embed the fish (nose-dive), others bounce with snow or ice restitution and then slide with snow or ice friction (`SNOW_FRICTION`, `ICE_FRICTION`).
  - `sim/track.ts`: `Track(seed)` generates 80 m chunks from `TRACK_START` (30 m) on: slick ice patches, bouncers (seal, walrus, snowman: relaunch and add to the combo once each), open ice holes and snowbanks (instant stops). Same seed, same track and same result.
- `game.ts` runs the phases (ready → toss → swing → flight → result → end), input (Space, Enter, click or tap; Space during a flight fast-forwards ×3; M sound, T colours, R new round), the spring camera, scoring and the local top five (`floefling-scores` in localStorage, a convenience only). Distance is shown in metres and feet; the leaderboard (`floefling` in `src/lib/module-scores.ts`, unit `feet`) stores tenths of a foot through `tlScores.progress`/`submit`. `window.__floe` exposes `phase`, `shots`, `press` and `fish` for headless checks.
- `render/world.ts` streams 100 m terrain chunks around the camera (drifts off the lane, flat lane with 10 m ticks, 50 m signs in m and ft, lakes, rocks, track elements) and disposes old ones; `render/bear.ts` (poses idle, toss, swing, whiff, cheer, slump; the swing reaches the contact angle exactly at `SWING_DELAY`), `render/fish.ts`, `render/fx.ts` (instanced snow puffs, stars, splash, ring), `audio.ts` (all sounds synthesised with Web Audio).
- Two colour sets, Original and WICS (`looks.ts`), defaulting to WICS when the site theme is WICS. Snow, wood and fur have no specular (gloss rule); ice and water get a little.
