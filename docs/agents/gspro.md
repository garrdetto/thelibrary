# GSPro Analyzer

The /gspro page, its bridge and course rounds. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- `src/server/routes/gspro.ts` + `src/server/gspro.ts`: `/api/gspro`, the GSPro Analyzer (admin only, its own page at `/gspro` from `src/routes/gspro.tsx`, linked from the admin console; `/admin/gspro` redirects there). D1 `gspro_sessions`, `gspro_shots` (normalized to mph, yards, degrees, rpm; positive angles point right) and `gspro_bridge` (one hashed `gsb_` key per admin) — migration 0041, also created on first use. `/api/gspro/ingest` (POST, `Authorization: Bearer gsb_…`, server-to-server) takes Open Connect v1 shot JSON from the local bridge `public/gspro/gspro-bridge.mjs` (plain Node, served for download): it listens on 127.0.0.1:9211, forwards every byte to GSPro's Open Connect port 921 unchanged, tags each shot with the club from GSPro's replies, keeps a CSV copy and an offline queue in `~/.gspro-bridge`, and sends heartbeats. Shots are grouped into bridge sessions split by 30-minute gaps. Only the clubs in `recordedClubCodes` (`src/lib/gspro/clubs.ts`: DR, 3W, 5W, 3–9i, PW, AW, 56° (SW), 60° (LW)) are recorded from the bridge and CSV imports; untagged shots are kept so they can be labelled. The replay dialog shows the view on the left and the shot list on the right (`ShotList` in `ShotReplay.tsx`). Admin GET `?live&since=`, `?sessions`, `?shots`; POST actions `create-key`, `revoke-key`, `new-session`, `import` (CSV parsed in the page by `src/lib/gspro/csv.ts`), `update-shot`, `delete-shots` (the trash button in shot tables and the replay), `rename-session`, `delete-session`, `coach` (Gemini tips from per-club averages). Analysis is browser-side in `src/lib/gspro/`: `flight.ts` estimates carry, offline, apex and landing angle when the monitor sends no carry (fitted to tour averages, ±5%), `stats.ts` per-club summaries, `tips.ts` rule-based tips, `library.ts` the tip hub guides. UI in `src/components/gspro/`; clicking any shot (shot tables, dispersion dots, the Live card) opens `ShotReplay.tsx`, which recreates it from `traceFlight` (airborne samples plus a hop and roll) as an animated down-the-line view with side and overhead plots and the same club's other shots in grey. Course rounds (bridge 1.1): the bridge also watches GSPro's plain-JSON round file (`%USERPROFILE%/AppData/LocalLow/GSPro/GSPro/currentRound.dat`; `GSPro.db` rounds are encrypted and not read) and uploads each shot (`courseShotsFromRound`: player, course, hole, par, lie codes 2 fairway/tee, 1 rough, 5 green, 11 sand, `ClubIndex` mapped by `clubFromGsproIndex`, GSPro's airborne `pos` samples and post-touchdown `path`, world coordinates in yards) to D1 `gspro_rounds` and `gspro_course_shots` (migration 0042, also created on first use; one bad shot is skipped, not the batch). `HoleResult` 7 marks the holed final shot, whose end point is the cup (`holePins`); `roundReport` in `src/lib/gspro/course.ts` builds the scorecard, fairways, greens in regulation, putts, up and downs, approach proximity by distance and lie, and putting by first-putt length for the On course tab (`CourseView.tsx`), whose shots replay GSPro's own flight. The bridge also reads ProTee Labs' club lofts (`%APPDATA%/ProTeeUnited/Configs/Bags`, `parseBag`) into `gspro_settings` for loft-delivery tips. ProTee Labs connects straight to GSPro's port 921 with no port setting, so with ProTee the live relay stays idle. The replay's main view is a three.js driving range (`src/components/gspro/range/`: `RangeScene.ts` builds the range, ball, tracer (`Line2`), landing marks and the Broadcast/Tee/Chase cameras; `golfer.ts` is a stylised golfer whose arms and club swing about the swing-plane axis with impact at clock 0; `timing.ts` holds the swing lead so `ShotReplay.tsx` can stay free of three.js; `riggedGolfer.ts` loads the Meshy golfer (`assets-src/models/gspro/golfer.glb`, rigged, bone names kept by the `character` asset preset) and poses it every frame on its own bones (knee flex, waist bend, chest turn about the spine, two-bone arm IK onto the grip) holding a Meshy club head (`club-driver` for the driver, `club-wood` for both woods, `club-iron` for every iron and wedge; fitted by `scripts/blender/gspro/fit-golf.py`) on a code-built shaft and grip, with `golfer.ts` as the fallback until it loads; `scenery.ts` adds the rough, the row of hitting bays, catch nets, bunkers, broadleaf and pine tree lines, the clubhouse, hills and clouds; `RangeView.tsx` is lazy-loaded and falls back to the 2D down-the-line canvas without WebGL). Shot tables (Live, Analyze, On course) sort by any column with missing values last, can group by club in bag order and filter to one club (`src/lib/gspro/sorting.ts`, `SortBar.tsx`, `CourseShotTable.tsx`; the choice is remembered per table in localStorage). Bridge 1.2 `--install-startup` writes `GSPro Bridge.cmd` to the Windows Startup folder so it starts minimised at sign-in (`--uninstall-startup` removes it). Tests: `scripts/gspro/gspro.test.ts` and `scripts/gspro/course.test.ts` (fixture: three anonymised holes of a real round).
