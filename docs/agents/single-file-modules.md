# Single-file learning modules

The small modules in public/modules/, Spin Rally, Skill Party and Song Maker. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- No scrollbars inside a module: `public/modules/module-fit.js` fits each page or menu screen to the frame (see `docs/ENGINE-MAP.md`); new modules load it, and only developer and settings popups may scroll. Sandlot Sluggers 3D's draft is two pages (roster, then ballpark and options).
- Newer learning modules: single-file pages in `public/modules/` sharing `module-base.css` — Alphabet Sprint (`alphabet`, fastest a–z; a wrong letter must be backspaced), Words Per Minute (`wpm`), Tetra Drop (`tetradrop`), Bubble Shooter (`bubbles`), Memory Match (`memory`), Echo Sequence (`simon`), Sudoku (`sudoku`, only Hard no-hint solves are ranked), Minesweeper (`minesweeper`, only Intermediate clears are ranked), Hero Recall (`recall`, twenty shuffled cards of ten original characters shown for five seconds, then matched against the clock; board is time in ms, `order: 'asc'`, with the ten best times also kept on the device), Greible (`greible`, find a hidden 6, 7 or 8-letter word from colour clues with one more guess than letters; answers in `public/modules/greible/answers-<n>.txt` (common base-form words), accepted guesses in `words-<n>.txt`; the clock starts on the first letter; three boards `greible-6/7/8` grouped under `greible`, times in ms shown to two decimals (`decimals: 2`) with the solved word beside each time (`detail: 'Word'`; the page sends `tlScores.submit(module, ms, { detail: word })`, stored in D1 `module_score_details`, migration 0036, also created on first use, and shown as an extra column by `ModuleScoreboard`)), Math Rush (`mathrush`), Blockhead Siege (`blockhead`, top-down wave survival, solo or two on one keyboard), Food Chain (`foodchain`, eat smaller fish to grow), Hold the Keep (`keep`, grab-and-fling wall defense with a between-wave shop), Giant's Hand (`giant`, ninety-second toss round with a juggle multiplier), Polar Swat (`gonefishing`, a polar bear swats fish out of an ice hole; five fish a round and the longest distance is the score, stored in tenths of a foot with board unit `feet`; Original and WICS colour sets, defaulting to WICS when the site theme is WICS; its art is rendered in Blender by `scripts/gonefishing-art.py` (run with `pip install bpy`, then `python scripts/gonefishing-art.py -- <out-dir> [name-prefixes]`, convert the PNGs to WebP into `public/modules/gonefishing/`), and the sprite pixel origins in the page's `ART` table must match the script's cameras), Cookie Factory (`cookies`, five-minute timed clicker; total baked is the score) and Corner Cart (`hotdog`, thirty-day cart business; final cash is the score, board unit `dollars`). All submit to the module leaderboards; board settings (including `order: 'asc'` time boards stored in ms) are in `src/lib/module-scores.ts`. Spin Rally (`pingpong`, `modules-src/pingpong/`) is table tennis with spin physics, a stroke set (loop, chop, smash, drop, sidespin aim, a charged Comet, dash and stretch saves), a CPU at three levels, two players on one keyboard and host-authoritative PeerJS rooms `tl-pingpong-<code>`; its board is the longest rally against the CPU (unit `hits`); visible copy says set and match, never game. Skill Party (`party`, `modules-src/party/`) is an original party arena for up to four players: character lobby, players take turns picking themed mini-games, everyone plays the same seeded challenge (hot-seat on one screen, simultaneously online through PeerJS rooms `tl-party-<code>`), and a running scoreboard; it has no leaderboard. Song Maker (`songmaker`, `public/modules/songmaker.html` + `/api/songmaker` in `src/server/routes/songmaker.ts`) turns a signed-in user's lyrics into a sung song with Google Lyria (`lyria-3.5`, or `lyria-3-clip-preview` for 30 s clips) through the Gemini Interactions REST API with the same `GEMINI_API_KEY`; songs are D1 `songmaker_songs` rows (migration 0019) with audio in KV `song/<userId>/<id>`, five per user per 24 hours (admin unlimited).
