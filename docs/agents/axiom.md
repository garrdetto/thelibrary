# Axiom Radar, paper-trading bot and Solana bot

Owner-only trading research tools. Moved out of AGENTS.md so it loads only when this area is worked on; keep it current here, not in AGENTS.md.

- Access: the owner, plus any account the owner grants `axiom-radar` in People & permissions. Every browser-facing Axiom endpoint (data, stream, settings, bot) answers 403 to anyone else; settings, pins and bot commands are owner only. The page is `src/routes/axiom-radar.tsx` (`/axiom-radar`, gated by `radar` from `/api/workspace-access`), and the Radar link at the left of the site ribbon and the admin console entry only show for viewers who have access.
- `src/components/axiom-radar/`: the radar UI (`AxiomRadar.tsx` with the live top-3 viewer gainers, alerts and tabs; `PicksView`, `RadarTable`, `MoversView`, `WalletsView`, `SettingsView`, `TokenDrawer`, `useRadarFeed.ts`, `axiom-radar.css`; `CandleChart.tsx` draws GeckoTerminal candles (1m to 1D, market cap or price) in the drawer; `AxiomLink.tsx` is the Axiom logo link shown beside every coin name; `LearnedViews.tsx`, `LearnedCoin.tsx`, `MakerView.tsx` and `ResearchView.tsx` render the Could explode, Watch List, Millionaire Maker and Research Info tabs from the tracker's research). It is a standalone page at `src/routes/axiom-radar.tsx` (`/axiom-radar`; `/admin/axiom-radar` redirects there) with its own header and no site navigation; the `axiom-radar` entry in the admin console (Site → Tools) navigates there. `PriceSpark.tsx` draws the chart previews and the 30s/1m/5m/10m switch; `theme.ts` applies the radar's own colour themes (presets and custom colours, stored in the radar settings) as `--rt-*` variables on `.radar-page`, which `axiom-radar.css` maps onto the site tokens. `src/lib/axiom-radar-types.ts`, `src/lib/axiom-radar-learner.ts` and `src/lib/axiom-radar-settings.ts` hold the shared types and the settings shape/defaults. Millionaire Maker filters are view-only: they never change what the tracker studies.
- `src/server/routes/axiom-radar.ts` + `src/server/axiom-radar-store.ts`: POST `/api/admin/axiom/ingest` (server-to-server from the tracker, `Authorization: Bearer AXIOM_RADAR_TOKEN` Worker secret, skips the same-origin check) stores the latest payload, per-coin details and the per-second `live` figures (viewers, price) in D1 `axiom_radar_state` (created on first use; the stream relays `live` as SSE `live` events between full payloads) and replies whether an admin is watching plus the saved settings; GET `/api/admin/axiom/data` (`?mint=` for details) and `/api/admin/axiom/stream` (SSE) need Radar access; GET/PUT `/api/admin/axiom/settings` (Radar access to read, owner to save; same-origin, Zod) reads and saves the radar settings (key `settings`).
- `scripts/axiom-tracker/`: Node worker run by PM2 on the owner's machine. It runs in Axiom-only mode by default (`sources.ts`, owner's choice): it reads only Axiom (the bridge extension and axiom.trade) and talks only to this site; DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, Jupiter, Solana RPC and Helius are switched off at `fetchJson`/`solanaRpc`, the loops that only read them (wallet-tracker, wallet-pnl, flip-traders, follow-up, holder-refresh, jupiter, fresh-wallets, dex-paid, chart-history) report `disabled`, and coins come only from Pulse cards (`pairFromPulse`). `AXIOM_SOURCES=all` turns everything back on; nothing was removed. The radar's `score` is the Axiom Score (`axiom-score.ts`, owner: "scores should be based off of axiom only and be scaled out of 100"): every measure from the Pulse feed (market-cap change over 30 s/1 min/5 min, a calm measure that drops to 0 for a 30-second jump past 10% then 30%, viewers and their change, holders and trades per minute, buy share over the last minute, buy-volume share, volume to market cap, top-10/dev/sniper/insider/bundler/fresh-wallet shares, pro traders, KOLs, dev launches and migrations, socials, DEX paid) becomes 0-100 by its rank among the coins seen in the last 3 hours (lower-is-better ones flipped; shares already 0-100 used as they are), then they are averaged with the weights in `axiom-score-weights.json`, which the strategy lab re-tunes. Each token carries `axiomScore` (parts, ranks, weights) and the old `runnerScore`; every coin's measures and ranks are saved every 5 s in `axiom_score_readings` (kept 24 h) for the lab; the payload carries Axiom's own SOL price as `solUsd`. Everything runs every second (owner: "everything about the tracker needs to be in realtime"): discovery, snapshots, scoring, live prices, bursts, learner and advice cycles, a `coin_readings` row per tracked coin under a day old (kept 24 h), and the site publisher sends the full payload every second while someone watches (every 5 s otherwise; the bot no longer depends on it). `index.ts` runs discovery (DexScreener, GeckoTerminal, Pulse coins; requirements from the saved settings), `live-market.ts` (1-second prices and in-memory ticks), the collector, the Helius wallet tracker, scoring/paper trades/good-coin marking, `site-publisher.ts` (pushes to the ingest route and applies returned settings) and `extension-intake.ts` on `127.0.0.1:8791` (bridge reports from `extensions/axiom-radar-bridge/`, an unpacked Chrome extension reading Pulse cards the owner has open (since 1.18 an orphaned Pulse tab, cut off when the extension restarts, reloads itself at most once a minute), plus the origin-locked 1-second `/live/*` feed the page reads when opened on that PC). `pulse-cards.ts` parses Pulse cards and builds the quality checklist; `radar-query.ts` builds the payload. Research runs continuously in `learner.ts` (coin lifecycles in `coin_lifecycles`, runner model, time before a crash), `bursts.ts` (viewer and buy jumps in `radar_bursts`, who held the coin then) and `winners.ts` (coins that grew 3x+, their shared traits and wallets, kept permanently in `winner_wallets`), `steady-climbers.ts` (coins that rose for an hour or more, up in most 5-minute steps and never 40% off their high, with $5K+ average 5-minute volume and $30K+ liquidity, to a $200K+ peak (`climberRules`): each is saved permanently in `steady_climbers` with its climb curve, its numbers at the start, 15, 30, 60 and 120 minutes in and at the peak, and the wallets that bought before the peak (from Axiom trades, or its archived full record once the raw rows are gone); saved climbers count as positive winners for the Millionaire Maker match, keep their full archive record, survive a fresh start, and every live coin gets `maker.climb`, `maker.climberLike` and `maker.climberWallets`, shown by the Maker's two climber filters and its Steady climbers saved list (`ClimbersSection.tsx`)), sharing `logistic.ts`; results reach the payload through `learner-state.ts`. `advice.ts` re-tests a fixed list of warning signs against the next 5 minutes of history every 15 minutes and publishes per-coin `advice` (exit: hold/tighten/exit, rebuy: ok/wait/never) that trading bots read; only signs the data supports are used. `follow-up.ts` keeps reading prices for coins after they leave the radar so exits and rebuys can be scored. The bot contract is in `AXIOM_RADAR.md`. Research rows are never pruned. `radar-chrome.ps1` (`pnpm axiom:chrome`) opens a dedicated unthrottled Chrome window for the bridge, which also fills in viewers for coins not on Pulse by visiting their Axiom pages in one background tab. `AXIOM_RADAR.md`, `ecosystem.axiom.config.js` and `.env.example` cover setup and PM2.
- Axiom Radar exception: the self-hosted tracker keeps its working data in SQLite at `data/axiom-tracker.db` (git-ignored) on the owner's machine and pushes snapshots to D1 `axiom_radar_state`, and the paper-trading bot keeps its pretend account in SQLite at `data/axiom-paper.db` beside it. Do not move other features onto it.
- `scripts/axiom-paper-bot/` (Python 3.11+, standard library only; see its `README.md`): trades the Radar with pretend money on the owner's machine and never places a real order. It runs in Axiom-only mode by default (`axiom_paper/sources.py`, `Engine(axiom_only=True)` from `cli.py`): no DexScreener quotes or SOL price and no live Jito/Solana fee levels; it always trades the feed alone (`rules.FEED_ONLY`), takes the SOL price from the tracker's feed (else the last saved one, else `AXIOM_PAPER_SOL_USD` or 69.4) and fees from the priority fee and bribe dials. `AXIOM_SOURCES=all` turns them back on. The bot reads the Radar straight from the tracker's `127.0.0.1:8791/live/data` every second (`site.LocalFeedSite`, with the site's origin as `Origin`) and syncs settings, commands and its report with the site every 5 s or whenever it has something to send; it falls back to the site's copy if the tracker does not answer, and `--site-feed` restores the old path. `run.py` starts `axiom_paper/cli.py`; `engine.py` is the loop (pull, mark prices, sell, buy, report), `strategy.py` the buy, bet-size and sell rules (including the Radar's per-coin `advice`: sell on `exit`, a tighter trailing stop on `tighten` or when a held coin leaves the Radar, no buys on `never`/`wait`; never hardcode which warning signs matter), `broker.py` the simulated fills (fees, constant-product price impact, slippage), `ledger.py` the account in SQLite `data/axiom-paper.db`, `fees.py` the network fee per trade (auto fee from live Jito tip and Solana priority-fee levels capped by a max fee, or the owner's own priority fee and bribe), `money.py` the amount format, `prices.py` DexScreener prices for held coins, `dashboard.py` the terminal view. Every action is a `run.py` subcommand (`status`, `settings`, `set <dial> <value>`, `pause`, `resume`, `flatten`, `reset`, `logs`); `flatten` and `reset` reach a running bot through `data/paper-bot.inbox.json` (`Engine.check_inbox`), and read-only commands open the account with `Engine(..., observer=True)` so they never touch the dial bookkeeping. `console.py` + `console.html` are the prompt console (`run.py console`, `127.0.0.1:8793`): a request in plain words goes to Claude through the local Claude Code command line with every tool off (`claude -p --tools "" --json-schema`), which returns command lines; `build_step` turns each into a checked `bot`/`pm2` argv (no shell) or a PowerShell line that always needs a click, and the page shows the output. Its API needs the key in `data/console-key.json`, a local `Host` and no foreign `Origin`. `ecosystem.config.cjs` runs the bot and the console under PM2. The account, buys and fees are in SOL: coin prices and liquidity arrive in dollars and are divided by the live SOL price (`Engine.refresh_market`, DexScreener), a ledger kept in dollars is converted once (`Ledger.convert_to_sol`), and every amount is shown as SOL with dollars in brackets. The report sent to the site carries both (`accountSol`, `costSol`, `pnlSol`, `solUsd`, `fees` beside the dollar fields), so the page works with either. The owner's dials (about twenty, each with a plain description) are defined once in `axiom_paper/dials.json`, which `src/lib/axiom-bot.ts` imports for the site; add or change dials only there. `rules.py` expands them, with the picky level's presets, into the full rule set in `rules.json` that `strategy.py` reads (`Engine.rules`). A coin the bot already traded is bought again only while it is up at least `buy.rebuy_gain_1m_pct` (default 5%, owner's rule) over the last minute, with no cooldown, per-coin limit or old-high rule (`Engine._coin_limits`; `entry.rebuy_gain_1m_pct` -1 restores those, which the older tests pin). `pricegate.py` holds back any price move over 15% until it has lasted 15 seconds, so a stale DexScreener or Radar price cannot set off a stop. At the profit target the bot sells everything unless `sell.ride_momentum` is on and `strategy.momentum` reads the chart as still climbing; it then holds until momentum fades, the price returns to the target or falls the trailing amount. `learner.py` samples coins near the buy level and every closed trade into `learn_samples`, banded by the coin's own figures (Score, age, buy share, liquidity, viewers, momentum, dip, volume and liquidity trends, holder growth, and holder shares: top 10, bundlers, pro traders, snipers, insiders, dev, fresh wallets; never the time of day, owner's rule), skips bands that keep losing, waits out bad timing bands, and raises the buy level after losing runs; the report's `learning` shows it. Tests: `python -m unittest discover -s scripts/axiom-paper-bot/tests` (not part of `pnpm test`).
- `src/server/routes/axiom-bot.ts` + `src/server/axiom-bot-store.ts`: POST `/api/admin/axiom/bot/sync` (server-to-server, `Authorization: Bearer AXIOM_RADAR_TOKEN`, skips the same-origin check) takes the bot's report and any dials changed in its file (`data/paper-bot.toml`), and returns the stored Radar payload (left out when the bot already has it), the dials saved on the page when they are newer, and pending commands; `fast: true` marks the Radar as watched so the tracker sends every 3 seconds. GET `/api/admin/axiom/bot`, PUT `/bot/settings` (merges the dials it is sent into the saved ones, so the form and the Pause button never overwrite each other) and POST `/bot/command` (`flatten`, `reset`) are admin only (same-origin, Zod). Rows `bot-settings`, `bot-report` and `bot-command` live in D1 `axiom_radar_state`. Dials are last-writer-wins by `updatedAt` between the file and the page.
- `src/components/axiom-radar/BotView.tsx` is the Radar's **Trading bot** tab, the bot's control screen: status, the Careful/Balanced/Bold styles (`botPresets` in `src/lib/axiom-bot.ts`), the owner's dials from `scripts/axiom-paper-bot/axiom_paper/dials.json` in open cards (fields from `SettingsFields.tsx`; about twenty, each with a plain description; the bot's full rule set is filled in from them by `rules.py` and the picky level), the learner's card (`BotLearning.tsx`, from the report's `learning`) and a sticky Save bar that says when the bot has applied the change. `BotControls.tsx` holds the pause, close-all and reset buttons and the status chip; `BotActivity.tsx` (below the dials: account value, closest to a buy, activity, open positions, closed trades) completes the tab. There is no separate Paper trades tab. The sync route trims over-long report text (event notes up to 1,000 characters) instead of refusing the report, and a refused sync names the field in its error, which the bot logs. `bot.enabled` is changed only by the Pause button, never by the form. `useBot.ts` polls `/api/admin/axiom/bot`; styles are in `axiom-bot.css`.
- `src/components/admin/AxiomBot.tsx` + `AxiomBotFeed.tsx` is the console's **Axiom Bot** section (Site → Tools, `#admin/axiom-bot`, lazy-loaded): connect to the owner's bot API (address kept in `localStorage` `tl-axiom-bot-url`, password only in `sessionStorage` for the tab), then a status bar (Offline / Live · dry run / Live badge, wallet with copy, SOL balance, trades, feed state), controls (Sniper or Copy-Trade with target wallet, buy SOL capped by the API's `max_buy_amount_sol`, slippage %, the Dry Run switch, on by default, with a confirmation box before LIVE, Start and Emergency stop) and the live feed with Solscan coin and transaction links. It talks to the bot directly from the browser through `src/lib/solana-bot-client.ts` (Zod-checked responses and events, WebSocket reconnect with backoff, stops on close code 4401); nothing goes through the Worker or D1. Styles are the `.axb-*` rules in `src/styles.css`.
- `scripts/solana-bot-api/` (Python 3.11+, runs on the owner's machine or VPS with its own `.env`; see its `README.md` and `DEPLOYMENT.md`) is the bot and its web API. Sniper watches the pump.fun program and Copy-Trade watches one wallet (token in, SOL out), through an RPC WebSocket `logsSubscribe` with reconnect (`listener.py`). Sniper decodes pump.fun's own launch, trade and migration events from the logs (`pumpevents.py`) into live per-coin trade books (`market.py`); with a ruleset (`rules.json` or `RULES_PATH`, parsed by `strategy.py`) it scores each classic launch `decide_after_s` after launch on the features in `strategy.FEATURES` and buys only the coins whose filters pass, then the executor keeps a position per buy (on paper, priced on the bonding curve, in Dry Run) and sells it by the exit rule (profit target, stop, trailing stop, time limit, creator sold, migrated) through Jupiter token → SOL; without a ruleset it buys every classic launch and never sells. `strategy.py` is shared with the backtests in `lab/` (`recorder.py` records every launch and trade from the chain into SQLite, `sim.py` replays a coin with the bot's delay, curve maths, the 1.25 % pump.fun fee each way and network fees, `processor.py` stores each coin's features and exit results, `search.py` runs the walk-forward test), so a tested ruleset runs unchanged. Each buy signal goes Jupiter Swap API v1 quote → build → sign → simulate → send → confirm (`jupiter.py` with `JUPITER_API_KEY`, `executor.py`), with Dry Run (simulate, never send) as the default. Rails in `executor.py` and `config.py`: `MAX_BUY_AMOUNT_SOL`, `MAX_TRADES_PER_RUN` live buys per start, `MIN_SOL_RESERVE`, `MAX_PRICE_IMPACT_PCT`, `MAX_SIGNAL_AGE_S`, `SKIP_CURVE_VARIANTS` (`pumpcurve.py` reads the coin's bonding curve and skips newer curve variants, which rugged most often in live paper tests), one buy per coin per run, a failed simulation is never sent, and `rpc_client.Wallet.sign` refuses a transaction whose fee payer is not the wallet. `rpc_client.py` holds the shared JSON-RPC client and wallet, `main.py` is the terminal runner (`--live` for real trades), `bot_manager.py` (`BotManager`: start/stop, validation, ring buffer of 100 events, broadcaster, thread-safe `emit`), `bot_adapter.py` (`ProjectHooks`: wires the manager to `run_listener`/`run_executor`, `preflight` refuses to start without `JUPITER_API_KEY` or, for LIVE, a key), `api.py` (`/api/health`, Bearer-protected `/api/status`, `/api/start`, `/api/stop`, `/ws/stream?token=`, CORS from `ALLOWED_ORIGINS`, per-IP lockout), the standalone panel `static/index.html`, the SDK `web/solana-bot-client.js` (+ `.d.ts`) and `web/ReactWidgetExample.tsx`. Tests: `python -m unittest discover -s scripts/solana-bot-api/tests` with its `requirements.txt` installed (fake RPC, Jupiter and WebSocket; not part of `pnpm test`).
