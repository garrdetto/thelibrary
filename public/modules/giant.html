Giant's Hand

A meadow full of tiny villagers and one enormous hand. Fling them skyward, catch them on the way down to build a juggle multiplier, pop balloons and sink them through the moving hoop. Nobody gets hurt: they just get very dizzy.

Press on a villager, drag and release; the speed of your swing sets the throw. When a villager lands you score for the height they reached and their time in the air, times your juggle multiplier. Catch a villager while they are still in the air and throw again to raise the multiplier (up to x8); it resets when anyone you threw touches the ground. Balloons pop for 100, a pass down through the gold hoop is worth 600, and a bounce off the side walls adds 50. Dizzy villagers can't be picked up until they shake it off. Your score is saved to the leaderboard as you play, so a round cut short still counts.
