Aim with the mouse, or drag on a touch screen and let go to fire. ← → aim and Space or ↑ fires. Click the waiting bubble or press X to swap. Shots bank off the side walls. Each pop is worth 10 points, and bubbles you cut loose from the ceiling fall for a bonus that grows with the size of the drop. Every few shots that don't pop anything, a new row pushes in from the top; clear the board to reach the next level. The round ends when a bubble crosses the dashed line. Your score is saved to the leaderboard as you play, so a round cut short still counts.
