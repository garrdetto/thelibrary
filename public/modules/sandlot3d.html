How to hit

Aim: drag on the field (or move the mouse) to put the bat circle where the pitch will cross. Swing: tap SWING, click, or press Space as the gold ring closes. Bat under the ball lifts it; early pulls it to left, late pushes it right. Steal: arm a steal before the pitch and the runner breaks on the delivery. Bunt to move runners over, or squeeze one home.
