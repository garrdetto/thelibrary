How to play

Batting: drag on the field (or move the mouse) to put the bat circle where the pitch will cross, then tap or click the field (or press Space) as the gold ring closes. Bat under the ball lifts it; early pulls it, late pushes it the other way. Arm a steal before the pitch and the runner breaks with the delivery, or bunt to move runners over.

Pitching: in the bottom half you take the mound. Pick a pitch, drag the gold target to aim, tap the field to start the windup, and tap again when the marker is in the green to hit your spot. Your fielders run down the ball and cover the bases on their own; tap a throw button (To 1st, To 2nd, To 3rd, Home) to choose where the ball goes.
