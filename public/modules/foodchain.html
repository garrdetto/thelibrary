Food Chain

You start as the smallest fish on the reef. Every fish you eat makes you bigger, and bigger fish can eat you. Fill the growth bar to clear the stage.

Steer with the mouse, drag on a touch screen, or use WASD or the arrow keys. Hold Shift or Space for a short burst of speed that recharges. Fish bigger than you have a red eye and a dark outline. Eating several fish in a row builds a combo multiplier. From stage 2, jellyfish shrink you on contact; from stage 3, a shark sweeps across now and then. Pearls are worth bonus points. Your score is saved to the leaderboard as you play, so a dive cut short still counts.
