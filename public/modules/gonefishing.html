Polar Swat

A polar bear, a hole in the ice and fish that just won't stay put. Wait for a fish to leap, then crush it with the bat as far across the lake as you can. Catch it square and it flies; catch it high or low and it pops up or skids along the ice.

Press Space, click or tap to swing. You get one swing per leap. Where the barrel of the bat meets the fish sets the launch: square on the sweet spot flies farthest, catching it high pops it up, catching it low drives it along the ice. Speed and launch angle decide how far it flies; a shallow landing skips a couple of times and slides on, a steep landing thuds and stops short. Wind blows toward or against the throw, and pike fly farther than walleye, which fly farther than perch. The distance from the hole to where the fish stops is measured in yards to the hundredth, and your longest fish of each five-fish round is saved to the leaderboard.
