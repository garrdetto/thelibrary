Gone Fishing

A polar bear, a hole in the ice and fish that just won't stay put. Wait for a fish to leap, then swat it as far across the lake as you can. Catch it square and it flies; catch it high or low and it pops up or skids along the ice.

Press Space, click or tap to swing. You get one swing per leap. Where the paw meets the fish sets the launch: dead centre is the sweet spot, under the fish sends it high, over the top drives it low along the ice. Speed and launch angle decide how far it flies; a shallow landing skips a couple of times and slides on, a steep landing thuds and stops short. Wind blows toward or against the throw, and pike fly farther than walleye, which fly farther than perch. The distance from the hole to where the fish stops is measured in feet, and your longest fish of each five-fish round is saved to the leaderboard. Press T to switch between the original colours and the WICS colours.
