Corner Cart

You have a cart, a grill and $50. Each morning, check the forecast, stock sausages, buns and toppings, pick a corner and set your price. Then watch the day play out. Your score is the cash you have after day 30.

Higher prices earn more per sale but turn away more customers. Toppings, a good reputation and a busy crowd let you charge more.

Cart upgrades

Customers pass by all day. Each decides whether your price is worth it, based on the weather, the corner, your reputation and how many toppings you offer. You can serve only as many as your grill keeps up with, and you can't sell without both a sausage and a bun, so an empty bin or a long line costs sales and reputation. Rent is paid each day you open. The office district is quiet on weekends, the stadium is packed on match days (shown in the forecast) and the park is busiest when it's warm. Your final cash is saved to the leaderboard at the end of the season.
