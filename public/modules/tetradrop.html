Tetra Drop

← → move (hold to slide) · ↓ soft drop (+1 per row) · Space hard drop (+2 per row) · ↑ or X rotate right · Z rotate left · C or Shift hold · P or Esc pause. Clearing 1, 2, 3 or 4 rows at once scores 100, 300, 500 or 800 times the level, and the level rises every 10 lines. Your score is saved to the leaderboard as you play, so a round cut short still counts.
