Apple Archer

Split the apple from 20 ft. Every hit, he steps back 1.70 ft, and gravity bends each longer shot further down. One miss ends the run.

Aim with the pointer, hold to draw, release to shoot. Keys: ↑ ↓ aim (Shift for fine), Space draw and shoot. Aim inside the apple cam for precision. Wind starts at 5 in a row; at 7 he starts to wobble. Suction-cup arrows only. Your longest run goes to the leaderboard.
