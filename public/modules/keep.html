Hold the Keep

Raiders march on your keep in waves. Pick them up, fling them into the sky or into each other, and keep the wall standing. Spend gold between waves on repairs and defenses.

Press on an attacker, drag and release: the faster you swing, the harder they fly. A fall from high enough is a knockout, and a thrown raider that hits another one knocks both down, so line up bowling strikes. Raiders: footmen hack at the wall, runners are quick, climbers scale the wall for heavy damage, brutes need a long drop, archers shoot from range, and sappers explode on landing, so throw them into crowds. Between waves, buy repairs, a thicker wall, a moat that slows the march and archers who shoot for you. Your score is saved to the leaderboard as you play, so a siege cut short still counts.
