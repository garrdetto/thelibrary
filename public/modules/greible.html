Find the hidden word. Pick 6, 7 or 8 letters; you get one more guess than the word has letters. Every guess must be a real word. After each guess the tiles turn green for the right letter in the right spot, gold for a letter that is in the word somewhere else, and grey for a letter that isn't in it. The clock starts on your first letter and stops when you solve it. Each length has its own leaderboard below, showing the word and your time; sign in to save yours. Keys: type letters, Enter to guess, Backspace to erase.
