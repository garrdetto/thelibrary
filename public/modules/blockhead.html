Hold the arena against waves of blocky raiders. Score milestones unlock new weapons.

Solo

Move WASD or arrows · fire the way you face with Space or J · switch weapon Q/E or 1–6. The mouse aims and a click fires too. On touch screens use the stick and the fire button.

Two players, one keyboard

P1: WASD + Space, switch Q/E. P2: arrows + Enter or /, switch ,/.. Shared score; a downed partner gets back up next wave at half health.

Pause with P or Esc. Kills in quick succession build a multiplier up to x10 that resets two seconds after the last one. Barrels explode when shot and set off their neighbours. Your score is saved to the leaderboard when the round ends.
