Spin Rally

Table tennis with real spin. Read the ball, step into position and choose your stroke: loop with topspin, chop with backspin, smash high balls, drop it short, or save up for a Comet.

The host plays from the near end and keeps score. Share the six-letter code, or invite from the chat.

Player 1 (near end)
Move: WASD or the mouse. Double-tap a direction to dash.
Stroke: hold W (or swing the mouse forward) as you hit for a topspin loop, S (or pull back) for a backspin chop, A/D (or swipe sideways) to aim and add sidespin.
F or left button: smash. G or right button: drop shot. H or middle button: Comet when the meter is full.
Player 2 (far end, same keyboard)
Move: arrow keys, double-tap to dash. Strokes the same way: hold toward the net for topspin, away for backspin, sideways to aim.
/ or Num 1: smash. . or Num 2: drop shot. , or Num 3: Comet.

Serve with any stroke key or a click. Sets go to 11, win by 2, and the serve changes every two points. A ball just out of reach is saved with a stretch, but weakly. Returning heavy spin with a flat stroke sends it into the net or long, so meet backspin with a loop and topspin with a chop or block. Your longest rallies against the CPU go on the leaderboard.
