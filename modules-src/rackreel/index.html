Carving the lake basin…

Upper Midwest field module · fishing & hunting

Two hundred and fifty acres of northern Wisconsin: pine, birch and maple ridges in fall color, a cattail marsh and a twenty-acre lake with an island. Fish the weed edges and deep breaks, drive the trails in the UTV or take the boat out, and still-hunt the ridges for whitetails and black bear. Every animal is weighed or scored the moment it appears: most are around average, one in fifty is a trophy, and a near-record or a new state record is a once-in-a-lifetime find. Live bait fishes under a float; lures only draw strikes while you reel them. Earn cash from every catch, tag and task, then outfit yourself at the log cabin. The day runs on a clock: after dark, find Wisco Wild Man on his cooler down the shore from the dock and go bowfishing for carp under the lights, out to the island flats or under the interstate bridge into the back bay.
