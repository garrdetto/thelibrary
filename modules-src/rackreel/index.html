Carving the lake basin…

Upper Midwest field module · fishing & hunting

Two hundred and fifty acres of northern Wisconsin: pine, birch and maple ridges in fall color, a cattail marsh and a twenty-acre lake with an island. Fish the weed edges and deep breaks, drive the trails in the UTV or take the boat out, and still-hunt the ridges for whitetails and black bear. Every animal is weighed or scored the moment it appears, and about one buck or fish in twenty-five is a record-book contender. Earn cash from every catch, tag and task, then outfit yourself at the log cabin.
