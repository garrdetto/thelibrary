How the physics and the rules work
  • Damage follows the impact force: momentum (mass × speed) lost in a split second. Hit harder, hurt more; a full-strength hit takes about a third of a car.
  • The nose carries the engine. Front hits cost power; backing into someone protects your motor, and the tailgate shrugs off most of it.
  • Dirt gives less sideways grip than pavement, so the tail slides on hard turns. Short, straight runs land the heaviest hits.
  • A car that goes 60 seconds without striking another one is called out of the heat, just like a fair derby.
  • Points: hits landed, knockouts, surviving seconds and 1000 for the win. The best heats lead the board.
