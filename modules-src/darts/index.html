How to throw Click and hold on the board to aim (move to fine-tune) and a power meter swings; let go in the green band. Between the two white lines is Perfect and flies true, the rest of the green misses slightly (more the further from the lines), and in the red the dart drops (too soft) or rides high (too hard). Right-click cancels. Keyboard: ←→↑↓ aim (Shift for fine), hold Space and release in the green, Esc cancels a throw. With the Cross (test) meter, letting go locks the height and a second click or Space stops the side meter; P or Esc pauses. The record book is below the board on this page (in pass & play and tournaments, Player 1 is you; team matches aren't recorded).
