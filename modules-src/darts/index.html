Darts

Board

A regulation board at 1.73 m, thrown from 2.37 m. Click and hold on the board to aim and a power meter swings up and down; let go to throw. The dead centre of the green flies true and anywhere in the green lands within seven holes of your aim; the meter fades to yellow and then red, where the dart drops (too soft) or rides high (too hard). Soft Tip plays its own rules: any bed can finish and Double Bull is on.

Format

Opponents

Open a room and share its code (or invite from the chat), or join a friend's room. Up to four at the oche; everyone throws with the same steadiness.

Mouse or touch: click and hold on the board to aim (move to fine-tune), watch the power meter and let go in the green band; right-click cancels. Keyboard: ←→↑↓ aim (Shift for fine), hold Space and release in the green band, Esc cancels a throw, and P or Esc pauses. Each board keeps its own record book: fewest darts for 501, 301 and Cricket and the best eight-round Count Up, from any leg on Normal or Hard (in pass & play, Player 1 is you).
