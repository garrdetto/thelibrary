# Fox Lake (source)

Source for `public/modules/foxlake.html`, module id `foxlake`. Fox Lake is one world made of two places: a Fox Plains town (lots, houses, cars and neighbors, built by the same rules as classic Fox Plains) and Rack & Reel's Bear Paw Lake country south of it, joined by a county road. It replaced Fox Plains 2 (`#module-foxplains2` redirects here). Every player starts fresh in Fox Lake: it has its own shared world, its own money and its own Rack & Reel profile, and classic Fox Plains and standalone Rack & Reel keep theirs.

Design and decisions: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-fox-lake-design.md`. Performance and leak results: `docs/foxlake/PERF.md`.

## How it is put together

Fox Lake **is** Rack & Reel's game (`modules-src/rackreel/game/Game.ts`) with a host-world extension. Everything the lake country does in Rack & Reel works here unchanged, because it is the same code: the renderer and its tone mapping, cached sun and moon shadows, sky and fog, terrain, water, foliage and grass, the skinned hunter and the character creator, fishing, hunting, the boat, UTV and quad, Wild Man's carp bowfishing, wildlife, the record books and the hunting party.

`index.ts` starts `new Game(canvas, { terrain, extension, bowfishAnyHour: true, gearAtCabin: true, profile: 'foxlake', outfitters: true })`:

- `terrain`: `new Terrain(FOX_LAKE_SHAPE)` (`region.ts`). The lake country's north edge has no rim of hills; it blends down to the town's ground (`TOWN_Y`), and land beyond the square is the town's (`TOWN_LAND`). Standalone Rack & Reel uses `STANDALONE_SHAPE` (every edge rimmed), so its heights are unchanged.
- `extension`: `FoxLakeTown` (`town/FoxLakeTown.ts`), Rack & Reel's `GameExtension` hook (build, ready, update, collide, obstacle, interact, prompt, spawn, place name; the shared world drives the clock).
- `bowfishAnyHour`: Wild Man takes you carp bowfishing at any hour. Fox Lake shares one clock with everyone, so nobody can skip to dark.
- `profile: 'foxlake'`: Rack & Reel's progress (character, gear, cash) is a separate profile (`/api/rackreel?game=foxlake`, stored as `foxlake:<user id>`), so Fox Lake starts fresh and standalone Rack & Reel is untouched.
- `outfitters`: Fox Outfitters (`world/Cabin.ts` `FOX_OUTFITTERS`, model `outfitters`) replaces the cabin: a bigger log store with shelving, the gear rack, wardrobe and counter, and an open garage bay on the east side where the side-by-side is parked on a concrete floor the wheels ride on. The FOX OUTFITTERS board over the bay is `world/StoreSign.ts`.

### Coordinates (`region.ts`)

The scene uses Rack & Reel's coordinates (left-handed, +x east, +z north, the lake country is the 1024 m square around the origin). The town keeps the shared world's right-handed coordinates and hangs under one node that moves it north of the square and mirrors z, so it reads exactly as classic Fox Plains shows it:

```
scene = (town.x - 356, town.y + 2, 1152 - town.z)
```

East Ave (town x = 87) runs south out of town and becomes the county road (`CONNECTOR`, `town/TownGround.ts`), which meets the interstate where it leaves the lake country's north edge. Snapshots sent to the shared world are in town coordinates. Fox Lake's world is its own Durable Object (`FoxLakeWorld`, `/api/maplestreet?world=foxlake`, saved in the `foxlake_world` table), so its players never appear in classic Fox Plains.

### The town (`fp/`, `town/`)

- `fp/` is Fox Plains 2's Babylon code for the town (it moved here when Fox Lake replaced Fox Plains 2): `world/Town.ts` (roads, sidewalks, driveways, lawns with mowing stripes, lamps, for-sale signs), `world/Downtown.ts` (shops and signs atlas), `world/Houses.ts` (every lot's house from the shared build data), `core/` (mesh building, procedural PBR surfaces, the ground material plugin, glTF assets), `physics/` (Rapier, the Fox Plains raycast cars with the same numbers, parking, vehicle views). Its builders draw through `setRenderer` (any `GfxHost`) and hang their roots under `setHostRoot`.
- Fox Lake's own picture is `FOX_LAKE_LOOK` in `index.ts`, passed to Rack & Reel as `GameOptions.look` (`LightingLook` in `rackreel/world/Lighting.ts`): light sun and moon shadows (darkness 0.9; Rack & Reel's is 0.62), shaded sides lifted by a 1.4× sky fill, ground bounce and ambient (`fill`), a night 15 % brighter (exposure scales with the night amount) and matte characters (no specular, a softer fill). Standalone Rack & Reel passes none of it. `FOX_LAKE_SHADOW_MAP` (1024, `GameOptions.shadowMapSize`) halves Rack & Reel's 2048 shadow map, with the blur kernel scaled to match so shadows stay as soft; the town has far more casters than the lake country.
- `town/TownGfx.ts` is that host inside Rack & Reel's renderer: static casters (merged shadow proxies) go to Rack & Reel's cached shadow layer by 128 m cell near the player, moving ones (vehicles) to its moving layer through `host.addCaster`; only those within the shadow frame's reach of the player cast (`Game.hostCasters`), and they are marked tight (`ShadowLayers.markTight`) so a car's own shadow on its body is redrawn at the near slack at any distance instead of lagging and jumping, and the town's PBR materials light from an environment cube baked from Rack & Reel's sky.
- `town/TownGround.ts` lays the ground north of the square the way Rack & Reel lays its own (one vertex-coloured mesh over its detail texture, 4 m apart) and colours it by the lake country's rules (`groundColor`: meadow and dry grass, needles and moss under the woods, gravel shoulders along the roads), so the two places blend instead of changing at the seam. Around town the land rolls (`region.ts` `outsideHeight`: gentle wooded hills up to about 7 m, level in town, on the roads and on the roadside lots, easing in over 75 m); the lake country's open north edge blends to it (`TerrainShape.outsideHeightAt`) and meets it exactly at the edge, and the interstate eases down to the town's ground over its last stretch, so there is no step anywhere at the seam. Rack & Reel's grass grows out there too (`TOWN_LAND.grassAt`, off the town, the roads and the lots). Roads are ribbons in the lake country's asphalt (`roadRibbon`, `countyRoadTexture`: the interstate's grit with white edge lines and a double yellow centre line); trees fill the strip east of town. The town's yard and forest-ring trees are planted as Rack & Reel foliage (`Foliage.plantTrees`) at the spots the town layout picks.
- `town/Cars.ts`: your vehicles (the starter pickup and trailer, the riding mower, V for the next one you own) are Fox Plains Rapier cars driven through Rack & Reel's `VehicleController` as external vehicles (`addExternal`), so seats, camera, enter and exit, HUD and audio are Rack & Reel's. A stand-in node in scene coordinates follows each car so the hunter sits upright in it.
- `town/Neighbors.ts`: other players and the simulated neighbors, as Rack & Reel hunters (an everyday outfit and a face picked from their name), and their vehicles, interpolated 120 ms behind.
- `town/Atlas.ts`: the map of all of Fox Lake, shared by the full map and the minimap. A raster base 2 m to the pixel, painted a slice per frame after loading: hill shading and 2 m contours (darker every 10 m), depth-shaded water with a lit shoreline, meadow, stippled woods, marsh, rock and the town's lawns, the woods beyond the edge of the world dimmed. On top, vectors at any zoom: every road with casing and centre line (town streets and curves, the county road, the Lake Road, the interstate with its bridge and barricade, the lakeshore trail), lots (yours gold, owned tan, for sale green), buildings, the roadside places, labels for areas, streets and places, and places (`Poi`) with icons: shops, the casino, town hall, library, hotel and cinema, the county road's cabins, gas station, motel and tavern, Fox Outfitters, the dock, Wild Man's camp, the stands and the bridge. Each place routes to its door or the road in front of it.
- `town/WorldMap.ts` + `town/RouteGrid.ts`: Ctrl opens the full map, centred on you and zoomed in (1.6 px a metre); drag to pan, scroll or the buttons to zoom, arrow keys and +/− on the focused map. Click a place (or pick it from the list beside the map) for directions to it, or click any spot. A banner at the top calls each turn with its distance, re-routing when you stray more than 30 m and clearing within 22 m ("You have arrived at …"). `RouteGrid` rasterises the world into 6 m cells (streets, county road, Lake Road and interstate cheapest, roadside lots, then trails, open ground and woods; water, buildings, the interstate's rails and the world's edge blocked), runs A*, simplifies the path into legs and names them (street names from `placeName`). A spot that can't be reached (open water, an island) routes to the closest reachable point.
- `town/FoxMinimap.ts`: the corner map is the same atlas everywhere (no switch between a town map and a lake map at the seam), turned with the camera, 190 m across (N cycles 110, 190, 340 and 640 m), with the route and its goal, places, your party and vehicles. It draws through Rack & Reel's `GameExtension.drawMinimap` and hides Rack & Reel's own.
- `town/TownHud.ts`: the wallet chip, who is around, the zone and the connection.
- `core/RegionCuller.ts`: far town cells and far lake-country tree stands are switched off (beyond 640 m the fog hides them), so the town costs nothing at the lake and the woods cost nothing in town.

### Vehicle look

Fox Lake always draws the game's own bundled vehicles (the Maple Hauler starter truck and the other `bundled-*` scans): a record re-published from the vehicle editor with a bundled id replaces it in Fox Plains but not here (`index.ts` `keepBundled`).

`fp/physics/ScanGlass.ts`: scanned bodies have their windows painted into the texture; they become clear glass. Each body triangle is a window when the texture under it is glass-coloured (`glassTexel`: dark and grey, or a grey-blue sky reflection), it sits between 50 % and 95 % of the body's height, faces sideways rather than up or down, and on the sides faces outward; lone hits are dropped and small gaps filled across shared edges. The window triangles move to the end of the index buffer (once per geometry) and draw as a second submesh in clear glass, and a clone of the body draws only its back faces in a dark upholstery colour: the cab's interior seen through the glass.

`fp/physics/VehicleModels.ts`: paint finishes keep little metal (`FINISH`: 0.02 gloss, 0.16 metallic, 0 matte) under a glossy clear coat that keeps the bright HDR highlights, and authored body panels keep at most `BODY_METAL_CAP` (0.25) metal. Every window (blended glass materials, and opaque materials named glass, window or windshield, but not glowing lamp lenses) is replaced by clear glass (`clearGlass`: nearly colourless, alpha 0.14, sky reflections kept over the alpha).

### Physics

The town and your cars are on Rapier (`fp/physics/Physics.ts`): the flat town ground, every building, house and tree as streamed static colliders, and the lake country as a heightfield, so a car can drive down the county road to the lake. On foot, the hunter walks Rack & Reel's way and is pushed out of the town's solids (`Physics.pushOut`) and out of every vehicle, yours and the neighbours', in town and out in the lake country (`Physics.pushOutVehicles`, a capsule contact query against the vehicle colliders); the UTV and quad bump into them (`Physics.blocked`). The boat stays on the lake.

The interstate is solid (`country/RoadPhysics.ts`): the median jersey wall, the bridge's side walls, the guardrails (with posts) and the road-closed barricade are Rapier boxes for the town's cars, and Rack & Reel's side-by-side and quad test the same pieces (`hitsBarrier` through `GameExtension.obstacle`). The bridge has a deck collider, and the lake country's heightfield is lifted to the interstate's and the Lake Road's surfaces so wheels ride on the asphalt. The land around town is a second heightfield (4 m) over the flat town ground.

Water (`town/Wading.ts`): a car in the lake country's water is dragged in proportion to the depth past 25 cm; at 85 cm (over the doors) the engine floods (no throttle) and 1.6 s later the car is towed back to the last spot where it stood on dry ground, facing away from the water. Trailers and parked cars that roll in are towed out the same way. Rack & Reel's own land vehicles already stop at their wading depth.

### Money

A signed-in player has one Fox Lake balance: Fox Lake's world holds it (`RoomLogic.adjustMoney`, the Durable Object's `/wallet`), town jobs pay into it, and `/api/rackreel?game=foxlake` (bounties, the catalog) and `/api/casino` pay and charge it (`src/server/shared-wallet.ts`). The Fox Lake profile's starting cash moves in once. Classic Fox Plains and standalone Rack & Reel money are separate. Guests play the casino with their town cash (by this browser's guest key); their lake cash is kept in the tab.

Lots are bought in Fox Lake itself: walk onto a lot for sale and press E.

## Northwoods Lodge Casino & Bar (`casino/`)

A walk-in casino on the north-east corner of East Ave and South St (town coordinates in `CasinoLayout.ts`), Fox Lake only (classic Fox Plains has woods there).

- **Building** (`CasinoBuilding.ts`, `CabinetModel.ts`): brick shell with the doorway and canopy on South St, carpet, a coffered ceiling with light panels, the bar (counter, brass rail, back bar with lit bottle shelves, pendant lamps, eight stools), the blackjack table and ten upright video-slot cabinets (plinth, lower door, bill acceptor, ticket printer, card reader, lit button deck, chrome-trimmed screen bezel with speaker grilles, topper, candle light, LED edges) with slot chairs. It is built with the town's builders and colliders, so it is culled, shadowed and solid like the rest of town.
- **Displays** (`screen/MachineDisplay.ts`): each cabinet's portrait screen and topper are self-lit textures in scene space: an attract page when idle; while playing, the header (title, meters), the reel window and CREDIT / BET / WIN; jackpot plaques on the topper at the current bet.
- **Math layer** (`math/`, no Babylon or DOM): `rng.ts` (crypto on the server, seeded for tests), `reels.ts` (strips, stops, 1,024/243-ways wins with multiplying wilds), `lines.ts` (25/20 lines), `machines.ts` (the ten machines; a spin resolves completely into a timeline the screen replays), `blackjack.ts` (six decks, S17, 3:2, double, one split). `node scripts/casino-sim.mjs [spins]` prints each machine's return, base share, feature frequency and average feature win; `scale` in each definition sets the return (about 94%).
- **The ten machines**, five game styles in two original themes each (rules researched from the real cabinets; names, symbols and art are this game's own): link Hold & Spin (Northern Lights Link, Gold Pan Link), house-building free games with a storm blowdown (Lodge Builders, Hay Day Barns), gold-head 1,024-ways free games (Moose Gold, Mustang Gold), the same with the Revolution wheel (Moose Gold Revolution, Mustang Gold Revolution), and gem collectors with Cash Collect, free games and a jackpot pick (Bigfoot Bucks, Lake Monster Loot).
- **Animation layer** (`screen/ReelSet.ts`, `screen/SlotSession.ts`): reels are strip textures moved by an `AnimationGroup` (wind-up, motion-blurred spin, staggered stops with overshoot, anticipation on reels that could complete a feature); wins pulse framed cells; features play on an overlay and on extra planes (the Revolution wheel is a disc rotated by an animation). `SlotSession.demo(kind)` plays a fun-money outcome with a given feature (debug and screenshots).
- **Money** (`CasinoClient.ts`, `src/server/routes/casino.ts`): everyone plays with their own Fox Lake money (signed in: the account's; guests: their town cash). Spins, hands and beers are decided and paid on the server (`/api/casino`: stake taken first, winnings after; blackjack tables and collector meters in D1). Solo town play runs the same rules in the page.
- **Limits** (`CasinoLayout.ts`): slots $0.40 to $5 a spin; blackjack $5 to $200 opening bets; a beer is $5.
- **Tables** (`TableSessions.ts`): blackjack and the bar as overlays while the camera looks at the table or the bartender; a beer ($5) appears on the counter.
- **Seating** uses Rack & Reel's host hooks: `setPerch` (sit, no walking), `setCameraShot` (fixed camera), and `data-host-modal` overlays (Rack & Reel's input pauses while one is open). At a machine the view is first person.

## Roads and the county road's places

- **One network** (`country/Roads.ts`): the town's streets, the county road, the interstate and the new Lake Road all connect. The Lake Road leaves the interstate at an at-grade junction east of the lake (`LAKE_JUNCTION_T`; the south-west rail and the median open there, `HIGHWAY_GAPS`, used by Rack & Reel's `buildHighway`) and runs south-west across the flats to a turning apron in front of Fox Outfitters' garage. Its bed is carved into the lake country (`TerrainShape.carve`): level across the road and easing back to the land over 6 m. The interstate's south-east end, where it runs into the hills, is closed with barricades and drums (`country/Barricade.ts`).
- **One road surface**: every road and lot is the lake country's asphalt (`rackreel/world/Highway.ts`: `paintAsphalt` is shared by the interstate's `roadTexture`, the two-lane `countyRoadTexture` and the plain tiling `asphaltTexture` the town's streets use through `TownOptions.asphalt`).
- **Keep-clear** (`region.ts` `roadClear`): a 2 m distance field to the county road, the Lake Road and its apron and the roadside lots; Rack & Reel keeps trees, rocks, ferns, logs and grass off them (`TerrainShape.roadClear`), paints gravel shoulders along them and leaves them out of tyre wear.
- **Roadside places** (`roadside/`, town coordinates, built with the town's builders under its node like the casino, every solid a Rapier collider, lettering in scene space by `RoadsideSigns.ts`), spaced along the county road between town and the interstate: Birch Hollow, Tamarack and Loon Call cabins (log walls with interlocking corners, porches with railings, chimneys, woodpiles, name boards at the drive), The Rusty Lure Tavern (barn-board roadhouse, tin roof, porch, neon OPEN / COLD BEER / LIVE BAIT · POOL / BURGERS, picnic table, pylon sign), the Pine Cone Motel (twelve rooms, office, covered walk, striped parking, VACANCY pylon) and Bear Paw Fuel & Bait (store, canopy with four pumps, ice chest, price pylon). E at a door says what's there; `placeName` names each lot.

## Tyre marks and two-tracks

`rackreel/world/GroundWear.ts`: every wheel of every land vehicle presses the 33 cm cell under it on the lake country's ground, a 3072 x 3072 byte map uploaded in small rectangles. The terrain (`WearPlugin`) darkens and flattens pressed grass and turns it to bare dirt where wear is high, and the grass and weeds (`wear` foliage look) flatten and yellow over the same cells. One pass leaves two pressed tracks; dirt starts after about 17 passes over a line and the ruts are bare after about 40. Wear lasts for the session.

## World map

`docs/foxlake/map/index.html` is an interactive map of the whole world, drawn from the game's layout (`tools/map-data.ts`) and its landmarks (`docs/foxlake/map/landmarks.json`). Rebuild it with `node scripts/foxlake-map.mjs`.

## Developer panel (`dev/`)

F8 opens it for a site administrator (the Esc menu lists the key for them; the site's Developer Panel button can toggle it with a same-origin `tl-dev-panel` message, and `?panel=1` opens it once you head out). For everyone else F8 does nothing. The shared world decides who that is: the Worker reads the session (`src/server/routes/maplestreet.ts`, `isAdmin`), the Durable Object carries it on the socket's identity, the world tells the page with `welcome.dev`, and it checks every `dev` message against the same flag (`maplestreet/shared/DevTools.ts`), so a hand-made message from anyone else is refused. Only Fox Lake's world turns the tools on (`FoxLakeWorld`, `WorldServer`'s `devTools`); classic Fox Plains ignores the messages.

- **Spawn a vehicle** (`FoxLakeDev.spawn`, `Placement.ts`): every registered vehicle that drives (built-in, bundled, published and retired; trailers are left out), a few metres in front of you or beside a chosen player. The page picks a clear, dry spot and lays the vehicle on the slope; the world accepts it within 30 m of that player, twelve at a time.
- **Spawned vehicles belong to nobody** and are in nobody's garage. The world keeps each one in its snapshots like any other vehicle (`owner` is the driver's session, or `shared` while parked), so `town/Neighbors.ts` draws it. E beside a parked one asks the world for it (`shared` `claim`); the world gives it to the first to ask within reach, one driver per vehicle and one spawned vehicle per driver. That player's page swaps the snapshot car for a real Fox Plains Rapier car driven through Rack & Reel's vehicle controller (`SharedCars.ts`) and reports its pose with each state message (`state.s`). Getting out lets it roll to a stop and hands it back (`release`); it stays parked there for everyone. A driver who disconnects leaves it parked where it was.
- **Five minutes**: the world removes a spawned vehicle five minutes after it was spawned, for everyone at once. One that is being driven then stays until its driver gets out. The panel lists the live ones with their time left and a Remove now button.
- **Teleport**: `Places.ts` is the list (`{ id, label, x, z }` in scene coordinates; add a row to add a place), plus Home and every player in the world. It puts you on foot at the nearest safe ground the way H does; a vehicle you were in stays where it was.
- **Give cash**: a whole amount up to $1,000,000 to yourself or a player in the world, through `RoomLogic.adjustMoney`, so it is the same balance the lake country and the casino use; the world saves it at once and the recipient's wallet chip updates over the socket. A guest's town cash (kept under their browser's guest key) is changed the same way and the panel says so; their lake cash lives in their own tab and cannot be changed from here.
- **Solo** (`?solo=1`): the same rules run in the page, so the panel is open to whoever plays ("Solo: changes stay on this device"). Solo money is unlimited, so there is nothing to give.

Spawned vehicles carry no paint, damage, fuel or hitch, and a spawned mower does not cut grass or earn mowing pay.

## Gear and bounties

Fox Lake starts Rack & Reel with `gearAtCabin`: the rod and rifle hang on the gear rack in Fox Outfitters; 1 or 2 at the rack takes one (swapping the other back); away from the rack you carry only that one. Bounties are flat everywhere: $10 a carp, $50 a deer, $100 a bear (`shared/Economy.ts`).

## Controls

Rack & Reel's, plus E at your car (drive), at shops (Maple Threads opens the wardrobe), at the roadside places, at a slot machine, bar stool or blackjack seat, V to bring out another of your vehicles, H to go home (every 15 minutes), Ctrl for the full map and directions, N to zoom the corner map. The Esc menu lists every control (`BASE_CONTROLS` in `rackreel/ui/PauseMenu.ts` plus `FoxLakeTown.controls`). 1 and 2 only work at the cabin's gear rack. F8 opens the developer panel (administrators).

## Debugging

`window.__foxlake.town.debug()` (connection, neighbors, colliders, cars, culling), `window.__foxlake.town.dev.debug()` (panel access, spawned vehicles, the one being driven), `window.__foxlake.game.host()`. `?solo=1` plays the town on this device; `?hour=21` pins the sky. `node scripts/foxlake-capture.mjs [baseUrl] [outDir]` loads the built page in Chrome, takes screenshots at fixed spots with frame-time and scene counts, and walks town → lake → town five times to check that mesh, material and texture counts stay flat.

## Assets

- Everyday outfits are Meshy retextures of the hunter (`assets-src/rackreel/meshy/hunter-everyday.meshy.json`), ingested with `modules-src/rackreel/tools/ingest-hunter.py` (`HUNTER_ONLY` limits it to named outfits) and mapped in `tools/builders_meshy_hunter.py`.
- The golf cart, ATV, zero-turn, riding mower and trailer are Meshy models (`assets-src/models/foxlake/vehicles.meshy.json`) turned, scaled and set on the built-in's wheels by `tools/build-vehicles.py` (Blender) into `assets-src/models/maplestreet/vehicles/`, then `pnpm assets`. `tools/build-vehicles.py` welds Meshy's triangle soup and smooths the shading (`scripts/blender/smooth_normals.py`: weld, smooth, creases over 40° sharp, weighted normals) and splits each wheel into a `wheel-<n>` node centred on its own axle (`scripts/blender/vehicle-fix.py` does the same to an existing body). `fp/physics/VehicleModels.ts` hangs each split wheel on the nearest steer/spin pivot moved to that centre (`hangSplitWheels`, `WheelView.lift`), so wheels spin and steer on their true axle where the model drew them. Vehicle models skip the glTF loader's handedness flip (`authoredFrame`: `__root__` turned 180° and mirrored is x → −x, which drew every body back to front inside the town's mirrored root); they now face the way the vehicle editor and Fox Plains show them. Lamp lenses draw with a polygon offset (`zOffset`) so the body never pokes through them, and glass has a depth pre-pass so its faces stop flickering against each other.
