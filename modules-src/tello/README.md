# Tello (source)

Montello's builder for Fox Lake (shown on the site as Life Sim); the standalone Tello page was removed and `#module-tello` opens Life Sim. It builds Montello, Wisconsin at 1:1, built from real survey data: the lidar ground, every mapped road, bridge, lake, river and pond, every building on its real footprint under its measured roof, and every tree at its measured height. You walk it or drive it and get directions on the map. It runs on this device only (no shared world).

## How it is put together

It runs inside Fox Lake (Rack & Reel's `Game` with Fox Lake's look, `foxlake/look.ts`), so these are the same code as Fox Lake and are never forked here:

- the renderer, tone mapping, sky, sun and moon, fog and cached shadows (`rackreel/core`, `rackreel/world/Lighting.ts`)
- the skinned hunter, his animations, the character creator and wardrobe (`rackreel/game/Avatar.ts`)
- the meadow grass and the trees (`rackreel/world/Foliage.ts`, `plantTrees`)
- the water shader (`rrWater` in `rackreel/world/Shaders.ts`)
- the building finishes, facades and Rapier world (`foxlake/fp/`)
- the vehicles: the bundled Maple Hauler pickup and the other Fox Plains vehicles, with Fox Lake's clear glass and paint (`foxlake/fp/physics/`, driven through `foxlake/town/Cars.ts`)

Rack & Reel's own lake country still stands in its square around the scene's origin, sealed by its rim of hills. Montello lies a kilometre and a half north of it, out of sight and out of reach, and the lake country's scenery is switched off (`RegionCuller`). The player has no rod or rifle here (`gearAtCabin`).

### Frames (`region.ts`)

- **map**: x east, z north, metres from the origin near City Hall (UTM zone 16N, 1 unit = 1 m), y metres above `frame.base` (228 m). The baked files and `TelloLand` use it.
- **scene**: map + `land.offset`. The map's centre is at scene (0, 4096): a whole map width from the origin, so Rack & Reel's water shader reads Tello's depth map through a repeating texture.
- **town**: (map.x, y, −map.z), right-handed, for Fox Lake's builders and car physics; it hangs under a root at `land.offset` with z mirrored.

`TelloLand` answers for the ground (`groundAt`), bridge decks (`deckAt`, `heightAt`), water (`waterAt`, `depthAt`), cover, tree crowns and the distance to pavement (`clearAt`). `TelloTerrain` is Rack & Reel's `Terrain` with Montello as the land beyond the square and depth taken from Montello's own water levels.

### The world (`world/`, `town/`)

- `world/Ground.ts`: vertex-coloured ground 4 m apart in 256 m meshes, coloured by the lake country's palette from the real ground cover. `GroundStream` builds the meshes only round whoever moves (700 m round the start while loading, then every chunk within 1 km, close ones at once and the rest one every 0.06 s) and disposes chunks past 1.9 km; a chunk's vertex data is dropped as soon as it is on the GPU. The seen ring is about 9.4 million nodes, which built whole cost about 700 MB of vertex copies and most of the load time. The Rapier heightfield is laid in 256-cell tiles, only where the ground is seen, and the water depth map is one byte a texel. `parseGrids` reads the grids as views of the unpacked file, not copies.
- `world/Clearance.ts`: where pavement and buildings collide, worked out once at load. Buildings a street, highway or lane runs through (a trailer across a park street, sheds on alleys) are left out of every builder and the map (`blockedBuildings`); places keep theirs, and a driveway that ends at its garage never blocks it. Walks and trails stop at the curb of any road and at walls (`pavementOf`, `insideBuilding`), and open onto lots.
- `data/Forecourts.ts`: each fuel station with pumps gets a paved forecourt over the islands, out to its street and up to the store door, with no stalls; the pavement field is marked under it, so no tree grows by the pumps. Baked trees whose trunk stands on pavement or in a wall are left out (`Forest`, `TRUNK_CLEAR`).
- `world/Water.ts`: a surface mesh over the wet cells that follows the level grid (Buffalo Lake 234.81 m, Lake Montello 238.71 m, the Fox River below the dam 233.28 m), drawn with `rrWater`.
- `world/Forest.ts`: Montello's trees. Every baked crown is indexed (407,000 on the three-mile map) and planted in 384 m stands only as the walker or driver comes within 720 m of a stand (`Forest.update`, one stand per 0.15 s; the ring round the start is planted while the town loads), so load time and memory follow where you go, not the 140 km2 map; Rack & Reel's foliage supplies the far models (culled at 640 m). Each stand is planted together with its infill: the bake thins the far woods (gaps of 9 to 20 m), so the stand grows extra trunks on a jittered 6 m grid wherever the map says woods cover with closed canopy (`INFILL_CANOPY`), dry, at least `INFILL_ROAD` (3.5 m) from pavement and walls, at least `INFILL_GAP` (7.5 m) from any other trunk, with the kind and height of the nearest lidar tree. Fields, lawns, lots and bare ground stay open. About 196,000 infill trees if every stand were planted. Trunks turn solid for cars within about 50 m (`solidTrees`) and push walkers (`push`). Tests: `tests/forest.test.ts`.
- `world/Roads.ts`: ribbons for every road by class (lined highway asphalt, plain streets and driveways, gravel, concrete walks, dirt trails), junction patches, parking lots, and bridge decks with railings.
- `world/Buildings.ts`: walls from each footprint up to each block's measured eave; the measured roof on each rectangle (gable along either axis, hip, single slope or flat); colliders from the same rectangles; windows and doors sized to the wall; merged per material and 128 m cell.
- `world/Storefronts.ts` + `data/Downtown.ts`: downtown's storefronts on West and East Montello Street and Main Street. Every store on those streets gets a commercial front over the wall that faces it (`planFronts`; a corner building can have one on each street): brick, stucco, paint or rock-faced block upstairs with tall windows under arched or flat hoods (or bricked in), a bracketed or corbelled cornice, bay windows, a corner turret with a spire, and a street storey of display windows, vertical boards, a log front or stone veneer, a raised stone terrace with a railing, iron balconies, shingled, striped or ribbed-metal awnings, sign boards and hanging signs (`world/SignAtlas.ts`, plain lettering, real business names). Fronts matched to the owner's own photos and videos are `KNOWN_FRONTS` (the south side of West Montello between West Street and the supper club), `MAIN_STREET_FRONTS` (the turreted corner of Montello and Main on both streets, the three fronts south of it, the long buff block and the rock-faced block by the alley on the east side), `WEST_MONTELLO_DRIVE_FRONTS` (the realty office, the mural building and the north side, and the three south-side fronts by the river), `MAIN_SOUTH_FRONTS` (H A Freitag & Son and the three fronts past the car alley) and `EAST_MONTELLO_FRONTS` (the theatre, the white building and the two porch houses); the rest are picked from each building's seed (`defaultFront`). Shop fronts put an entry in the middle of each shop bay (one per seven or eight metres) and a narrow stair door at one end of two-storey fronts (`entryPlan`), unless the photos place them (`doors`, `stairDoor`). Photos of Street View are never used.
- `world/StreetDressing.ts`: black acorn lamp posts along the downtown street (`lampSpots`, clear of corners and alleys) with the blue MONTELLO banner or a hanging painted bass, and a basket of petunias each; and the water tower on its single column at its mapped spot, painted as it is: navy below, cream above with "City of Montello", a gold cap.
- `data/Streets.ts`: corrections made at load on top of the baked map (renderer-free, so tests see them): West Montello, East Montello and Main Street are one lane each way with a parking lane along both curbs, at full width wherever the buildings leave room for a sidewalk (`streetProfiles`, which also marks the wider pavement in the pavement field); Main Street's two overlapping OpenStreetMap ways at its north end become one street up to a grass island and a lane each side of it to the light (`fixMainSplit`, `mainIsland`); the West Montello Street bridge is set level with the street, widened over both sidewalks, its approaches filled and the Montello River dug out under it (`fixBridge`); buildings the lidar measured low are raised and three the footprints miss on Main Street are added (`fixBuildings`); and the light's cycle (`signalPhase`).
- `data/RoadFixes.ts` + `world/JunctionShapes.ts` + `world/Guardrails.ts`: the road network made tidy at load. Corners inside one way become arcs; every street and highway is bent smoothly (a Gaussian bump, easing out toward junctions) so its full width and a sidewalk (`BUILDING_MARGIN` 1.6 m) clear every building, which is how the road now curves round the corner house by the fire station (downtown, junction nodes and bridges stay put; a street squeezed between two buildings is the only exception); the 2 m pavement field follows the moved roads. Ribbons are built on stations 3 m apart with capped miters, one hair-thin layer per road and a `zOffset` per class so crossing roads never z-fight; each junction notch wider than a right angle is filled to the corner where the two arms' edges meet and each dead end gets a half disc (`junctionFills`). Highways and county roads are plain asphalt with painted white edge lines and a broken yellow centre line where passing is allowed, solid double yellow through curves and near cross streets; residential streets carry no lines. Steel W-beam guardrails (`guardrailRuns`: deep water with a steep bank, or a drop of 2.2 m, within a few metres of the road's edge) stand on Main Street's west side where the river touches the road and on a few other stretches; every span is a solid box. The ground sinks 5 to 21 cm under pavement so ribbons never show it through (`groundSink`). `tests/roads.test.ts` checks all of it.
- `world/StreetPaint.ts`: raised sidewalks with a curb along both sides of every downtown street (ten feet from the fronts to the curb on Main and East Montello Street; on West Montello Street a five-foot walk along the curb with the storefront apron behind it up to the fronts), dropping to curb cuts at alleys and driveways; every other sidewalk in town is raised with a curb face too (`Roads.ts`). And the paint on those streets: double yellow centre line, the white parking-lane line with stall ticks (or an edge line where there's no room to park), gaps at cross streets and driveways, and stop bars and ladder crosswalks at the light.
- `world/Intersection.ts`: the light at Main Street and State Highway 23: the island's yellow curb and grass with the keep-right sign, mast-arm signals on the north corners and post signals (animated on `signalPhase`), the route sign assembly and two-way arrow in front of the falls, the green guide sign on Main Street, and the stone parapets with iron railings on the West Montello Street bridge.
- `world/Falls.ts`: the falls across the highway from the light: red granite in blocky tiers with three cascades streaming down chutes into a dark pool behind a cut-stone wall, a wishing well and iron fence; and the granite park's quarry faces along every steep shore of the river pool and the quarry pond (as tall as the bank, facing the water) with a small falls at each of the park's other mapped waterfalls. The falls, faces and small falls are Blender models; if they can't load, the built-in falls stand in and the quarry faces are left out.
- `tools/build-montello.py` + `world/Kit.ts`: the Blender builds (run with `blender -b -P modules-src/tello/tools/build-montello.py`, or `python3` with the `bpy` module, then `pnpm assets` and commit `public/models/tello/`): `downtown-kit.glb` (bracketed cornice with modillions and dentils, scroll brackets, arched hoods with keystones, flat hoods, double-hung window frames, stone sills, cast-iron pilasters, a panelled shop door, a bell-cast turret roof with finial) and `quarry.glb` (the falls, three quarry faces, the small falls). Each root node is a part and each material name a slot (trim, stone, door, shingle, metal, granite, wetrock, wallstone, water, foam, pool, wood); `ModelKit.put` lays a part's triangles into the merged builders, scaled to each window, front and turret and coloured by slot (or by the part's own vertex colours), so the detail adds no draw calls. The storefronts use the kit when it loads and their simpler shapes when it doesn't.
- `world/Landmarks.ts`: the municipal building on Underwood Avenue from the owner's photos (brown brick, MONTELLO MUNICIPAL BUILDING on its raised panel, the glass entry, the Police Dept. bay and porch, the fire station's three white bay doors with MONTELLO, the cross and FIRE, bollards, flagpoles and iron fence), the welcome mural on the river wall west of the bridge (our own painting: pines, a lake, a leaping bass, "Welcome to Montello") and the realty office's marquee with its three green pines.
- `tools/build-town-kit.py` (run with `blender -b -P modules-src/tello/tools/build-town-kit.py -- house places fields`, add `preview` for contact sheets or `demo` for assembled renders in `docs/tello/landmarks/`, then `pnpm assets` and commit `public/models/tello/`): `house-kit.glb` (double-hung windows with casings and sills, an entry door, garage doors, shutters, a columned porch, a stoop, chimney caps, gutters, downspouts, a heat pump), `places-kit.glb` (pump, island, bollard, school bus, lot light, cart corral, rooftop unit, dock door, wall light, entry canopy) and `fields-kit.glb` (bases, plate, pitcher's plate, backstop, dugout, bleacher, chain-link fence panel, scoreboard); closed convex pieces with no guessed normals, a few dozen triangles each.
- `world/HouseDetails.ts`: lays the house kit on every house and garage (`Buildings.ts` calls `dressOpenings` and `dressRoof` after the roof tone is known): windows on the front and long walls (flat pane-and-frame quads on short side and back walls, a third of the panes lit at night), the entry door under a columned porch or on a stoop, shutters, garage doors, gutters along every eave with a downspout, a brick chimney with a cap, a gable dormer cut to the roof's real pitch where the roof is tall enough, and a heat pump; `Buildings.ts` also shades each wall darker toward the ground and boards every corner of a sided house. About 15 kit parts a house, merged into the existing builders (no new draw calls). `shellSkips` (exported from `Buildings.ts`) is a set of building names whose procedural shell and collider are left out because another builder puts an enterable building on the footprint: add `Foxtails Gentlemen's Club` before `buildBuildings` runs when the casino has its own walls.
- `world/TownDetail.ts` builds the street scene `Landmarks.ts` hands it, with `Decals.ts` (flat paint on the ground in the town frame: stripes, curbs, infield dirt, chalk, mowing stripes, courts, the track), `PlaceLayer.ts` (kit parts into merged chunks) and `Signs.ts` (plain lettering on dark boards through the sign atlas). `world/LotPlan.ts` + `world/Lots.ts`: every parking lot gets 2.7 by 5.5 m stalls in head-to-head rows set by its long sides, a curb that opens at driveways and along building walls, and light poles down the aisles of the big ones (the bus lot, the school's paved court, its south-west yard and Freitag's service yard get no stripes). `world/Businesses.ts`: Kwik Trip, BP and Mobil (glass store front with a sign band and entry canopy, pump islands with dispensers where the bake put the pumps, a lit fuel canopy lettered on both sides, and Mobil's canopy built over its pumps because OpenStreetMap maps none), Freitag's Food Center (glass front, canopy, sign, roof units, cart corrals), Drew's Lakeside Bar & Grill (entry, sign, a deck with umbrella tables on the lake side), Yahooz's sign, Foxtails Gentlemen's Club (exterior only: blank dark front, canopy, sign and a sign pylon), the Marquette County Sheriff's Office (portico, flagpoles, monument sign) and the freight depot behind Freitag's (a metal warehouse with seven dock doors in Freitag's service yard; `BusinessesBuilt.depot` says where its dock face is). `world/School.ts`: the high school's bus lot gets a row of school buses and its entrance a canopy, lettered board and flagpoles. `world/FieldPlan.ts` + `world/BallFields.ts`: each ball diamond from its pitch record (regulation base paths, mound distance and second base; both park softball diamonds' headings are re-read from the aerial photo, which shows them opening the other way): dirt skin and base paths round a grass diamond, dirt at home and the bases, mown outfield stripes, a warning track, foul lines and batter's boxes in chalk, a raised mound with the plate, bases, home plate, a backstop with wings, dugouts, bleachers, and a chain-link fence along the foul lines and round the outfield; tennis and basketball courts get a coloured surface, lines and a tall fence, the school's track four red lanes.
- `world/Commercial.ts` + `world/WallKit.ts`: what every shop, civic and industrial building wears beyond its bare shell. Metal buildings (industrial, and photographed fronts whose upper wall is paint or siding) get a dark wainscot with a cap, panel seams every metre, corner and eave trim, downspouts, steel doors with a wall pack, overhead doors and an office window on the wall facing the pavement; stores get a plinth, pilasters, a pale band under the coping, rear steel doors and a dock door on the back of big stores. A wall no photo or business dresses and that faces the pavement gets a glass front, entry canopy and a lettered sign when the building has a name. Walls covered by a downtown storefront (`planFronts`), a business (`Businesses.ts` hands them over as `taken`) or a neighbour are skipped, and the high wall of a single-slope roof rises to its ridge. Everything goes into the place layer's merged builders (no new draw calls). `WallKit.ts` holds the wall helpers `Businesses.ts` shares (`wallNear`, `wallQuad`, `wallBox`, `storeFront`, `entryCanopy`, `wallPacks`); the freight depot is a ribbed metal building with seven dock doors under a steel canopy, a trailer at a dock and the delivery marker at its face, Freitag's Food Center has a glass entry vestibule, and the dealership a plain-lettered pole sign.
- `town/TelloMap.ts`, `town/Routes.ts`: the corner map, the full map (Ctrl) and directions over the real road network.

## World edits (`edits/`)

The administrator changes Life Sim's Montello in the World Editor (admin panel). The editor saves an ordered list of edits (`edits/WorldEdits.ts`: `{ v: 1, world: 'lifesim', base, ops }`), and the published list is served by `GET /api/world-edits`. Life Sim fetches it as it loads (`foxlake/index.ts`) and hands it to `TelloLand` as its fourth argument. The constructor replays it with `applyWorldEdits` (`edits/Apply.ts`) after `fixStreets` and before the bridge decks are read, so the edits work on the surveyed heights (map metres above `frame.base`) and Fox Lake moves the lake to y = 0 afterwards. `land.edits` lists the ops it applied and the ones it skipped (with the reason); an op that no longer fits the map is skipped, never thrown.

- Ops name what the map already has by id: `r<index>` roads, `b<index>` buildings and `l<index>` lots, numbered after Tello's fixes (`assignIds`). Features an op adds are named after the op.
- Terrain (raise, lower, flatten, smooth, grade, noise; buildings, lots, bridges and water ride with the ground unless `carry` is off), water (fill, level, paint, drain), rivers, roads (new or redrawn, with sidewalks and grading; removed; width, class and name changed), buildings (new, removed, changed or moved), parking lots (new, removed, reshaped, "no stalls", trimmed off the roads) and trees (planted or cleared) change the grids and the document directly. Every builder, the physics, the maps and the routes then see the edited world with no change of their own.
- Rails, lights and walls are left in `doc.extras` (`WorldExtras`) for `world/EditedFeatures.ts`: walls and fences that follow the ground in two-metre pieces with pillars, and acorn, cobra-head and lot lights, all solid. The automatic guardrails are `railRuns` (`guardrailRuns` less the editor's rail cuts, plus its rails), and the downtown lamp posts and lot lights inside a light cut are left out (`lightCut`). A lot with `ns` gets no stalls.
- Removing a road filters `doc.roads`, so road indexes shift; a removed building keeps its place in `doc.buildings` with `x` set (never built, solid or in a road's way), and new roads, buildings and lots go on the end. Code must not keep a road index from before the edits: the West Montello Street bridge is found by its `s` flag, not its road. The downtown street profiles (`townProfiles`) are worked out by `fixStreets`, before the edits, so a street keeps its cross-section when it is changed and only the streets still on the map are painted.
- Not moved by edits: what the shared world reads from `maplestreet/shared/MontelloPlaces.ts` (shop doors, pumps, the freight depot, lots for sale and their floors, house front steps, the casino site), which is generated from the baked map. A removed or redrawn road takes its bridge deck with it (`reindexBridges` keeps the rest pointing at their roads); a new road over water crosses on a graded fill, not a bridge.

Life Sim's `?edits=draft` loads the administrator's draft instead, `?edits=none` loads none, and `?at=x,z` starts on foot at that map point. Tests: `tests/edits.test.ts`.

## The map data (`tools/bake_map.py`)

`public/modules/tello/montello.bin` and `montello.json` are baked and committed; the page never reaches an outside service. Re-bake with:

```sh
python3 modules-src/tello/tools/bake_map.py            # needs numpy, scipy, pillow, pyproj, shapely, laspy[lazrs]
```

It downloads into `.tello-cache/` (git-ignored, about 8 GB with the point cloud and the working rasters) and writes both files. `--debug <dir>` also writes a picture of the cover, roads and buildings.

| Layer | Source |
| --- | --- |
| Roads, water outlines, land use, places, some buildings | OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) |
| Ground height and water levels | USGS 3DEP bare-earth lidar |
| Roof shape, eave and ridge height of each building; buildings no footprint source has; bridge deck heights; every tree and its height | The 2018 FEMA/USGS lidar point cloud of Marquette County (178 one-kilometre tiles, about 4 points per square metre, from NOAA Digital Coast's open bucket) |
| Ground cover | USDA NAIP aerial imagery with near infrared |
| Most building footprints | Microsoft Global ML Building Footprints (ODbL) |

What the bake does: finds each water body's level in the lidar and carves a bed under it; lays every road's bed level across the road and smooths it along; makes long water crossings bridges (at the deck height the point cloud shows) and short ones culverts; levels a yard under every building and parking lot; merges OpenStreetMap's buildings with Microsoft's and covers each footprint with rectangles for roofs and colliders.

From the point cloud (`load_lidar`, one-metre rasters plus the last returns over buildings):

- **Roofs** (`measure_roofs`, `fit_roof`): the last returns on each rectangle are fitted with a flat roof, a gable along either axis (the ridge tried at nine offsets), a hip and a single slope; the best fit gives the roof type and the eave and ridge heights. 3,469 of 3,646 buildings are measured; the rest (built since 2018, or hidden) get a roof by their kind.
- **Missing buildings** (`find_buildings`): patches at least 2.2 m up whose surface is solid (one return per pulse) and planar, clear of mapped footprints, roads and water: 103 outbuildings the footprint sources lack.
- **Trees** (`detect_trees`): one tree at every crown top in the canopy height model, with its real height (median 16 m, tallest 34 m). Crowns that let little through to the ground in the leaf-off survey are conifers. Every tree near buildings is kept; farther out only the tallest in each patch, so the far woods stay drawable: 406,749 trees (the ring is about 140 km2).
- **Woods on the ground**: ground cover under crowns comes from the same canopy model.

### City limits

Only the City of Montello and the country three miles round it are built. The bake reads the city's boundary from
OpenStreetMap (relation, admin level 8, holes filled) and ships it with the playable area, the limits widened by three
miles (`PLAY_PAD` 4,828.032 m; it was a mile, trimmed on the east toward Fox Lake, until the 2026-11-15 re-bake, and
Fox Lake is now Montello alone, so the area is the plain ring): `limits` in the JSON (`city`, `play`, the road `exits`,
the `link` State Highway 23 way) and the signed-distance grid `play` in the bin. The frame is 16,384 m
(x -8600..7784, z -7700..8684, still a power of two and still a whole map width from the origin in the scene) and holds the ring plus
`VIS_PAD` (240 m) of drawn land past it. Buildings outside the ring are dropped; ground, water, roads and the lidar
woods are drawn only `seen` (240 m) past the playable edge (`TelloLand.seen`); everything beyond that is empty in the
bin (flat ground, no cover, no trees), which also keeps the file small. Invisible walls follow the edge
(`Montello.addWalls`), and every road that leaves it ends at a striped barricade (`world/Barricades.ts`). Every roof
takes its colour from the NAIP aerial photo (`rc`, the median of the bare pixels inside the footprint, darkened a
fifth for the haze by `aerialRoof`).

The bake works one window at a time so a 16 km frame fits in memory: the point cloud goes into one-metre rasters on
disk (`.tello-cache/lidar_*.mm`, about 2.7 GB, tile by tile, resumable) and buildings from the cloud, trees and the
pavement field are made in 1 km windows with a 40 m halo (`CHUNK`, `HALO`). Trees are thinned by distance: a tight gap
within 40 m of pavement or 150 m of a building, 9 m to 450 m, 13 m to 1.2 km and 20 m past that. Positions in the bin are
quarter metres (`frame.treeUnits`, 4; the old decimetre scale overflowed 16 bits past 6.5 km). Streets and highways
are eased round the houses, garages, sheds, churches, civic and industrial buildings their width would run into
(`avoid_buildings`: the corner house by the fire station on Underwood Avenue no longer sits in the road). Places the
open data lacks (Freitag's Food Center, Drew's Lakeside Bar & Grill, Foxtails Gentlemen's Club) are `EXTRA_PLACES`
in the bake: each takes the nearest sizeable footprint and becomes a store there; the dealership is OpenStreetMap's
H A Freitag & Son (`shop=car`) on Main Street. Each place's `pois` row carries its front door `d`, the street `s`,
its building's index `i`, a spot ten metres behind the building `b`, and fuel stations without a mapped canopy get
four pumps in front (`pumpsApprox`). The JSON also lists `pitches` (ball diamonds and fields) and `slipways` (boat
launches). The real addresses and map coordinates of every named place are in `docs/tello/MONTELLO-PLACES.md`.

### What is exact and what is not

- **Exact to the data:** the lie of the land (lidar, sampled 4 m apart), water levels, every mapped road's line and class, shorelines, each building's place and footprint, its roof type and its eave and ridge heights as they stood in spring 2018, bridge deck heights, and each tree's place and height.
- **From photos and video:** the storefronts and street furniture of West Montello, East Montello and Main Street, the light, the falls, the island and the street paint, the bridge's level and parapets, and the water tower, from the owner's photos and drives.
- **Not real:** what walls, windows and doors look like elsewhere (no open data has facades, so they wear Fox Lake's finishes by type; the rest of downtown gets fronts in the same spirit), roof colours, dormers, porches and chimneys, road widths where OpenStreetMap has none (set by class), tree species beyond conifer or broadleaf, lake depths (a bed sloped from the shore), signs (except downtown's, which carry the real business names in plain lettering), fences and street furniture outside downtown. Anything built or cut down since 2018 is as it was then; 38 footprints had no building under them in 2018 and keep a roof by their kind.
- **Bounds:** a 16,384 m square that holds the city limits, three miles round them and the woods seen past that. The edge is a wall.

## Controls

Rack & Reel's walking and driving, plus E (drive), V (bring out the next vehicle), H (back to Main Street), Ctrl (map and directions), N (corner map zoom). The Esc menu lists them.

## Checks

- `node scripts/tello-tests.mjs` (in `pnpm test`; `tests/places.test.ts` and `tests/houses.test.ts` cover the kits, the lots' stalls, the diamonds' geometry and every house's parts): the map files, the frame and projection, water levels, road beds, bridges, buildings, measured roofs, trees and their heights, triangulation and routing.
- `node scripts/foxlake-capture.mjs [baseUrl] [outDir] [spots]`: screenshots and frame times of Fox Lake.
- `window.__tello.town.debug()`.
