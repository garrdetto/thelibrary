    How the physics and the rules work
    • Every pitch is flown with drag and Magnus lift: backspin rides, topspin dives, side spin sweeps. Thinner air at altitude carries farther.
    • Your swing has timing and a contact cursor. Early swings pull the ball, late swings go the other way; hitting under the ball lifts it.
    • Fair or foul, infield fly, tag-ups, forces, double plays, steals, balks and walk-offs all follow the rulebook.
    • The box score credits hits, errors, earned runs, wins, losses, saves and holds the way an official scorer would.
